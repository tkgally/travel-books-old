[[subtitle]] A Camp in a Crater.

S<sc>t. vincent</sc> contains the last of the West Indian volcanoes from which the present century has witnessed destructive eruptions; the Soufrière, that towered above and overlooked the Richmond plantation, having, in 1812, burst upon the island with terrible force.
