My precious bird had landed safely at the bottom of the gulch, though somewhat soiled, and he now reposed in my game-basket, wrapped in a paper cone. This was the first soufrière-bird I secured; the next day I shot three others; they proved to be a <i>new</i> species and were named the <i>Myiadestes sibilans.</i> Another species, shot in the same locality, proved to be also new, and was afterwards named the <i>Leucopeza Bishopi.</i>

The day following, Toby’s patience gave out entirely, and I was obliged to descend the mountain to the Carib country, which, as the cloud of fog lifted, I could see from my cave as a lovely green slope, lying between dark mountains and blue, white-rimmed ocean.
