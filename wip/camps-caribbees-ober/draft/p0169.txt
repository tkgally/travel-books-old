A very old French writer gives the only account that we can find of these crabs; and were it not that I had seen them on the march, there are some things he says the truth of which I should be inclined to doubt. They live not only in a kind of orderly and quiet society in their retreats in the mountains, but regularly once a year march down to the seaside in a body, some millions at a time.
