[[cont]]The fire had burned low, and it was only by its fitful gleams that I saw this strange vision. Meyong touched me, and whispered, “Coryet see jumbie.”

So it was; he had seen the visitor of whom I had but felt the presence. Looking upon this event, or chain of events, in the light of subsequent revelations, I laugh; but at that time I almost believed, with my boys, in the existence of a forest spirit.
