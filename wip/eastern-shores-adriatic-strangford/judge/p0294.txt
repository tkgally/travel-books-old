Standing on a rock in front of the pretty port is one of the three famous castles belonging to the Frangipani family; two other castles belonging to them still bear their name—one at Tersatto, by Fiume, the other at Osaly, near Karlstadt. This Castle of Tersatto is the most prominent object as one approaches beautiful Fiume; but I confess to having soon forgotten it, and everything else, in the pleasure of finding myself once more in a first-rate hotel, with spacious airy rooms, modern furniture, and a good <i>cuisine</i>. The Hôtel de l’Europe is situated on the quay, one side facing the sea, the other opposite to the new Casino and the <i>Piazza Grande</i>.

Fiume is itself very pretty, and all the views from it are still more so; it is a healthy, pleasant residence, and very cheap. Steamers from Dalmatia arrive once a week, from Trieste three times à week. The town is not in the least crowded, but, on the contrary, the streets are wide and airy; and the modern aspect of the place, with its bustling commercial sounds and sights, formed a striking contrast to the old-fashioned, walled-in cities I had so lately seen.
