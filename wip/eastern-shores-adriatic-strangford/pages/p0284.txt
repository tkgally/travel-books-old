Happily it ceased to blow at midnight; but the captain of a steamer I sailed in from Fiume a few days afterwards told me he had made his passage that afternoon across this Quarnero Gulf in imminent danger. It is a very rare occurrence for the Bora to blow in this manner in the middle of summer; but, for several months of the year, a collect is added to the daily litany in all the churches of Istria and Dalmatia for deliverance from this dreadful wind.

Zara, the seat of the Diet, the bureaucratic capital, and the archiepiscopal throne of Dalmatia, is a very small city, standing on an island completely inclosed in high ramparts. These have been lined with grassy banks, and thickly planted with trees; so that Zara is pleasantly inclosed within leafy as well as stone walls. In the centre of her sea-front is a gate, Venetian on one side, Roman on the other; it leads into the principal street of the city, which is a quaint little place, clean and bright-looking, with very narrow streets and several piazzas.
