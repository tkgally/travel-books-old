There is very good coal close to Sebenico, and quantities of iron in all the country round. The port is one of the best and largest in the world, but such is the want of all energy or capital there, that it was cheaper to import all their coal from England rather than cut or carry it to their own doors. I was glad to learn, however, that a company of six gentlemen had been formed to build a small theatre at the gate of the town. It is to cost 3,000<i>l.</i>, and was to be commenced in a few days; and it is to be hoped that other works will follow this beginning of combined interests.

Our drive across this uncultivated tableland had lasted two hours, when we found ourselves standing at the mouth of the Kerka, where it falls into the Gulf of Sebenico. On the other side of the gulf was Scardona—an unhealthy place on the borders of a marsh, which, however, could be easily drained and freed from malaria.
