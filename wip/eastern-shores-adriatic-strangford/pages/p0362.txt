The Mussulmans of Servian race in Bosnia, a million of men more or less, are the haughtiest and most fanatical of European Moslems. They are to be the soonest converted to Christianity; and this will be done off the reel by means and for the sake of the ‘idea of nationality’ so soon as the Turks are ‘driven to Asia,’ which, to be sure, will give you a very good kind of Christianity to begin with. The borderer of Servian race in another country, under the name and aspect of ‘South Sclavonian,’ is the actual incarnation of every military and civic virtue; he is to cry for all he wants, and what he cries for he is to get.
