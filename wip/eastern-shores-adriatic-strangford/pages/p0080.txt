# Chapter II.

[[subtitle]] A Few Words on Corfu Politics.

I SUPPOSE it is not easy for an Englishman to disguise his satisfaction at the termination of our thankless and unprofitable trust, however unpolite, not to say impolitic, it may be to express it. At any rate we have not cared to disguise it. Our press at home has spoken out from the fulness of its heart, and rated the Ionians in good round terms for a set of fractious and impracticable creatures, who will neither govern themselves nor let others govern them, and of whom it will be well to be rid once and for ever. Such opinions, carefully pointed and edged with incisive wit, as is the manner of periodical writing, have been the cause of much natural offence among the Ionians.
