# Chapter III.

[[subtitle]] Ragusa.

IT had been decided that I should employ the next two months in seeing as much as I could of Dalmatia, and on my return to Corfu I determined to take the next ‘Dalmato-Albanese’ steamer northwards. The summer months were certainly not the best for the purpose, if it could have been avoided, as the heat forced one to confine one’s travelling almost entirely to the coast; and although Dalmatia is the narrowest country in the world, I should have been glad to go more inland than I ultimately found possible. Thinking, however, that ‘half a loaf was better than no bread,’ I resolved to see all that was feasible; and I was very glad to take advantage of a kind offer made to me by the Lord High Commissioner, of arranging that his aide-de-camp, Captain Strahan, should accompany me in the early part of my journey.
