The mountaineer used to wander all over the low country as a gardener or field-labourer, even as far as Constantinople, or stay at home and fight his border enemies, with equal indifference, and with nobody by to settle whether this was peace or war, according to Vattel and Puffendorf, or to invent and pile up the airy, glowing romance of his being a sort of successful Christian Leonidas, triumphantly holding his Thermopylæ for hundreds of years against the whole force of Turkey. Nor did they care a straw at Constantinople whether the unruly and unprofitable Albanian, or the untamable infidel, did or suffered the most in the way of cutting throats. But at present, the Montenegrine is not encouraged, by one side or the other, to leave his country, for the defence of which lately every Montenegrine was wanted, for temporary field-labour in Turkey. He does not care to gain his bread that way, and his poverty is pressing very hard upon him.
