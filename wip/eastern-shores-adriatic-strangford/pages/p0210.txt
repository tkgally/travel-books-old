I never saw such a ludicrous-looking bridge; but it is too grotesque to be picturesque. It was built in 1768 by, and at the expense of, Ahmed Pasha: but he is said to have built a really beautiful bridge at Podgoritza. Over the Boyana there is nothing but a miserable broken wooden thing, over which it is almost dangerous to pass.

Riding round the noble rock of the fortress, which is grand on every side, we came to the bazaars. They are sufficiently poor for a town of so much importance as Skodra. No traveller, however, would stop to think of the bazaars, even were they much handsomer. The beauty of the surrounding mountains is more than enough to fill one's eyes and thoughts. For myself, I know few places lovelier or so lovely as Skodra. Its beauty must, of course, be much enhanced when the winter snows clothe the mountain summits. They are not seen later than May, although snow is to be found throughout the summer on many of the heights.
