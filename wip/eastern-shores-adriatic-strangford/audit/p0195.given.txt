If this derivation be really correct, it is curious and valuable, for it shows that modern Albanian is capable of interpreting a name that dates back as far as the days of the Roman Republic, at all events in one instance. No fortress could be more finely sitnated; it looks up the whole lake and commands all the windings of the river for a considerable distance, while the town lies at its feet; so richly embedded in gardens and groves as hardly to he distinguished from the woods beyond it.

We landed under shade of a clump of lofty plane trees, and sat for some time admiring the scene before us.
