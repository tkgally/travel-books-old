Both nations are industrious, quick, and healthy-minded; both, as regards the Christians at least, are anxious to improve; and while the Dalmatians, although very poor, are immeasurably superior to the Bosnians in civilisation and refinement, they would draw the latter towards them in the double bonds of interest and affection. But Dalmatia has no money, and Austria spends but very little upon her, and she is scarcely likely to accomplish any part of this railway without English capital. We may hope that this latter may be forthcoming some day, for the line, as described to me, is one from which much success may confidently be anticipated.
