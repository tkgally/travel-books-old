But the grand scheme, dearest of all to his heart, is that of a railway from Spálato to Belgrade—a line which, I am told, is naturally very easy, with no heavy gradients in the Bosnian mountains or other expensive obstacles to be overcome, and which would be of incalculable advantage to the whole of that country. This railway would not only open up the whole of Bosnia and give the rich produce of a perfectly virgin soil to Europe by the sea coast, but it might pave the way to a more real connection of Bosnia with Dalmatia. It would be but sorry work for either the Austrian or the Turkish Government to thwart this scheme out of mere political jealousy or dread of South-Sclavism, whenever it may be laid before them as a substantial proposition.
