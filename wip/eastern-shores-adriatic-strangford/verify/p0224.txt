This, of course, refers to the mountaineers and country people, not to the townspeople. Thus, for instance, the Mussulmans of Retchi celebrate the feasts of Christmas, Easter, St. Nicholas, and St. George; and in illness or distress they are sure to send for a Catholic priest to pray for them. The tribe of Skreli derives its name from St. Charles—Shen Kerli—to whom it was anciently dedicated; they pay tithes to the Catholic priest and join in the church festivals, although professing Islamism. Things have so changed, that at this day the Christian mountaineer has infinitely the advantage over the Mussulman, as he escapes the conscription by avowing his religion.
