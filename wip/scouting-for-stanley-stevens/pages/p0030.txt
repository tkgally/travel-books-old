Here Thomson, when retreating from the menaces of the Sigarari Masai, obtained refuge for his demoralized followers for a couple of months, while he returned to Mombasa for more men and goods; and at Taveta, also, Count Teleki found it necessary to spend three months in the work of organization before undertaking his great journey to Suk and Elgumi.

The middle of March had arrived, and nothing had been heard of Stanley since the simultaneous and doubtful dispatches from St. Thomé and Zanzibar three months before. Dr. Abbott and myself having decided to join forces as far as Kilimanjaro, we determined to proceed to Mombasa.

One hundred and ten men had been engaged for us by our Zanzibar agent, and we learned that we should be able to secure as many more as we might want at the mission stations of Frere Town and Rabai when we reached the port of our departure for the interior.
