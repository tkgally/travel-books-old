I handed to my companion the clipping from the <i>London Times</i>, through the medium of which Stanley's long message had reached me, and sat down, to reflect.

Accompanying the clippings from the <i>Times</i>, was a private letter stating that Stanley was understood to be coming down through Masai-land toward Mombasa. It did not take me long to make up my mind what course to pursue. Both Abbott and myself wanted to see something of the Masai. If Stanley was coming down through Masai-land, why not go and meet him, as the best thing left to be done by a newspaper?
