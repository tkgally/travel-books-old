Jack and I in the dead of night ascended to this place to collect skulls. I had marked out two which I thought could be easily got at without being observed from the town. We felt rather nervous, not from any feeling of seeing ghosts, but of the natives seeing us, so, while Jack watched, I explored with my arm among the <i>débris</i> of broken timber and stones, of which the graves had been built, and got two skulls which were almost perfect. Shaking the sand and dust out, we tied them up in handkerchiefs, and stealthily returned to camp. Conway packed and sealed them up in a box, and the next morning sent them on to Gilgit.
