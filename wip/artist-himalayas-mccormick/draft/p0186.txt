Striking out over the crevasse and stone-covered glacier at the side we reached the clear ice. The men were now in good spirits and came along splendidly, for Conway had promised them a regular feast of plenty when we got to Askole.

I have made no mention of Shah Murat, our so-called guide, who was absolutely useless for any purpose whatever, for he knew nothing of the route, the country being as new to him as it was to us. However, he was some sort of ornament to the party as he wore a long black velvet coat.

This day I had a pleasant march down, resting at frequent intervals. Karbir had attached himself to me, and attended me in my sketching excursions, carrying my traps, and learning to make all the necessary preparations for work whenever I found a subject. He was a most delightful companion and very intelligent, and we spent many agreeable hours together; he was always cheery, and laughed at all difficulties.
