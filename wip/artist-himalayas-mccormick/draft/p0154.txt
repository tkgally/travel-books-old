The old boy was very chary of giving any information about the entrances to his kingdom from the north, and tried to persuade us not to attempt to find out. He had a very intelligent-looking Wazier, who was more communicative, and from whom Conway got a lot of curious and useful information about the passes over the Hispar and the dangers thereof, and about fairies, and legends of the people and their origin. Of course the royal family were descendants of Alexander, like all other rajahs of petty states in these mountains.

Giving our salaams we departed, paying a visit on the way to the mosque of the castle. The archaic wall-paintings of teapots, or things that looked like them, and of swords surrounded by scroll work, were very interesting and quaint.
