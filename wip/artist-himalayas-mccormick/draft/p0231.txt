The snowstorm continued all night, and in the morning the glacier was covered a foot deep, yet Rahim Ali managed to make us a cup of chocolate for breakfast, which comforted us greatly. Wrapped in all our warm clothing, we awaited the sun, which came out in a couple of hours, and soon melted the greater part of the new snow, and enabled us to continue our way down.
