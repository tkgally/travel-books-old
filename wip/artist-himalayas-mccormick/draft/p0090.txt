A long journey was still before us, and with no refreshment except half a hard-boiled egg, which the Colonel shared with me, we began our long descent to the Chelong hut. We went on and on over hard snow, sometimes plunging into soft, snow-covered gullies up to the waist; but the bright sun and clear avenue of the valley made these incidents of the way of little moment.
