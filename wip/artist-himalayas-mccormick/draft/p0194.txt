By this time Zurbriggen came up with us, so we all had a rest and a smoke, and dried our clothes and sweated in the sun.

We had a more exciting time on our next march, having to cross a rope bridge over the Punmah river. This, with our numerous caravan, occupied much time.

[[image: pen sketch of turbaned men squatting round a smoking camp fire, with baggage boxes in the foreground and tents and figures behind]]

The bridge had been newly repaired, and the leaves having been left on the twigs gave it a gay and festive appearance. The coolies got their loads over one at a time at first, but later on three or four dared to cross at once.
