At last we came to fields of waving grain almost ripe for harvest, and passing on through a tree-bordered lane we entered the village and were surrounded by the natives and greeted by the old Lambadhar with hearty welcome; the tents were up in the old place amongst the shady trees, and we entered on bliss unspeakable.
