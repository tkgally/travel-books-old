But night in the Canal is the most wonderful: when one ties up to let another boat pass, one sees at first its white eye, the big search-light, that grows and grows and turns the sand into piles of drifting snow; then it shoots over and past, and the gloomy ship glides by with its unknown folk who cheer. And then on again through the lakes, by day shallow and green, but at night dark waters illuminated with lighted buoys, till they look like a Chinese garden.
