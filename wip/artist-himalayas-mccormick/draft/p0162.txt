Jack selected me for his guide on the return journey across the glaciers. We started off with a coolie, and luckily reaching the opposite side without any mishap, we chose a suitable place for camping, and awaited the others, who arrived shortly after.

Next morning Jack was given charge of the heavy baggage, and was to cross by the regular pass over the ridge to the Hispar valley, while Conway, with Zurbriggen and I, were to try for a new route higher up and meet him at Hispar. Unfortunately Zurbriggen, while Conway and I were working on the top, had mistaken the way and lost sight of us, which gave us a long and weary march. We found him late in the evening when we had almost made up our minds to settle in the open for the night. He had got back to the track over the old pass, so that we had gained nothing in the way of a new route.
