Merchants came with their beautiful, soft, snowy webs of <i>Pashmina</i>, and though we did not buy, we enjoyed the sensation of shopping as much as a woman in Regent Street. The novelty of seeing anything in the nature of a shop was so strange. They soon left, and I went out sketching.

What a change of subject from the ice and snows to white-flowered fields, through which walked natives in yellow, blue, and white muslin robes! In the background were rich dark green trees, and, under them, white-walled and dark-timbered houses, beautiful to look on and to sketch.

Next morning, after the baggage had been sent off to Scardu, we wandered, in the cool shadow of hills cast right across the valley by the rising sun, down through fields of grain, and past busy farmyards to the sandy beds of the river on which was lying a collection of blown-out sheepskins lashed to a lot of very thin poles.
