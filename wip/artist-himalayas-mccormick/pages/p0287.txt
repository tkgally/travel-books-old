[[cont]]began again, and suddenly turning to a gap through crumbling sand-mounds, the broad, wide, bleak, treeless plain of Leh, spread out before us, with a straight dusty white track leading to the terraced houses which sparkled in a green setting, a mere speck in the blank plain. Natives with ponies and donkeys, laden with grass, were coming and going; but all made way for us as we cantered in clouds of dust towards the town. My animal gave up as we entered, and walking slowly through the streets to the bazaar, we saw big horses whose bodies were hidden under bales of cloth, on the top of which the rider sat, his legs at right angles to his body on either side.
