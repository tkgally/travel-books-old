Next day we arrived at Bandipur, where the baggage was unshipped, and the kiltas in which most of it was packed were hoisted on the backs of the coolies who awaited us.

We started on our first march, a short one to Sanarwain, by a way which rose gently to the valley at the foot of the mountains, up which ran a steep zig-zag path. Here our first camp was pitched.
