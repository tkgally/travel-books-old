A plunge into a dark doorway, and a scramble up a great trunk of a tree with roughly hewn steps, leaning against a hole above, brought us to the first story. Going up the same sort of stair again, we arrived in the <i>durbar</i> chamber of the King, who was seated in an armchair that looked as if it had just left a second-hand furniture shop in Tottenham Court Road; I believe it was a present from a Gilgit resident. Around the chamber and on the ground were seated the members of the court; in the centre was a square hole, disclosing another on the floor below, like the hatchways
