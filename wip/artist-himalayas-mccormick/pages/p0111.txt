Taking care not to trip over any more servants who were lying about on the verandah outside, we said good-bye to our pleasant host, and Jack and I made for a deserted tent where Zurbriggen had fixed up his bed. We made ourselves as comfortable as we could on the ground after a few <i>Donnerwetters</i> from Zurbriggen, at our waking him. Conway and Bruce were put up by the officers in their quarters.

[[image: sketch of a figure seen from behind, bending forward, with legs in striped puttees and pointed boots]]

We were all kindly provided with ponies by Dr. Roberts next morning.
