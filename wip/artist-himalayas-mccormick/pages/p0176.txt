Shortly after, one of them espied figures far out on the glacier, and shouted “a letter from the Rajah,” evidently thinking it would make us let them return to their homes, but it turned out to be Zurbriggen and a coolie, who had made a marvellous march. Jack was all right, he had seen him safely on his way.

That evening we gathered together the coolies and Shah Murat, and showing them our revolvers, which we explained to them contained the lives of ten men, told them that the first man who ventured to show signs of leaving us would be shot. We gave Shah Murat a special word of advice for himself, as we suspected him of causing all the trouble.

Next morning we found that they had not cooked all the food required at Haigutum, and there being no wood for fire near us, this necessitated their returning there for it.
