The men returned with the wood in a marvellously short time, when cooking went on with vigour, every one seeming in good spirits since the return of Zurbriggen. His coolie had evidently informed them of his wonderful skill, for they were prepared to follow him anywhere.

The evening turned out one of the most beautiful we had witnessed. The icy faces of the mountains were lit up with the setting sun to the brilliancy of jewels, and later the whole valley was enveloped in a soft haze of golden sunlight, wonderful to behold. Sitting smoking on the rocks beside our tents, we watched it fade away into the grey silver night.
