Making for the opposite bank we got off the ice, and camped at the junction of the great side glacier with the Hispar.

[[image: sketch of two cloaked figures with staffs standing on a glacier above crevasses, mountains behind]]

Continuing our journey next day, as we neared the pass snow-covered crevasses made the travelling very dangerous, but we got to a good camping ground at the side, on the bare spur of a mountain which cut into the glacier like a huge black wedge. Against its snout the ice broke in great masses and made long beautiful curves far out on the glacier in its flow round the obstruction.
