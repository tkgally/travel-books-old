One day Jack came in big with exciting news. His friend Mr. Conway (now Sir William M. Conway) was to lead a surveying and climbing expedition through the Himalayas, and through parts of it which meant real original exploration. He thought of taking an artist with him, and so, said Jack, it was just possible that he might select me. He had seen some work of mine which had pleased him.
