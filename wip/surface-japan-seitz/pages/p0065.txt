The message was delivered to Asano when he came with his next deposit. “I work until twelve each night,” he said shortly. “I have no time for visiting. If he wishes to see me he can come to me after that hour.” The banker went and so began the long relationship, successful alike to the two men and for Japan. Neither speaks English. Both carry on very large affairs.

To-day the house of Asano, at Shiba, is one of the sights of Tokyo. It is the largest private edifice in the city, built in purest Japanese style of unpainted pine so seasoned that not a crack or flaw is visible.
