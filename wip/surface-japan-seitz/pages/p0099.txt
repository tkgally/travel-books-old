Neither Buddhism nor Shintoism, the two religious elements in Japan, maintain what can be called a church organization. The municipalities keep the temples in repair after a fashion, and the priests subsist on the humble offerings of the worshipers, who kneel for prayers. Yet there is a religious, or, rather, moral weekly press in Japan something on the lines of the <i>Outlook</i> without the contributing editor.
