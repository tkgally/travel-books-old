The carpenters work methodically and without haste. What is time, that it should drive a man when he does not wish to hurry? Thus the rewards are small and the economic waste of skill and muscle is great, but life is easy and the soul is free. But it does not afford any resistless legion of workers drilled to effective outputs with which Japan can compete forcefully with the Western world. She has made her military arms concrete and to be well measured with those of other lands, but casual industry cannot compete with the organized article any more than guerillas can beat battalions. Life is so much a mental state that it does not follow that the people are not better so, but it is not a situation that can create wealth for either the many or the few.
