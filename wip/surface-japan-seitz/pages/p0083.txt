He was educated in Germany and spent a year in prison under suspicion of having been mixed up in a conspiracy. He vindicated himself and fills the one really great administrative office in Japan. Baron Matsuo, former governor, and Baron Takahashi, present governor, of the Bank of Japan, which handles the national finances, are men of wide knowledge and singular administrative strength. The latter was educated here and knows his United States well. They are not men to be led into follies or to make mistakes easily. Mr. Juichi Soyeda, president of the Industrial Bank of Japan, has shown ability of the highest order in banking and industrial promotion. Every one of these men rose by individual effort and merit. Opportunity is as democratic in Japan as in America!
