Tokyo, the capital and first city of Japan, covers one hundred square miles and houses two million souls. Because Yokohama is its port the impression prevails that it lies inland. This is not so. Tokyo is on the sea, eighteen miles north of Yokohama, at the head of the Bay of Yedo, that formerly gave the city its name.
