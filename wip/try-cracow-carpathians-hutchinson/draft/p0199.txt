# Chapter IX.

[[subtitle]] The Felker Thal—Horse flesh is cheap—Granite peaks and Felker Lake—Its refuge—Alpine flower-garden—The marmot, or mountain rat—We see many winter houses already commenced—We play at “Tombola,” and lose—The lost one—Up the Schlagendorf Spitze—What I saw there—Hungarian friendliness—Their views of England—The Robbers’ Stone—Toad stools and jumping frogs—Five little lakes.

O<sc>f</sc> all the expeditions round Schmeks, the one to the Felker Thal is the grandest and best, and one that any lady may accomplish with as little difficulty as can be expected in any mountain excursion. The facilities that our little Bad affords for healthy amusement and sport, combined with cheapness, endear it to every tourist. Shooting, fishing, mountaineering, and riding! all to be had as it were, within a stone’s throw of your own quarters!
