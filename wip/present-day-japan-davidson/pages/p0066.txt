How their eyes can stand the strain of deciphering small and badly printed Chinese characters in a jolting train I do not know; indeed, I rather suspect it does even them no good, for one sees an extraordinary number of people in spectacles. They will read for hours together, and if they are alone, or nearly so, they have an irritating habit of doing it half aloud.
