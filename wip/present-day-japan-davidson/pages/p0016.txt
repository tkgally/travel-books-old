[[cont]]amplified the notes on historical and religious subjects meant originally for the information of an intending visitor.

The letters are not dated or separated, because they are not presented in order as written, but have been so arranged that the places and subjects spoken of might be treated with some degree of continuity, passages written soon after arrival being retained by way of introduction to each chapter for the sake of the descriptions which they contain.
