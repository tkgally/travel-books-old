The kettle had to be boiled, of course, and I thought it never would, in this present world at least. A watched pot, one knows, never boils, but it is nothing to a ceremonial kettle on two sticks of charcoal. Then the taste of the achieved result is abominable; it is not what you would call tea at all, but a sort of horrible pea-green porridge rather like very thick green-pea soup.
