The mosque had been converted into a chapel this gentleman told me, and was confided to his care. We entered the old mosque together. Over the altar was a handsome painting of the Holy Family, which was a present from the late Queen of the French. He told me that he was all alone; that he had not even a clerk. He said that in Nauplia there was not a single Catholic, but that there were a few Bavarians employed in the arsenal, and that on Sundays and feast-days they came to assist at the sacrifice of the mass. He said that until that day, for a long time, he had not spoken Italian, which was his native tongue. He spoke Greek well, but he said it was pleasant to him to hear the language of his childhood. He leaned his head pensively on one side as he said this. I saw that he was thinking of his convent-garden in the Alban hills.
