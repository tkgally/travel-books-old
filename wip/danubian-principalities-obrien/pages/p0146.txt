Thereupon the aide-de-camp introduced a young officer, who spoke French very well, and who turned out a very good-tempered, agreeable companion. I agreed fully with the aide-de-camp that even knouting the Cossack to death who might bring me to an untimely end, could scarcely do me much good as I lay stark and cold on the banks of the Ardgish. I therefore accepted the companionship of the young officer with lively gratitude, and I begged of the aide-de-camp to express my acknowledgments to the Prince and to General Dannenberg for their thoughtful kindness.
