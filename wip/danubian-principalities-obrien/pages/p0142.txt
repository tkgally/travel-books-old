# Chapter XV.

W<sc>e</sc> made a detour to get ahead of the soldiers, and as night was setting in we reached the banks of the Ardgish. At every few paces along the road we met with baggage waggons, detachments of soldiers and stragglers of different kinds. But these were all staid regular troops, and not practical jokers, like the Cossacks. The Cossacks prefer playing off their pranks about the advanced posts of the army, where skirmishing is going on, and where there is a little pleasant license.
