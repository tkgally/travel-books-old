Ionians have been amongst the most active agents of the Russian propaganda since the commencement of the present question. They hope that when the Czar will have taken Constantinople, he will then lend his benign protection to the Seven Islands. The Ionians may, at times, have to complain of their Lord High Commissioner and his government; but that they should desire to exchange the protectorate of a free nation like Great Britain for the serfdom of Russia appears incredible.

Another project entertained very generally by the Ionians is, to unite their country with Greece; and they hope to effect this through the agency of Russia.
