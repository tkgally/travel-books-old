7th. “Wallachian subjects who may have occasion to go into any part of the Ottoman dominions, shall not be there called upon for the haratsh, or capitation tax, paid by other rayahs.

8th. “If any Turk have a law-suit in Wallachia, with a subject of the country, his cause shall be heard and decided by the Wallachian divan, conformably to the local laws.

9th. “All Turkish merchants coming to buy and sell goods in the Principality, shall, on their arrival, have to give notice to the local authorities for the time necessary for their stay, and shall depart when that time is expired.

10th. “No Turk is authorized to take away one or more servants of either sex, natives of Wallachia, and no Turkish mosque shall ever exist on any part of the Wallachian territory.
