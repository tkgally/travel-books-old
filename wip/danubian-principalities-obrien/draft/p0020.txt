[[cont]]Ibraila are khans. The one said to be the least dirty is the Locanda Rossa. It is a quadrangular wooden building, with a court-yard in the centre. It is one story high, and the doors of the rooms open into a gallery, which is about three feet from the ground, and runs round the court. The place was chiefly inhabited by Russian officers and some other well-dressed people, who seemed quite contented with their place of abode, though they must have had a constant struggle for possession with the myriads of small inmates with which every crevice and corner of the old khan abounds. Outside each room is a tin box, hung against the wall, and filled with water; and from this a tiny thread of fluid issues, with which the guest performs his ablutions. In this charming abode I passed a night. The only preparations made for my repose were a straw mattress and a rug.
