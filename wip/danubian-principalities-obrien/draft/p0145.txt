[[cont]]It had not rained sufficiently to penetrate the thick layer of coarse, gritty dust with which the road was covered, and the horses, as they galloped along, kicked it up in a mass, and the strong wind, joined to the speed at which we were going, kept it whirling round us in a cloud. My face was as black as if I had come out of a coal-pit, and to my dismay I found that the roast fowls, when unpacked, were covered with a coating of dirt, as if they had been rolled in the ashes. The dust had penetrated through everything. It got into the tube of my chibouk, it incorporated itself with the tobacco, and become a component part of the bread, meat, and wine. The rain had converted the dust on my hat and cloak into a thick coating of stucco, and my hair and beard, from the same cause, had become of the colour and consistency of a tile.
