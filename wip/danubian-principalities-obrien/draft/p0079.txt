The Consul-General invited him into his house, and made him his guest. He had no baggage, not even the bundle which the wayfarer carries on the end of his stick; but he was heartily welcome, nevertheless, and had the best place at the Consul’s table.

He was an Englishman of rank and large fortune. A wound in the affections had driven him from home, and he wandered on as far as the Carpathian mountains, where for some time he led the rude life of a hunter, hoping that in change of scene and the excitement of the chase he might find a cure.
