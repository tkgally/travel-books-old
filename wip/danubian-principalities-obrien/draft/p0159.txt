The original motive of the Turkish general in occupying Mokan was evidently to cause a diversion, whilst he was sending troops across to Oltanitza.

In the beginning of November, the Turks occupied two important points on the left bank of the Danube, as well as the island of Mokan, opposite Giurgevo. At that time, the Russian army in the Principalities did not consist of much more than fifty thousand effective men. If from these three points Omar Pasha had made a simultaneous movement, he might have established his head-quarters at Bucharest.
