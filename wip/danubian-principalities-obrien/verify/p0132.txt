I have seen them struck, and even cruelly beaten by those above them in rank, without murmuring, or even dreaming of seeking redress. The Greeks have a traditional hatred to the Turks; but I have never met one amongst them who would aid Russia in making war against the Porte if he thought that, on the overthrow of the Sultan, he should fall under the yoke of the Czar.
