Riding beside a squadron of hussars was a young officer, mounted on a very spirited Arab horse. He wore the Circassian dress, with the cartouch-boxes on the breast of his coat in silver, richly chased. This seems to me the handsomest uniform I have as yet seen in the Russian army.

Shortly after my arrival at Bucharest, I went with Mr. Colquhoun to pay a visit to the French Consul-General, who then resided in a handsome chateau, the property of the Dowager Princess Ghika, and which is situated about two miles from the town. Close to this chateau is a lake, across which the Russians had thrown a bridge of pontoons on the day previous to our visit.
