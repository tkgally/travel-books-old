# Chapter II.

A<sc>ll</sc> persons arriving in Moldavia or Wallachia from Turkey are obliged to perform four days’ quarantine. This is simply a measure of police, for European Turkey has, for some years past, been in as healthy a state as any other country in Europe. Between Constantinople and Malta there is at present but a nominal quarantine; and between the former port and Trieste there is none. The quarantine in the Principalities is a polite incarceration of four or five days, during which the police have every necessary facility for making inquiries into your political opinions and your object in visiting the country.
