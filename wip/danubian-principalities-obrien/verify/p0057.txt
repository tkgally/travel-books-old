# Chapter VI.

As we proceeded along the road to Bucharest, we saw small parties of Russian soldiers encamped on either side of the way, and in one place we passed a battery of heavy guns, drawn up in line, and pointed menacingly down the road towards Giurgevo. At about six miles from Bucharest we saw a large encampment upon our right, and every few minutes we met a mounted Cossack, hurrying along as if he were the bearer of despatches.
