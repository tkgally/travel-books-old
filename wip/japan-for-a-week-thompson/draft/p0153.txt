“In Japan they live in the glory and radiance of a war for their independence and in the rosy morn of a new period of greatness. We lose our time in bickerings and see our days pass away to no use; in Japan they are silent and work.”

I commend the example of “efficient” Germany and Japan to the emulation of my fellow-Britons. I trust and pray that when they come to be tried—as they surely will be before many years—their achievement will not prove as piffling and as futile as their Mafficking.
