That was the effect of the fuller knowledge and understanding produced by what we had seen and heard in the course of our wanderings.

We began with a day in Berlin; we ended with nearly seven weeks’ intimate daily discourse with German passengers and officers on a German ship; and the concluding experience not only confirmed but very effectually “rubbed in” the impression of German ambition conveyed in our Berlin friend’s frank and breezy explanation of <i>Welt Politik</i>.

Our commercial and financial passengers, like our Berlin friend, talked of the German annexation of Holland and Belgium as certainties of the immediate future. Denmark and Sweden are to follow. Germany is within our time to occupy the whole of Central Europe from North Sea to Adriatic.
