More than a fifth part of the globe is under British rule. To allow all this effort and achievement to be thrown back into the melting pot, to be scrambled for again in fire and slaughter, would be neither wise nor humane. It would be the greatest disaster and set-back that could possibly happen to the progress of internationalism and humanity.

For the Pax Britannica, I repeat, is the most hopeful start the world has ever yet made towards the establishment of Universal Peace.
