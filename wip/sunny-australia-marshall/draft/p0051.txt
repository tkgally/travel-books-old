Dinner-parties, theatre-parties, and public and private balls are crowded into the evenings and nights of this festival of race week. I wished I had been a bit younger for the sake of the dances: they were so well done, and there were so many beautiful people I might have danced with. The Australian women have had so many compliments paid to the remarkably high level they keep up in the matter of good looks that I need not do more than cordially subscribe to all that has been said in their praise. The young girls especially are lovely, with their slim grace and delicate complexions. And they are entirely English-looking, without any of the languor that one might expect as a result of the climate, and with none of the nervous delicacy of the Americans. Perhaps they lose their early freshness sooner than English girls, but they make handsome women, and to the male eye, at least, the well-to-do among them are as well dressed as any women in the capitals of the world.
