[[cont]]A man must be able not only to handle horses, but somewhat intricate machinery, for it is time-saving implements that win such great rewards in the new country.

The settler must be prepared to work like a day-labourer himself. There will be little riding about and overseeing. But if he is so prepared he will only need to employ two hands for a farm of 2,000 acres all the year round, and three during ploughing and harvesting.

So, having made a good start, with average seasons, he may expect to make an income which many an English country gentleman would envy.
