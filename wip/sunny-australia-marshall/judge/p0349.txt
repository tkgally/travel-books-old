## III

We went into the crushing- and roasting-mills afterwards. They are full of fine grey dust from the crushed ore, the grinding and clangor of the mills and the heat of the furnaces. The ore goes through a score of processes before the gold is extracted from it. It is ground and reground, roasted in slow furnaces that never go out night or day, mixed with cyanide solutions, passed in a dirty brown solution through boiling and agitated vats, put through filter presses, and then passed into the locked and guarded precipitation house.
