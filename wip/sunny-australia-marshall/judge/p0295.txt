# XIV
[[subtitle]] Port Arthur

## I

[[right]] H<sc>obart</sc>, T<sc>asmania</sc>

I<sc>t</sc> was only in the year 1877 that the convict station at Port Arthur was finally abandoned, although during the later years of its existence it held a mere handful of prisoners, and those not of the desperate sort who had given the settlement its name of horror.

The oldest inhabitants of Hobart, and some who are not yet old, remember the convict days very well, and have many stories to tell of them. But it is difficult now for the stranger to realise those days, when the whole Colony of Tasmania was practically under police supervision, when every outdoor and indoor servant, male or female, was a transported criminal, and a large proportion of its inhabitants were men and women on ticket-of-leave.
