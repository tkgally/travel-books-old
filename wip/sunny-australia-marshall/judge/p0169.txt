The whole atmosphere of the ship is actively against it, and there seems to be something in the movement, or in the air, which dulls the brain. It needs determination even to write letters before you reach a port. If there is no work that you <i>must</i> do, if you can give yourself up to the spirit of the thing, and your fellow-passengers comprise the usual percentage of companionable and congenial ones, the month's voyage will be an agreeable experience. What with books and music, indoor and outdoor games, much talk, some slumber, meal-time and dressing-time, the day seems to be over almost as soon as it has begun; and you are a couple of hundred miles or so nearer your destination.
