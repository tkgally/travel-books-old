It is no more trouble to dine on one of the ships than at a house on shore, for the time it takes to get there; and the only criticism I have heard of the Australian station, from the naval point of view, is that it is difficult to find time for any work beyond the necessary daily duties.

But the prettiest ball of any I went to was one given at this time by the Governor-General’s staff at Government House, which brings me to what is a pleasant subject for the globe-trotter to remember—the functions and hospitalities of the Governor-General and the six State Governors of Australia.

## V
