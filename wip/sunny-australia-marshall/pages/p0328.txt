# XVII

[[subtitle]] Green Pastures

## I

[[right]] S<sc>ydney</sc>, N.S.W.

T<sc>here</sc> is no more beautiful part of Australia than that district of New South Wales known as the Northern Rivers. And besides its beauty it has a rich soil, a good climate, and an abundant rainfall. After a ten days’ trip, by train, motor-car, and river, in the best of company, I have had to admit that if this part of my experience had been left out, I should have left Australia with impressions quite incomplete, although by this time I have covered thousands of miles of ground in every State of the Commonwealth except Western Australia, which I am to visit on my way home.
