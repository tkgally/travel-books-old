# XVIII

[[subtitle]] Timber

## I

[[right]] P<sc>erth</sc>, W.A.

T<sc>he</sc> trees of Australia are of many kinds. Here are the names of some of them which yield marketable timber: Jarrah, karri, tuart, blackbutt, wandoo, mallet, morrell, gimlet, jamwood. To the stranger there is little variety in them; they are all evergreen, of the same rather dull hue, and in the distance they take on a lovely colour of warm, vaporous blue, which makes a far Australian forest view like nothing else in the world. At first you call them all simply gum-trees, and only when you have seen many different kinds of bush and scrub do you learn to distinguish.
