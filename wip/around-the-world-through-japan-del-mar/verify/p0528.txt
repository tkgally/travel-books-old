A small minority affect to see a danger to their liberties in the necessity for a larger standing army which colonial expansion entails, and look with disfavour upon it, as they do upon that other great agency of American expansion, the so-called “trusts.” As one eloquent labour leader summed up the arguments, “We don’t want no standing army to force us to work for the trusts at a dollar a day.”
