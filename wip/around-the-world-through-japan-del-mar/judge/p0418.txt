The right of domiciliary search is not put by law into the policeman’s hands; but he continually usurps it, and meets with no protest. On the other hand, the policeman is never disposed, even when appealed to, to enforce the “rules of the road,” the regulation of vehicular traffic being a matter beneath his dignity.
