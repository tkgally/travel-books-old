As in England, the trains to the capital are called “up” trains, and those from it “down” trains. The line from Yokohama follows the ancient Tōkaidō (“Eastern Sea road”) close to the coast of Tōkyō Bay, through paddy-fields and orchards of pear-trees trained on trellises. In the fields are big advertisement-boards, as in England and the States. One which proclaims the merits of a certain cigarette bears a representation of the arms of the United States, which contains two stars short of the proper number, but equalises matters by throwing in three extra stripes.
