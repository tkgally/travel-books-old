# Chapter XX

[[subtitle]] The Main Island from Tokuyama to Nagoya

[[subtitle]] Kōbe. Across Country. “The Ladder of Heaven.” A Rough Passage. Lake Biwa. A Curious Tree. Beggars. Awata. The Temples of Ise. The Ise Ondo. The Kagura. Fine Scenery. A Portland Cement Factory.

T<sc>he</sc> Sanyō Railway, in course of construction to Shimonoseki, was finished to Tokuyama and open even as far as Mitajiri. We went by this line, in seven hours and a half, to Okayama, to see the famous Kōraku-En Landscape Garden, where the best effect is made by a tiny waterfall surrounded by a clump of bamboos. We found at Okayama an addition to our commissariat in the shape of tins of preserved Japanese beef, the “manufacturer” of which labels the tins with the following:—
