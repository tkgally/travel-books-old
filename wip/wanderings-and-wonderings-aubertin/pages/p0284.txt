Somewhat apart are handsomer craft, occupied by the more wealthy, and devoted to more wealthy and less public purposes.

We each got into our lifted chair, and were paraded through the city. One word immediately springs to my lips. Canton is a glorious kaleidoscope. There are no streets, they are all broad flat paved passages; all are crowded with variegated Chinese walking to and fro, and very busy about something or about nothing; the shops are open on both sides; some gorgeous, and all well ornamented, and every trade and calling makes appearance. Among the number, mark the butcher with heaps of pork roastings, and a few black dogs into the bargain. Among other glitterings, one most remarkable, and indeed I might almost say gorgeous, effect is produced by the peculiar mode of hanging out signs: a custom pursued by every one. My guide began by leading the way in his chair, but I shortly altered this in order to enjoy the perspective. Large polished black long parallelogram boards are hung out vertically; and in large Chinese character, which is very handsome, the name and the trade are emblazoned on each in very marked, broad, golden characters.
