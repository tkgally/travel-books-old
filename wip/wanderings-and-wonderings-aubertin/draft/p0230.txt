This I did, and suffice it to say that, although I had not known him personally among others of his family there, yet during our long interview I was at Banstead in my early youth again, until I left the house, when I was at once in Darjeeling again. Thus readily can thought adapt itself and wander where it wills, or where it must, but would not. The Colonel had passed his ninety years; and it was about the time of my early days that his brother, “Tom Crommelin,” was a very well-known name among sportsmen, nor can it be yet forgotten by many now living.
