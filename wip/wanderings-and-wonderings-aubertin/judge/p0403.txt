At a later date, while I was in New Zealand, papers arrived with the satisfactory account that the ringleader and several others had been caught, and had been tried, condemned, and decapitated within the twelve hours. That they would die with either bravado or indifference appeared to be expected by those who are best acquainted with the Chinese character, and it was thus, in verity, they met their death.
