[[cont]]Madras time, on Saturday, the 9th of February, I started for my 310 miles to Ahmedabad, which Lady Reay had earnestly recommended me not to miss, by the Bombay, Baroda, and Central India Railway. I must not forget, by the way, that the day before Mr. Behrens, one of the tiger-hunters, suddenly appeared in perfect safety, and pledged me to dine with him at the Byculla. He had been out again and seen the tiger, and nearly got a shot at him; but a companion spoiled the sport. That the tiger was really there was true; for a few days afterwards the newspapers reported that it had been hunted and shot by a young officer.
