We are very comfortable at this house, managed by a European, but owing to rain we have been kept indoors. However, as there is not a very great deal to be seen, the deprivation is not great; but in fine weather there are some pretty drives, and for the Alpine climber some peaks that will test the mettle.

There is a choice of hotels in this place—the Criterion being almost next door—but as this is better filled, and as Prince Napoleon (son of Prince Jerome Napoleon) and his companions are to arrive this evening, I presume we may flatter ourselves that we are at the best one.

The above is rather a rough description of a trip to the mountains of Ceylon, but let me urge upon any of your readers who contemplate a visit to this Island not to leave this journey out, as it more than pays; besides it may save the necessity of a trip to Java; at least, I propose making it answer.
