“Again, on the other side are two officers whose presence here is as grateful to my feelings as that of any of their colleagues, inasmuch as they represent the Army of the United States, a country which I had often occasion to visit when Governor-General of Canada, and whose border I never passed without experiencing at the hands of its inhabitants such an amount of kindness and hospitality as it would be impossible for me to forget.”

[[break]]

Before closing I wish to add my testimony to the personal magnetism of General Roberts, the Commander-in-Chief. I listened to the brief and extemporaneous dinner speech welcoming the foreign officers, and it seemed so happy, so easily delivered, and so telling, that I regretted seeing him sit down. He is known among the troops as “Our Bobs,” and I can readily understand how it is that I hear from every quarter that they are greatly attached and ready to go wherever he leads.
