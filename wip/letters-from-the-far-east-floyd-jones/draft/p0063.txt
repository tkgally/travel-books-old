Among the numerous tempting dishes that were served our “national bird” was conspicuous, Of course, we drank to sweethearts, wives and absent friends. It was a late hour when the party broke up.

I found Paris exceedingly neat and clean—at least it was so in the parts I visited. The streets are in admirable condition, and many are now paved with wood—this having been laid down within the past three years. It seems to stand the heavy traffic well, as I saw no holes or broken places; but it remains to be seen if this style of pavement lasts. It seems, however, to be a success in London, where it has been down some years longer.

Coming from Paris to this city one has a choice of routes. He may reach here either by way of the Mont-Cenis Tunnel, which route passes through Macon, Aix-les-bains and Modene, Turin and Milan, or by the St. Gothard Tunnel, which takes you by Basle, Lucerne, Bellinzona and Milan.
