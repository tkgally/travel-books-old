This I was told, was due in a measure to the stubble fields—most of the oat crop having been recently gathered—but is mainly owing to the very dry season, which has been unprecedented throughout many parts of Ireland.

We have still nearly two hundred and forty miles to make before reaching Liverpool, but, as our voyage is practically over, I bring my letter to a close. You may hear from me again somewhere in England; till then I say <i>au revoir</i>.
