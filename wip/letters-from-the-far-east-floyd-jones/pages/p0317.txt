Judge Grant, as he is known in this section—a man of over seventy-three years—came out to welcome us, and busied himself about our various needs—such as showing us the washroom, taking our hats, dusting us off—in fact, filling the position of both host and helper. He also assisted at table during dinner, and was ready to answer the numerous questions propounded by curious travellers. I am thus minute in my details concerning our host, as we learned afterwards that he was a man of large wealth—variously estimated at from a million upward—and had chosen this spot to end his days in—believing the climate of the country would add a few more years to his present three score and twelve.
