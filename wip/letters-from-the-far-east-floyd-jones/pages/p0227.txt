[[subtitle]] Hong Kong to Shanghai.

[[right]] <sc>stmr. “melbourne,” eastern sea, nearing shanghai, temperature 55°.,</sc>

[[right]] <i>March 25th 1886.</i>

FROM the caption of this you will learn I'm now traveling in a French steamer, my first experience in this long Eastern tour. Three times have I tried the Peninsular and Oriental boats; once the Austrian Lloyds, and now a Messagerie; and I'm bound to admit that I find this ship exceedingly comfortable. The hours of meals are not as congenial to my taste, but the food, combined with the mode of serving, is the best I've yet found, and in point of speed this French boat is much the faster. I also discover that many of the most cosmopolitan class of travelers in this Eastern land give the French line the preference.
