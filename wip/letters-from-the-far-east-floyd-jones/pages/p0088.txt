Should he go to the body and lick the forehead it is regarded as a good omen and evidence that the departed is enjoying Paradise; but on the other hand, should he refuse, the decree has been purgatory. A procession is then formed of the friends of the deceased, preceded by the priests, who convey the body on a bier to the “Towers of Silence.” Only the male relatives attend, and they are clothed in white and follow the corpse in pairs, each pair holding a white handkerchief between them. Having arrived at the main gate of the enclosure, the body is taken in charge of by another set of priests who carry the body to the tower intended for its reception. These towers, five in number, differ somewhat in size—the largest being about 270 feet in circumference and some 40 feet in height. The interior of each tower, which is open to the sky, is covered at a height of ten feet from the ground. with a circular-flooring, which slopes towards a central well and contains numerous open grooves on which the bodies are laid. The outer ring of this flooring, or one nearest the inner wall of the tower, is appropriated for men, and signifies good actions; the second ring, signifying good words, is for women, and the inner ring, meaning good thoughts, for children.
