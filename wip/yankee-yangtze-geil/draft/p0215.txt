[[image: Coffin boards leaning against a ledge in a street, with carved wooden spirit shrines and hanging bundles of paper ingots]]
[[caption]] A Street Scene in Sui-Fu, Near the Gospel Hall. Note the Coffins and Spirit Shrines in which the spirits of the departed are supposed to dwell for three years after death, when these wooden houses are taken to the grave and burnt, thus liberating the spirit, who at once takes its departure to the Realms of Rest. The name of the deceased is written in gold on a tablet, and placed inside the shrine. The bundles hanging up are ingots of gold and silver made in paper and burned at the graveside to supply the deceased with money in the regions beyond.
