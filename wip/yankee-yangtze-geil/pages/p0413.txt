# Chapter XXII.

[[subtitle]] Myothit—A Reckless Cook—Bhamo, the City of Pots—Missions in Bhamo—Burmese Nats—The Shans—Bound for Mandalay.

[[center]] 大人不見小人過

[[center]] A great man will not see a little man’s faults.

[[caption]] Sculling.

AT Nampoung the coolies had no pukais, therefore they were up and had their early rice before our black pot with rice gruel made its appearance at four-thirty <sc>a.m.</sc> The moon was shining clearly in a bright sky. I had left Nampoung Rest-House by daylight and moonlight mixed. The baggage went out through the “back door,” which is a rear exit through the split bamboo fence. The chairs left at the other end by a short, steep, zig-zag path up to a splendid road. The rank vegetation of the mountain was being cleared from the road two hundred feet on either side. This is done at Government expense. Frequently I saw evidences of the fantastic worship of the Shans—notched posts and long poles bearing split bamboo designs being the most common.
