[[cont]]located, crossing a narrow valley, ascending and descending another slight eminence and you are upon the northern side of the island, where is as fine a spot for surf-bathing as can be found anywhere in the world. To many of us surf-bathing is a novelty and at first we got roughly handled by the breakers, but the semi-daily exercise of our week’s stay here has made us nearly as expert as the oldest surf-bathers.

As I said in the forepart of my letter the benefits are plainly visible in the crew, and there has not been a more pleasant period in the cruise. I have no idea how much longer we shall remain here; but hope that our next port will be Hong Kong, there to fit out for the homeward bound passage. Even for that, the crew are not anxious to bid Pootoo “good-bye,” so much are they charmed by this lovely spot, and so pleasantly and swiftly do the days of our stay here pass by.
