To-morrow we leave here for Cape Town, so that to-night we will look upon the lighting up of the convents on the hills, and of the churches on the oblong plain beneath, of rockets, and other signs of a religious holiday; hear the sweet sounds from the bands of the English, French, and Portuguese frigates near us, and hear the many other evening attractions and pleasures of our stay at Rio, for the last time in many months or years.
