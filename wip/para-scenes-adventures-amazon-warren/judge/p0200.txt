It was evening when our schooner parted from its picturesque moorings, and noiselessly floated down the stream, by the magic light of ten thousand stars. Overpowered with fatigue, I finally threw myself into my hammock, and soon sank into a delicious repose. When morning came not a trace of land was to be seen; a wide waste of untroubled waters was before and around us! * * * Our voyage was a pleasant one, and, on the evening of the fifth day, our vessel was once more riding snugly at anchor in the commodious harbor of Para.
