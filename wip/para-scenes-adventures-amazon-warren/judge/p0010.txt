[[cont]]a murmur of civilization breaks upon the prevailing solitude! The gigantic forests are inhabited by tribes of savage Indians, birds of the most brilliant plumage, and serpents of innumerable varieties and enormous size. With truth therefore, may it be said, that

<verse>
“Wildly here without control,
Nature reigns and rules the whole.”
</verse>

<i>New York, May</i> 10, 1851.
