# Para.

# Chapter I.
[[subtitle]] Evening on the Amazon—The Harbor of Para—Promiscuous Bathing—A Brazilian Dinner—Beautiful Garden—Manufacture of India Rubber Shoes—First Night Ashore.

T<sc>he</sc> shades of evening were gathering fast upon the waters, when the little bark, in which we had safely crossed the wide expanse of ocean, now quietly anchored in the mighty river of the Amazons.

Through the rich twilight we were able to discern the white sandy shore, skirting a dense forest of perennial luxuriance and beauty. Gentle zephyrs, fraught with the most delightful fragrance from the wilderness of flowers, softly saluted our senses; while occasionally the soft and plaintive voices of southern nightingales came with mellowed sweetness to our ears.
