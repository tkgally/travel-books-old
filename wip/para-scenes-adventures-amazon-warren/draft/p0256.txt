Having acquired a sufficiency of strength, he commenced a slow ascent of the bank; as my dry ammunition was now exhausted, it was out of my power to offer him further molestation, therefore he stole on through the bushes with safety, and to my great chagrin and mortification eventually disappeared amid the shrubbery!

Dejected and sorrowful I turned my canoe, and began to retrace my winding course towards L’Aranjeira. “What!” I ruminated, “will J. say when I tell him of my encounter with the snake? What a capital handle it will be for the quizzing humor of my friend W. at the mills! Will they not say that my optics were egregiously imposed upon, and that they did not believe the snake was more than five or six feet in length (if, indeed, I saw any), but that the fear and excitement I was under at the time, magnified him to quadruple his actual size?” This I was confident would be said; and if ever an Indian regretted the loss of an enemy’s scalp, so did I mourn for the loss of that huge and valuable snake!
