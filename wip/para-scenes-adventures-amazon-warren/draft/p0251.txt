# Chapter XXIV.

[[subtitle]] Solitary Canoe Excursion.—Adventure with an Enormous Snake.—Return to the Mills.—The fatal End of a Somnambulist.—An extraordinary Marriage.—Invitation to visit “Vigia.”

L<sc>eaving</sc> L’Aranjeira, I proceeded slowly up the stream. The day was beautiful, and the air so sublimated with the burning sunlight, that even the smallest object shone amid the deep green of the foliage with picture-like distinctness; scarcely a flower, or tree, or plant, which gemmed the banks, escaped my observation. The sky above me was like that of Italy, pure and translucent, and so etherial and cloudless as to exhaust the human vision, which seeks in vain for something upon which to concentrate its scattered rays. A few insects floated about in the clear atmosphere; but they were the only signs of life that were manifest. The birds had retired to their shady bowers in the thickets, but at intervals I caught hasty glimpses of their gay plumes through the interstices of the thickly matted foliage.
