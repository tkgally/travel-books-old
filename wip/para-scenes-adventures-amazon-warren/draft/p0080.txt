But we will not dwell upon the incidents of this Festa. Suffice it to say, that for two weeks nothing is known but gaiety and pleasure, at the expiration of which time it is brought to its termination.

Although this extraordinary festival usually passes by without any serious accidents or public disturbances, yet it is much to be questioned whether it exerts any other than a debasing tendency upon the morals of the people.
