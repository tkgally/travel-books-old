Our transport had but recently left the dock at the Union Works, San Francisco, and was in fine sailing trim. It seemed but a little while, as we stood upon the aft deck, before nothing remained to the vision of the three tragic figures in the legend of Mariveles but a faint outline of the grim old island of Corregidor with her bristling batteries of twelve-inch guns.

Although the two lonely rocks on the right and left of the ancient alderman had gradually melted away in the distance, the posted visitor who leaves the Bay of Manila could not fail to carry away sad recollections of the unfortunate young priest and nun who died with unrequited love three centuries ago.
