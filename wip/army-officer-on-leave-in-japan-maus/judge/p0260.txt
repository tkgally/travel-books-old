“In front of the temple of the benign goddess stands a shrine presided over by Tema who holds the keys of Hades. Occasionally the noise and din which one hears in passing result from the howls and the drum-beating of the fanatical priests, who at that particular time have removed the lid of the infernal regions to give the occupants a whiff of fresh air.

“Beyond the valley on the opposite hill you will see the Koyokwan or the Maple Club, noted for its excellent Japanese dinners and attractive geisha girls, and a little farther on is the English church of St. Andrew.”
