BEFORE leaving Manila my army friends had induced me to purchase one of those comfortable bamboo steamer-chairs made by the Chinese and known as the Hong Kong chair. Although the voyage was to last but four days the purchase proved to be an excellent investment.

With the Hong Kong chair, travel at sea is made easy, and more than that, it becomes a positive luxury. It effaces time and distance and by its hypnotic influence, lulls the seasick traveller into the enchanted land of <i>Sans-Souci.</i>

My friends, it is a wonderful institution, a <i>multum in parvo</i> and the need of every family. With it alone housekeeping is made easy and further furniture unnecessary. It is so cunningly arranged that the good housewife can use it for a serving-table, a bookcase, a couch, a rostrum from which to administer Caudle lectures to her husband, a crib, a playhouse for the children, a place where the cook can entertain her gentlemen friends, and a dozen other useful purposes.
