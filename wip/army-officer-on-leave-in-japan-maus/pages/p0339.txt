“IN order to obtain a clear idea,” said the Major, “of the causes which led to the Russo-Japanese War, it will be necessary to refer to the Boxer Insurrection of 1900. Although a number of misguided people, opposed to the missionary propaganda of the Christian churches, insist that the missionaries excited the movement, the world knows that the Boxer trouble was initiated by the ultra Conservatives, or Know Nothing Party of the Flowery Kingdom, whose slogan was ‘China for the Chinese.’ Under the Tokugawas, a similar party existed in Japan, from the beginning of the seventeenth century and until Commodore Perry unsealed the country in 1853 through the invincible argument of an American battle-ship fleet.
