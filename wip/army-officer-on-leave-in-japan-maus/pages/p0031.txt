I HAD just finished my after-breakfast cigar and was awaiting a friend in the lobby of the Palace Hotel, San Francisco, one morning last August, when my attention was attracted by a telegraph boy who approached me with the familiar yellow envelope of the Western Union. It contained a despatch from Washington directing me to proceed by the first available transportation to Manila, on important business connected with the Government, adding that full instructions had been forwarded by mail.
