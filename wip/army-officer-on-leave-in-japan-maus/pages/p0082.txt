“COME, Major,” said the Judge, “let us move forward on the upper deck with Mr. Rhodes and point out to him the interesting points along the coast <i>en route</i> to Mariveles.”

The day was clear and balmy and the sun shining as brightly as on a June day in Arizona, although we were in the midst of the rainy season.
