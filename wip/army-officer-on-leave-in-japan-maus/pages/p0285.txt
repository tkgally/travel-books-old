THE day following our visit to Tokio and the Tombs of the Forty-Seven Ronin we left on an early train for Nik-ko, the crowning necropolis of Nippon’s greatest dead, which was reached after a pleasant journey of eight hours. The road runs almost directly north from Tokio and follows closely the Old Highway which for centuries past has been threaded by countless processions of holy pilgrims, <i>en route</i> to worship at the royal tombs.
