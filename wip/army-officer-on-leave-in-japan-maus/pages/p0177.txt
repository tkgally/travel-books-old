I sincerely believe, Mr. Rhodes, that had Catholicism never gotten a foothold in the Philippines the natives there would have been as far advanced along the lines of civilization as pagan Japan is to-day.”

“Do you believe Christianity retards the civilization of a pagan nation, Judge?” asked the Major.

“By no means,” replied the Judge. “I believe that modern Christianity has done more for civilization in the Orient than every other factor combined, but when I say Christianity, I refer to the principles enunciated by Christ on Calvary and not to the dogmas conceived by ambitious and unscrupulous men in order to acquire wealth and to gain control over men and governments.”
