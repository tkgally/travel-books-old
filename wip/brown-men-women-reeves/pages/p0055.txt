<blockquote>
[[cont]]gold and silver is everywhere. I am taking down a thousand pounds’ worth of silver with me this trip. I wasn’t going to pay any forty-eight dollars, not likely! So I said to my five Solomon Islanders, ‘Go and find my horse’ (it was removed from the pound for security), ‘and bring it here, no matter where you find it’; and I went to bed.

“In the middle of the night I heard an awful row, and getting up I saw about two hundred Tongans outside the house, and my horse, as I thought, in the yard; so I went down. Here were my five Solomon boys defending the yard. They were covered with wounds and bruises, but they had beaten off the whole two hundred Tongans! I had great trouble to make peace. It appears my boys, seeing a horse exactly like mine in a native village, had seized it, and the whole village, aroused at the theft of their horse, had followed.
</blockquote>
