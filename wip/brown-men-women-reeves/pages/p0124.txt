Much may be due to the example of the pure, simple, private life and character of early missionaries <i>as men</i>, and to the influence they obtained by their skill in medicine and in the use of tools, and by the new inventions they brought, which were so wonderful that to ignorant, superstitious savages all white people seemed as gods.
