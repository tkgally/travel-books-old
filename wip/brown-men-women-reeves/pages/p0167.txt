In the next piece an imitation baby, made of <i>faow</i>, is supposed to be dying. The audience screams with delight, “Mahlia! mahlia!” The baby dies suddenly. Without any ceremony in the remotest form representing grief, a cloth is put over it, and the native audience laughs still more. A funeral dirge is then sung while the baby is being buried.
