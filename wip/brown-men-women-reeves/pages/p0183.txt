In the Divorce Court damages were at first allowed to the amount of £25 against co-respondents. But this regulation had to be repealed, for proceedings to obtain such a large sum (to a native) as £25 in such an easy way (from a Fijian point of view) became quite a business. It would be well were the similar law of Tonga repealed.
