A great authority on Melanesian matters gives interesting samples of savage music notated by himself, and states,[[fnref 1]] “Tous les peuples civilisées ou sauvages de l’Oceanie aiment passionément la musique.” With all due deference to Rienzi, I prefer to agree with Mr. Whitcombe of Rotumah, and with the French bandmaster of Tahiti.
