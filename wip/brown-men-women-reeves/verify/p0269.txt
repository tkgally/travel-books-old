The dispute between B and P raged bitterly, but in the end the Catholics, who probably were etymologically right, got Mr. Baker, the Missionary-Premier of King George, on their side, and he carried a resolution of Parliament in Tonga authorizing the introduction (or, as some contend, the reintroduction) of the letter P into the Tongan language and into Government papers and books. Some rival missionaries to this day use only B, and deny the necessity of P. For instance, Roman Catholic missionaries always write Togatapu; some Protestants, always Togatabu (g is sounded ng). That resolution of Parliament is unique in history.
