The government is carried on by <i>Josaleki Toga</i> (pronounced Tonga, not Tong-ga) <i>Verkune,</i> prime minister to the king. I was introduced by Mr. Whitcombe, his secretary, an Englishman of considerable influence, to this official—a fine old courteous Tongan gentleman, and a much more important person than the king.
