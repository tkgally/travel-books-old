The whole service is gone through in a sitting posture, heads merely bent down when the minister prays—a sedate congregation on plain wooden pews all across the floor of the church—but not an impassive one. When the minister, who speaks fluently in the native language and seems much in earnest, smiles, and as I guess from his manner makes a humorous or sarcastic sally, an audible, murmuring laugh goes round the whole church, and I think what an encouragement such softly decorous demonstrations must be to the preacher. There is perfect silence while prayer is being made, but the long sermon is relieved by a constant murmur of voices talking low.
