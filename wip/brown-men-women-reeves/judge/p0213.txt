Rarotonga is in holiday mood and high fashion just now, for a queen of Samoa is on a visit to Makea, and with her retinue is being fêted at one of the villages with much beating of drums and ceremony of gifts. This great lady, Tui Ariki, wife of Malietoa, arrived last week by the missionary steamer <i>John Williams.</i> She is rather good-looking, but I am sorry to say would not allow herself to be photographed.
