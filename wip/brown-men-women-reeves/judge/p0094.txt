A native pilot joins us in the roadstead, takes us through a narrow channel, and brings the steamer to an anchor inside the reefs looking on to the little township in the island of Lifuka. The native pilot’s office is a sinecure. Very important he looks, evidently blissfully unconscious that our captain could do better without him!

[[caption]] The Papaw (Mummy-apple) Tree.

Lifuka is a flat, low coral island about a mile to two miles wide, and five to seven miles long. Although much smaller than Tongatabu, the Haapai Group is much more densely populated, containing 5168 inhabitants, while Tongatabu has only 7126. Lifuka is one mass of cocoanut, banana, pine-apple, mummy-apple, bread-fruit, mango, chestnut—the nuts as big as halfpenny buns!—and many strange trees,[[fnref 1]]
