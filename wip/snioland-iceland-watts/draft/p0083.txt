Again, nothing is more probable, if these lakes are craters at all, that they, like Katlugia, for lengthened periods, if not at all periods of eruption, gave out only scalding water, mud, and sand. Our guide informed us that there used to be fish in Gœsir Vatn, as he called the lake in the depression of Gœsir Tindr, but that now there are none. I could not learn the date at which they ceased to exist.
