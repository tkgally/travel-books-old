# Preface.

I <sc>have</sc> three objects in publishing the few following pages:—

First—It is my wish to direct the attention of the travelling public to a land (but little beyond the beaten track of tourists, and within the limit of a six weeks’ holiday) that abounds in natural phenomena of equal interest to the geologist and the physical geographer.

Secondly—To inform habitual travellers, many of whom have already visited Iceland, that in that island are tracts of land and mountain solitudes which have never been explored; and to remind them that amongst these untrodden fastnesses have occurred some of the mightiest phenomena the world has ever witnessed, of which we know nothing certain, either of their birthplace or their character, and scarcely anything of their effects.
