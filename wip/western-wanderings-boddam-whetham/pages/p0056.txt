# Chapter IV.

[[subtitle]] Chicago to Omaha.

[[subtitle]] Trials—Enterprise—A moving story—Pig-sticking—New version of one of the labours of Hercules—Hotel life <i>versus</i> home life—To the Mississippi—Reserve—Iowa—Farmers' granges—A bad track—Council Bluffs—Baths—The Missouri.

C<sc>hicago</sc>, within little more than the last two years, has been most sorely tried. The first disaster was the great fire, which burnt down about two-thirds of the city; in the following year business was almost suspended because of the raging epizootic; and, more recently, an almost unparalleled financial collapse has occurred, bringing with it distress and ruin. Phœnix-like, she rose from the ashes of the conflagration in an incredibly short space of time, and with handsomer buildings and more magnificent warehouses even than before. The vitality of Chicago is remarkable, and we may form in this city a correct idea of the wealth and prosperity of a great nation.
