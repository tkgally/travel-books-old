Anderson had been closely watching for this, and tried to save himself by dropping on the ground. Too late, however, as the bullet struck him in the abdomen, and he, like M’Cluskey, was now a dying man. By a last supreme effort M’Cluskey drew his knife, and feebly tried to crawl in the direction of his enemy. The latter managed to raise himself to a sitting posture, and prepared to meet him. Both had dropped their revolvers, and Anderson was unable to move any portion of his body except his right arm. With this he raised his knife, and as M’Cluskey crawled up within reach dealt him a terrible blow in the neck, half-severing the head from the body. The great effort caused him to pitch forward on his face, leaving the knife sticking in the wound.

Everyone supposed that the blow must instantly have killed M’Cluskey, yet before falling he twice plunged his own knife into the body of Anderson. The dead bodies, firmly locked in each other’s embrace, were taken to a house and laid out side by side on a gaming-table, and in a few hours after were removed for burial.
