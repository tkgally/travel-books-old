Tunnels and snow-sheds now begin to appear, and continually shut out from view the finest scenery along the road. Just before reaching Summit Station we caught a glorious view of Donner Lake, which lay three miles below us, and which is infinitely more picturesque when seen from a distant height than it looks when standing at its edge. When we saw it, the morning sun had just spread a glittering sheen over the lake, leaving some corners hidden in deep shadow, which presented the strangest contrasts of sombre purple hues. The soft yellow tints of early morning enveloped the north side, whilst the eastern slopes were shaded by a dark purple haze, wonderfully contrasting with the brightness of the sun and the lake. From our distant point of view, it looked deserving of its name—‘The Gem of the Sierras.’
