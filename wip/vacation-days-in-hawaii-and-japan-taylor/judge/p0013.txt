To every traveler in a strange land is given an individual experience, which, joined to the impressions of other travelers, helps to make a true picture of that country—and so I add my mite to the many volumes already written, feeling that much still remains unsaid regarding the customs, habits, manners, temperaments, and traditions of these most interesting people.
