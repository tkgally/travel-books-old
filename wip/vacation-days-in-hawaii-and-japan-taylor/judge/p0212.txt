Here, too, is Iwa-fune-san (Iwa, rock, fune, boat, and san, mountain), named from the shape of its peaks, which are like a huge boat, from stem to stern. In the distance, on our right is Ikao mountain, not least in this region, where all is interesting and beautiful. Nearer may be seen Miyogi-san, or “rocky mountain,” rugged and picturesque, with pointed cliffs, great arches, and seeming castles that inspire one with a longing for the artist’s brush and the poet’s pen to carry these wonderful scenes to all the countries in the world.
