[[cont]]cipal characteristics of the islands we are about to visit. The Hawaiian Islands are situated in the west Pacific Ocean. They lie between 18° 50′ and 22° 20′ north latitude, between 154° 53′ and 160° 15′ west longitude from Greenwich. The islands are eight in number: Hawaii, Maui, Oahu, Kanai, Malokai, Lanai, Niihau, and Kahoolawe. The three latter are comparatively unimportant. This group was named the Sandwich Islands on their discovery by Captain Cook, in honor of his patron, the Earl of Sandwich, then First Lord of the Admiralty.
