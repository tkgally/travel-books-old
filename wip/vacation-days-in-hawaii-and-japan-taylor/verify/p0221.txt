Last evening my guide received a call from a gentleman cousin and a pretty young niece, who gave us both a warm invitation to visit their home in a village some distance away. This village is also the birthplace of my guide. This is an opportunity for which I have longed. The people are educated country people, in comfortable circumstances. When they bade us farewell, they presented me with a handsome box of bon-bons, exquisitely wrapped, with the daintiness for which this nation is so celebrated. I am informed that this custom of presenting gifts to strangers is quite common.
