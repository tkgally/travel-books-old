[[cont]]indigo, tobacco, and sugar-cane are successfully raised; also yams, sweet potatoes, and arrow root.

The forests are usually very dense, broken here and there by deep chasms, which appear to have been once active craters. The trees are overgrown with mosses and ferns, which render penetration almost impossible.

[[caption]] Hawaiian Hotel, Honolulu.

Wild geese, ducks, plover, hogs, dogs, steers, and wild horses abound in the islands.

The climate is salubrious, and the temperature even. During twelve years the greatest heat in the shade was ninety degrees, greatest cold fifty-four. The mean temperature is about seventy-five.
