Our first meal in this strange country is breakfast. The tables are filled with native fruits, such as alligator pears, bananas, pineapples, apples, and last, but not least, the universal dish called “poi.”

[[image: Photograph of a taro field with rows of broad-leaved plants standing in water, trees and buildings in the distance]]
[[caption]] Taro Patch, Honolulu.
