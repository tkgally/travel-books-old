We walk about the temple grounds. In the spacious courtyard, in front of the Daibutsu, we see an ancient bronze lantern, carved in open work with Buddhist images and conventional animals. It was executed, it is said, by a Chinese artist of the eighth century, and is not only the finest, but the earliest specimen of such work in existence.
