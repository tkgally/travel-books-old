[[cont]]Gingoro, also a well executed group of monkeys, called Koshin. This group represents a blind monkey, a deaf monkey, and a dumb monkey, and is symbolic of the Japanese principle, never to see, hear, or speak evil of any one. Throughout Japan you will everywhere see slabs of stone and wood with this group of monkeys in relief upon them.
