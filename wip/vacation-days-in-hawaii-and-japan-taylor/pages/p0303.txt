[[cont]]news gives us great pleasure, and we soon find ourselves in the presence of the first master of this art in the city. The contestants are seated in a small room about ten feet square, waiting to be called to the arena. This arena is a space twenty feet by forty, with rough seats facing it and an awning overhead to protect us from the sun. Here we are entertained for an hour and a half. Japanese fencing is quite different from that taught in the Italian or French schools.
