[[cont]]Upon its summit is the inactive crater Mokuaweoweo, which was last in operation April 24th, 1896. On the side of the mountain, four thousand feet above sea level, are the craters Kilauea and Halemaumau, the former inactive, the latter in operation.

From the rear of the hotel can be seen Mauna Kea, the highest mountain upon the Hawaiian group. It rises heavenward thirteen thousand eight hundred and five feet.
