Kyoto is a city of two hundred and fifty thousand inhabitants, and is famous for its pottery and porcelain, its fine embroideries, its velvets and brocades, its bronzes and its cloisonnés; also for its many temples, for there are at least a hundred and fifty of no mean size in the city.
