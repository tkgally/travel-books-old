There are about a hundred sampans, or shore boats, around the steamer; they are rowed by one or two oarsmen, who, by hideous yells, make known their mission, which is to carry baggage or steerage passengers to the shore. Many accept, at a small cost, rather than wait for the steamer's barge. The hotel launch lies beside the “China,” and when we have said farewell to our friends, we step upon it and steam to the wharf.
