We are shown to a box on the second floor, from which we view the strange mass of human beings. The performance is poor, compared with those of our great Barnum or Forepaugh, the most famous shows in the world. The lower floor is bare of everything save the leather cushions that can be had for one sen, in addition to the admission fee, which is three sen in this part of the house.
