We have a very good lunch at the Foreign Hotel, the only one in this section and the first one of the kind built in Tokyo. We then visit the Zoological Gardens close by, where we see the usual elephants, lions, tigers, monkeys, etc. The gardens are attractive but the number of animals is small.
