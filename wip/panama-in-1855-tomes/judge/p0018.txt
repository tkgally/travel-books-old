An interview with the representative of the Board of Directors followed, and I looked upon this gentleman—the impersonation of the Great Railroad Company, in whom was concentrated the gigantic hospitality of which I had been invited to partake—with a sentiment of sublime respect, not unmingled with a substantial sense of satisfaction, especially as he confirmed, word for word, all my friend’s glowing description of the rounds of beef, and the dozens of Champagne in prospect.
