Along the narrow street some of us go, out into the Plaza, bare and desert-like, and see here and there a shackled mule cropping the parched grass, a group of naked Negro children playing upon the steps of the great ruined church, and a line of galley slaves clanking their manacles, as with sodden looks and lingering steps they are driven by the armed Mulatto guard to the prison hard by, through the iron bars of which, and from the darkness within, start out the glaring eye-balls of an ugly-looking Negro fellow, who, we congratulate ourselves, is well secured.
