I say, steward, a glass, some iced water, and that——;” with which Nos. 2 and 3, protesting mildly at first, soon learned to conform. When that infernal gong ceased to roar, No. 1, having dismissed the steward, and smacked his lips with the satisfaction of a man who had fulfilled the first duty of the day, tumbled out, went through the absurd practice of shaving his beard and cutting his face, dressed himself, and proceeded to ventilate on deck. No. 2, then poising himself upon the rim of his shelf, and waiting anxiously for a convenient pitch of the ship, also tumbled out, and securing his back against the berth, his abdomen (the polite appellation we believe) against the wash-stand, propping his starboard with the baggage, and his port-side with the door, went through the small ablutions and meagre course of toilet to which the traveler by sea has to resign himself. Thus began the second day of the voyage.
