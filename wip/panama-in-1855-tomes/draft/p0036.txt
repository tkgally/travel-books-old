The Colonel had a magnificent brace of silver-plated Colt’s revolvers, which he was fond of exhibiting, and of which, it is hoped, his bellicose qualities will not lead him to any dangerous usage. He is not as highly valued in Panama as such a representative of our country should be, probably because the rude Border style of the man falls somewhat short, as it must be confessed, of the high standard of Castilian manners.
