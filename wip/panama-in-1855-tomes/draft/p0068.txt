[[cont]]following washing bill, which will show that a fair day's labor receives a fair day's wages in Aspinwall:

[[right]] <sc>Aspinwall</sc>.

[[center]] To <i>Charlotte Miles.</i>

<i>February</i> 23, 1855.

| To washing twenty-four pieces wearing apparel at 20 cents each | $4 80 |

Charlotte Miles, whom I had never the pleasure of seeing in person, as she did her business with me through her negro proxy, Sam of the Mess House, might have become the owner in perpetuity of my stockings, for washing each one of which she charged twenty cents, at the tenth of the sum a pair, and she would have had no bargain at that. Charlotte Miles having received her money, I prepared to leave Aspinwall for Panama.
