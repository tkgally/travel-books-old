Captain S—— of our party, who prided himself upon his gallantry, soon crowded himself alongside of this Isthmian belle, and in a very few minutes the gallant skipper had more than his fair share of the seat, while his companion grinned her white teeth, and wagged her tails of wool with infinite laughter. The Captain insisted afterward, confidentially, that it was a decided conquest; but I shrewdly suspect that it was the worthy navigator's Spanish, which was not certainly of the purest Castilian, and not his personal attractions, or his wit, which had almost dislocated those grinning jaws and shaken off those tails of wool.
