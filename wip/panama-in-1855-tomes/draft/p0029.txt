If he is at all curious—as I do not recommend him to be, provided he values his ease of mind—he will find, if he has had any hope in those tin things, he has been a victim of misplaced confidence, and will see that, although like a millstone about the neck, they might assist in expediting the agony, they can not prevent it. Let him console himself then with the joke, as my friend M—— did, that those who go to sea preserve the flesh of human creatures in tin-cans, as they do that of other beasts!
