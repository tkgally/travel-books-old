As the night advances, the streets become emptied, and are left to silence; and the moon, glowing in those tropical latitudes almost with the glare of a noonday sun, brightens up the houses opposite with a golden light, and throws the shadow of the balcony of the Aspinwall House upon the pavement, with the distinct outline of a drawing. Yet late in the night, the clattering of the billiard-balls, and the clinking of glasses in the bar-room below, are distinctly heard, which somewhat disturb the pleasant thoughts of Gil Blas and his romantic companions, the licentiates, caballeros, and señoritas of Madrid, Salamanca, and Toledo, suggested by the tingling of a guitar from beneath the balcony opposite. With a sleepy confusion of the old and the new, I turn into my cot, and sleep until morning.
