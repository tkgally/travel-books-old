At Gatun station the railroad comes close to the right bank of the Chagres, and keeps it for twelve miles or so, during which there is an occasional view of the tortuous course of the river, always upon the right of the traveler facing Panama. From the Gatun station we saw the native village of that name, with its huts scattered on the opposite bank of the Chagres, facing the mouth of the river Gatun.
