# Chapter III.

[[subtitle]] Ride on the Railroad.

[[image: Map showing the line of the Panama Rail-road from Aspinwall to Panama, with a scale of miles]]

I WAS pleased when it was announced, in accordance with the projected programme of the entertainment of our party on the Isthmus, that we were to take an early departure from that plague-spot, Aspinwall. That disease and premature death were endemic in that region of swamp and vegetable rottenness, was quite evident without any minute investigation
