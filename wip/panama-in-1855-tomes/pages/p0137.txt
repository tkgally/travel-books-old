Out of church our unbelieving countrymen pass, perhaps, into the neighboring drinking-saloon, where their patriotism is no doubt warmed by the sight of a spruce bar-keeper in linen jacket from their own native land, compounding a draught for a brother Yankee, whose bilious eyes, yellow face, and shaky hand, tell of the effects of the climate, and oft-repeated calls for bitters. Two meagre youths of native blood are busy at the billiard-table. Some half-dozen Spaniards and Frenchmen are playing dominoes under the porch, and refreshing themselves with beer and absynthe.
