The stout Director, parboiled with the tropical heat, and very perceptibly shrunk in the boiling, had returned from Aspinwall, and was prepared to refresh himself, after his prodigious official labors in connection with the railroad, in the cool breezes of the Pacific. Accordingly, we were off, one fine day, in the steamer <i>Panama</i>, and sailed upon the broad bay of Panama for the Pearl Islands. In addition to our especial party of four, the remnant of the company from New York, there were the engineers of the railroad, some of the steamboat agents and their wives, a doctor or so from the steamers in the harbor, a grave Spanish Don or two, and the British Consul with his daughter, and the young lord in the white hat again.
