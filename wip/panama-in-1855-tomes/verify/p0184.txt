He is a happy mortal, beloved of his simple flock, and an especial favorite of the Taboga women. By a free interpretation of the law of celibacy, or somehow or other, he has contrived to become the father of more than his share of the dark-faced and black-eyed urchins that indiscriminately toddle about the village.
