The pathway soon opens into a freer space, where the tamarind trees extend over a level spot of earth that forms the southern end of the island. Inviting walks stretch winding in every direction through the trees, shaded above by the close intermingling of the green foliage, and lead as it may be to some palm-thatched hut nestling in the grove, or up the green hill into the tangled growth, or to the quiet bay, or down to the roaring sea-shore.

Happy, and careless as to time, we will linger and make a day of it in these ways of pleasantness and of peace.
