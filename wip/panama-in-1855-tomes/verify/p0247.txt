He, early in the voyage, became dissatisfied with the slowness of two-shilling whist, and, confident in his skill at <i>poker</i>, which he confessed to having acquired from some shrewd Yankees at the cost of a thousand pounds, he devoted himself to that tempting game, by which he became a debtor, in the books of some astute Californians, to the amount of several hundreds of dollars. This debt was left unpaid, not from any want of means, for I saw an abundance of money in his possession; and surely not from dishonesty, for it is preposterous to suspect that a lord <i>in esse</i>, and a duke <i>in futuro</i>, would fly from a debt like a runaway rogue.
