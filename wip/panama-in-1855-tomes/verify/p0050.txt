Did not the artful Y—— inform his father, in the brokerage line, that he had better metamorphose himself into a bear? Did not the truthful Z—— tell the public, as he was bound on his conscience to do, the whole truth, and nothing but the truth, and get terribly slandered and berated for having ventured upon so bold a step as to publish facts in the newspaper which employed and paid him for that purpose, which is supposed to be honorable, although it appeared otherwise to the confused conscience of the pure-minded stock-jobbers of Wall Street?

There was but little temptation at the Mess House to do much else than eat, drink, smoke, and sleep; the provision for which, it must be confessed, was of the most excellent kind. The zealous Methodist chaplain had made a praiseworthy effort to improve a taste for reading, by a generous diffusion of tracts on the hall table, with such attractive titles as, “Prepare to Die,” “Will you go to Hell?” and other equally comforting suggestions; but it is feared the heathen only used them to light their cigars, or to serve a less worthy purpose.
