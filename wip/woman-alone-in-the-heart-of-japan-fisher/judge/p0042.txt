## <i>The Kindly Natives</i>

If we are but a little kind to them they are supremely kind to us. One day in the train a wee creature cuddled up on her knees to me and began a voluble output of the lingo. I nodded and grinned like an idiot, but her astonished gaze told me I was unsatisfactory. At last she ventured, “Air you a chreeschin? Me too, me chreeschin.” This is the constant query of the native, and, though I had started out with a confident reply, constant hammering of the question had brought doubts as to my surety, and in despair I sometimes startled the native with the answer, “No, I am an American.”

This little lady fished in the depths of her cavernous sleeves, and intuitively I clapped my hand on my pocket. Why do we distrust the very ones who would befriend us?
