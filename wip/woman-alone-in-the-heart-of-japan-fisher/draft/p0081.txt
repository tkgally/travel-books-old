## <i>Traditions of a Temple Bell</i>

Beneath a tiresome flight of steps, the view stretched out to thatched roofs wrapped in purple and white wistaria, and flashes of colour lay beside the pearly line of road which ran beside the blue lake, whose deep green hills rose like protecting giants by the edge. Descending to the sombre forest, by paths of velvet moss, we sought the monastery bell in the thicket. Generally the tongueless temple bell has its separate home, and stands unmoved till it resounds to the push of the big battering ram which hangs at its side, as the devotee offers up his prayer.
