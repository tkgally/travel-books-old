“What, Heihachee!” I exclaimed, in despair, “you go away with English American gentlemen, and leave me here to fight my way through the woods! You can’t do it. You promised to stay with me, and you must see me through. Bring up the gentlemen and we will settle this.”

He tossed back his head with a laugh and ran away, while I was left guessing as to the doleful situation. Then I learned how mean a thing it is to be suspicious, and how often we misjudge the native who has only our kindly interest at heart. Noble, faithful old Heihachee! How cruelly and brutally I had suspected him! He was simple-hearted as a child, and had only the child’s natural desire to stand well in my esteem and to impress me with his record.
