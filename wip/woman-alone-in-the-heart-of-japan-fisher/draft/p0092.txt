The fat lady from the West here lost her balance, but remarked, “Australia is a bit off the civilized route, if such fairy-tales are credited by its countrymen.”

As we slipped into a foaming maelstrom, and eddied and whirled among towering rocks, all sparring ceased, and the fat lady’s eyes dropped out on her face and her wide mouth stretched as if to take in a tidal wave. Three men at the prow tugged mightily at the oars, pulling themselves up on the cross-beams and straining at the oarlocks. Two men poled us off, as we swirled in the seething caldron. Men in the stern drove their bamboo rods against the huge boulders. A sharp command, a quick retort, the speedy stroke, the strained eye, proved the desperate effort of the men to keep us from the whirling rapids. Bang! broke the waves, and the scow swung around to a monster rock, and took a shipload of water. Swamping or splitting would soon end our troubles in the wild stream.
