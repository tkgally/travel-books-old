Before morning sharp dyspepsia followed on the heels of the nocturnal orgy, and the feasters emitted grunts and groans, while one rioter made a dreamy attack on his neighbour, which the victim evidently resented, and the chorus of wails suggested Welsh rarebit at the banquet board. Before daylight they were all volubly discussing the party, while the midgets ran away with the rolls of quilts. As I trundled back my screens, the neighbours across the way were dramatically waving their angel sleeves, flourishing their towels, and brandishing tooth-brushes. There was no water in the bare room, and every one washed at the public sink in the hall, an immaculate wooden trough, with a brass basin polished to the reflection of a mirror.
