# Chapter VI
[[subtitle]] Alone in Nikko

## <i>Mutual Distrust</i>

A<sc>s</sc> lovely Nikko is the Mecca of the pilgrim and the stamping-ground of the tourist, so is it the restful resort of the weary; and where the trotters swarm for two days of celebration, it was my fortune to linger alone six weeks, to walk and talk with the natives, and many a heart to heart experience was mine.

While wandering among the monarchs of the forest, I met a peasant lad, perhaps of eighteen years, with pack on back, peering from a wall into the realm below. With all a browsing wanderer’s interest in things novel, I climbed the bank to get his point of view. Never had he seen a thing so queer and strange drive straight for him.
