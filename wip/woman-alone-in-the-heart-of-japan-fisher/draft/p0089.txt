Other adventurers had said, “Do not fail to take it. The scenery repays by its charm, even if the season be dry, and the Rapids tame.” Another’s experience is never a sure test, as it is the personal factor which must solve every problem.

I was fortunate to make the shoot in time of heavy freshet. “We have had no telegram to warn us, so we know you can get through, but the waters will be very high and dangerous, it will cost you more, and, if you have the time to wait, you would better delay a day,” was the advice which decided us to “stand not on the order of our going, but go <i>at once.”</i> The following day, the mad waters had abated two feet, and the rousing sport that goes with danger was all lost.
