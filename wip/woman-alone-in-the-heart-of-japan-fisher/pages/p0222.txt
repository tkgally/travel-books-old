These certainly were weak points, not greatly to be praised by the best judges, and, luckily, they were not essentials to bringing us through the woods. Among his valued papers, I found this doggerel:

<verse>
“If you’re in want of a man,
You may search through this lan’,
An’ I trow that right weary you’ll be,
Ere you’re likely to find
One more to your mind
Than Kaidzu Heihachee.”
</verse>

He performed with the dignity and solemnity of an old Roman Senator in control of the empire, and he went through his programme, marking off on his fingers the bill of fare. “Mornin’, Oksan. Wat you haf? Omlet, bifstek, ’am an’ eggs, table hôte.” This was his notion of a French cuisine, this was his menu, and no chef in white cap could have been more serious.
