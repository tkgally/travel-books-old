## <i>A Ceremonious Tea-party</i>

The tea ceremony preceded the dance, and we waited in the anteroom, shod in moccasins and armed with wooden tickets. The usher waved us to the inner shrine, where low stools and lacquer tables lined the walls, and the guests in solemn silence awaited developments.

Mincing, but quiet and dignified, five wee fairies toddled in, each bringing a much-flowered earthen saucer and a pasty ball stabbed with a skewer. She dropped her offering before a guest, bent herself double in salute, and tottered away. Back and forth they flitted, like rainbows running across the carpet, till all were served.
