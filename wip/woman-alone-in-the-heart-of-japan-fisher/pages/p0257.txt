## <i>A Dancing Lesson</i>

The geisha is the public dancer, all will admit, but “dancing,” in our sense, does not exist in Japan. No spinning top reel, or grasshopper jump, with awkward bounce and breathless hurry of the Western world, would ever mark or mar the graceful sweep of the geisha’s movements. Slow lines, easy waves of motion, pretty attitudes, and gentle poses constitute the dance, which is taught and performed individually. One cannot picture two geishas wheeling about in each other’s arms. Old age and homeliness do not shelve a teacher in Japan.
