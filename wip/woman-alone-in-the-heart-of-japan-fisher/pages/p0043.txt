## <i>The Theatre</i>

The theatre is a continuous vaudeville, the delight of the native, where two-cent, three-cent, and five-cent shows keep the people in wild guffaws over the most childish nonsense. Clogs by the hundred rest at the door, and the patron is checked with a billet of wood in exchange for his shoes. The cheapest places are nearest the stage, and the high-priced people are banked in the rear, while the natives squat on mats in front. Flags and banners, dragons and goblins, array the walls.
