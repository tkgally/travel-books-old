## <i>The Red Cross Hospital</i>

A very long and rambling ride from the centre of the limitless city brings one to the wide grounds, whose large buildings were erected twenty years ago. Pest-houses, devoted to infection, are a little removed from the main buildings, which are conspicuous by the emblem of the order. The usher made obsequious recognition of my visitor’s pass, and conducted me to a sad reception-room. A doctor appeared, immaculate in white duck, which contrasted with his swarthy skin, and we conversed in German, as we had no English-Japanese base. Our efforts were pitifully weak, but I tried to resurrect a few phrases, which might match the atrocious wreckage of the little man, who <i>thought</i> he spoke as to the manor born the language of the Fatherland. We waxed eloquent over the tea-cups, which seemed the first step to support me in my general survey.
