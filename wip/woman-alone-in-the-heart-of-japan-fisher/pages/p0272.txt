## <i>Swami Rah Tirth</i>

To the faithful, calling for the purification of the faith, he seemed the bright star in the night, pointing to a resurrection. He was the embodiment of the doctrine. His mind was regarded as a well of wisdom and his life as an open book. Long years of concrete and abstract study, of mathematical and scientific work in the university of Lahore, of occult meditation beneath the snowy Himalayas, had ripened him in knowledge.
