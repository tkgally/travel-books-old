## <i>A Tribute to the Red Cross</i>

What the Red Cross hospital has done for the people of Japan in time of peace was but an earnest of its power upon the battle-field in days of dire disaster. Its spirit and its strength have been terribly tested, and gloriously proved in the late war. A visit to the central home in Tokio, or the record of its work in time of sorest need, leaves supreme the thought of love and reverence for its work, honour and respect for its deep-seated purpose, wherever it lifts its flag and plants its relief corps throughout this needy world. As it helps the poor, comforts the sick, and soothes the dying, glory, honour, power be to its name, since the good which it <i>would</i> is the essence of God Himself.
