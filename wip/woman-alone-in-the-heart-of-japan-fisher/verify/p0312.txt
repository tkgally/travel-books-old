## <i>Tea-firing</i>

Mr. Otani is not a planter. He is simply a merchant who buys the leaf from all corners of the island, and fires, packs, and exports it to all parts of the world. The season yields three crops, and the best comes in about May 10th. Throughout the land the bushes, decked in their glossy green robes, are roofed with bamboo to protect them from bad weather, and the little natives, chiefly women, are busily picking the harvest.

From May till October fires burn and wheels turn in the factory, when everything stops for another season. The working day, of twelve hours, runs from five <sc>a. m.</sc> till six <sc>p. m.</sc>, with an hour’s rest at noon. Mr. Otani employs a hundred workers, men and women, but there is no child labour.
