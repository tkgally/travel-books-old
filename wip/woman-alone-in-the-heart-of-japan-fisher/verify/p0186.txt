In sunshine or in shower Nikko is lovely. The sombre forests have impressed additional dignity on the gentle natives, and the beauty of their character is in keeping with the harmony around them. And when for the last time rikky trundled me down their fascinating village street, everybody seemed to share my grief in going. Gentle voices rang along the way, of brass merchant and curio vendor, of the wooden lady and the furrier, of the toy-seller, the box and basket maker, “Good-bye, Oksan, come back gen, see Nikko nodder time.” Small wonder that their motto reads, “See Nikko, and die.”
