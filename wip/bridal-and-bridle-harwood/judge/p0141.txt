[[cont]]He answered, that it was a fine city, and prettily situated, but the people bad and turbulent.

The Bulgarian rebellion, he told me, was nearly at an end, and I should find tranquillity generally restored throughout the province; and even the mountain villages on our route, he seemed to think, we should find tolerably quiet. At the same time he offered me any escort I pleased, either infantry or cavalry, from Nissa to Sophia. “Take ten men,” he said; “take twenty, thirty, nay, a hundred, if you will: you shall be more than welcome.”
