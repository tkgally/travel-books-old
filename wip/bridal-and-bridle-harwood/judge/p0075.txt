Iovon, the Servian Tartar, was the beau ideal of his roving and nomadic caste. I never shall forget his appearance, nor his strange air of fierce docility, as he first stood before us, silent and motionless, his heavy brow bent, his stately form bowed, and his massive arms hanging down on each side of his vast chest, waiting patiently for orders, in a manner that reminded me of the unmurmuring and tranquil obedience of the mighty elephant.
