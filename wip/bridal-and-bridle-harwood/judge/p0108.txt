At Batuschin, the people were savage and unfriendly. We were housed almost by force, and the natives were consequently in a terrible passion. At Tagodin, Giovanni made us some soup, and came to us complaining bitterly that the Tartar had done his utmost to flavour it with lemons, and upon his resisting the innovation, honest Iovon almost insisted upon substituting instead the yolks of eggs, and could only be induced to desist from his attempts by Giovanni's frantic appeal to us. The Tartar doubtless derived his notions from some Turkish mess, and appeared greatly crestfallen, that his well-meant, though somewhat bizarre attempts to please our European palates, were not appreciated.
