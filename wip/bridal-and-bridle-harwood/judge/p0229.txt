We sat in silent grandeur and dignity, awaiting the moment when we should either be safe on <i>terra firma</i>, or smothered beneath the aruba, in the thick, black, slimy mud of which the river’s bed consisted. Never did Moslemah sit with more composure under the fire of a Giaour battery.
