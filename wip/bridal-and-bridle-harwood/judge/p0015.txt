All the aged are apparently beggars. We found one old man on his knees, at the brow of a hill, and the children pursued us in swarms, holding up their unwashed hands in an imploring attitude, and screaming at the top of their voices for “baccanal” and “schuszanore,” which I take to mean much the same as baksheesh and carità.
