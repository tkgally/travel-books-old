The Suridgee was an old white-bearded Osmanlee, with a venerable face and an awful turban, that looked as if its owner deserved some much more dignified employment than leading a baggage-horse. But the Turkish peasants, whenever you meet them, driving sheep or employod in field labour, look most completely out of place, with their loose, showy, picturesque garb, and fine, bold, noble faces. Besides, they always look as if they felt degraded, as a nomadic and warrior people, by the toils of agriculture.
