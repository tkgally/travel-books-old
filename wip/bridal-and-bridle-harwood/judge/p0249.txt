Between Karestan and Eska-baba I took a short cut across the plain on foot, and was surprised to see the clouds of locusts and grasshoppers that rose at every step. Turks never walk when they can ride, and regard it as an act of utter madness to use one’s own limbs for purposes of volition, when those of any quadruped can be pressed into the service.

Old Suleiman, accordingly, did not at all comprehend my sudden start across the country, under a sun that would soon have cooked a beefsteak: he turned to Emily and piteously shook his grey beard with a most compassionate look, as much as to say, “Ah, poor thing, what will you do now? The Frank Bezzarda is possessed by some evil spirit that is driving him into the sea.”
