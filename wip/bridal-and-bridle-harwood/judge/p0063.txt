But even here myriads of palga followed us, and the martyrdom we suffered was such, that, accustomed as I have since been to the usual Turkish nights’ entertainments, I look back to the voracity of the fleas of that village as to some marvellous and unprecedented exploit in the annals of the blood-sucking world. Add to the rapacity of these countless devourers the buzz of the viewless but keen lanceted mosquito, the invisible surgeon of the air, and you may get a clear idea of our sufferings.
