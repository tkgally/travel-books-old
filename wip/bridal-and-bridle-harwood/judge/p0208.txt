We saw all this from the verandah of the police-station, where we dined, waited on by Iovon and a handsome young Albanian in a white kilt, with silver-covered pistols and bandeliers, and were regaled with chickens and eggs, execrable Turkish coffee, and pure water. Salads are very common at a Moslem repast, and I am sure the Osmanlee look upon a cucumber or a lettuce as we do on a melon or a pine-apple.
