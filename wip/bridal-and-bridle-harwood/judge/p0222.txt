Tatar Bazardchick is a holy city in miniature, crested with numbers of slender minarets, and studded with the round-doomed snowy mosque of the Moslem worship. We were housed in a very large ruinous old Khan, with a painting of St. George or St. Michael over the outer gate. It was pretty full of people, and being built in a semi-circular form, was a fine specimen of the old Caravanserai of the Arabian Nights.
