The costumes of the Contadini grew gradually wilder and stranger: bare-footed women, with long, white cloths bound round their heads, and floating in the wind, and with simple white chemises, or rather blouses, for all other garments, toiled in the fields like so many ghastly spectres, and looked, in the dim grey mist, like so many Ghoules tearing open graves, to furnish forth their hellish banquet. Very few men were seen: they were, in all probability, mostly serving in the army; and those few were indolently lounging by the road-side, smoking huge pipes.
