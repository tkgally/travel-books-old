We were gazed at like wild beasts in their den at feeding time. These things can only be laughed at. <i>Venetian blinds</i> are unknown here!

We gave a bottle of unutterably nasty pink rosolio, bought at Sophia, to the most thoroughly savage of the—I cannot say <i>fair</i>—bevy, and she returned with a bouquet of marigolds as a recompense.
