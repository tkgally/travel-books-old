My whip and spurs were not idle; but my horse was the most tired of the party. although he still exerted himself to clear the trifling ditches in the way, to the best of his ability.

The only light was afforded by the fitful glow of the pale fire-fly, whose bright wings and lustrous lamp shone the clearer for the dim obscurity. It was only very rarely that we could make out the towering form of the Tartar in front of us, whose giant bulk made him far more conspicuous than his companions.
