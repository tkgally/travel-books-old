The Cardinal inhabited the room opposite our own, the only door of which was thick red curtain. Here he smoked his pipe all day on the divan, and dined in company with our servants! If Greek Cardinals make a constant practice of dining with servants, I should be curious to know who the usual companions of their ordinary priests can be.
