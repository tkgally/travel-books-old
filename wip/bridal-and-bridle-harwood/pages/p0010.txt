As far as I can remember, the colloquy that preceded our departure from Paris was pretty much as follows:—

“Shall we go to Turkey, Emily?”

“I am quite ready.”

“Shall we go through Bosnia, and ride to Constantinople?”

“Nothing I should like better.”

And in two days we were off for Marseilles, whence we soon reached Leghorn, and passing rapidly through Pisa, Florence, Bologna, and Padua, sailed from Venice to Trieste, and inquired which was the best route to the Turkish frontier.
