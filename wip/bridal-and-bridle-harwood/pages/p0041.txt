We left Voynich in a rickety kind of old wheel-barrow, drawn by two bad horses, and with a little dirty straw, by way of a cushion; poor Giovanni piteously murmuring, that in Italy, even the prisoners were allowed comfortable carts and clean straw, but that here no such luxuries were to be obtained, even by Forestieri, who could pay for them.

The bridge, near the village, was a fine specimen of primeval architecture: a number of narrow loose planks had been laid across the torrent, so that at every step which the horses took, the cart bounded as if on spring boards.
