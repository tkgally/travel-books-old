Two hours afterwards, we saw the truck which served as a bier standing against a wall for hire, with many other hand-barrows of the same kind, and I speculated upon its next probable destination. The women of the neighbourhood had decked their coal-black hair with the gay flowers they had plucked from the body of the dead. The idea seemed ghastly to me.
