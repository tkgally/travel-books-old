Trusting to the Prophet’s aid, and to the edge of his Koran-lettered yataghan, this turbaned shopkeeper was simply and tranquilly returning to his home at Bihach, Gliouch, savage Glamotab, or even the distant Trawnik. Vainly did rough hills, and wild torrents, and men wilder and ruder still, essay to bar the way to this Osmanlee trader.

In Bulgaria I learned from some intelligent Turkish officers of rank the true character of Bosnia and its people. The Bosnians apostatized, some two centuries ago, to retain their lands, and are mostly now fanatical Mussulmen, though of the Sclave race, and but thinly mixed with the genuine Tartar blood.
