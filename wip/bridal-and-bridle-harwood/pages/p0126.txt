The rain came driving in heavy sheets before the wind, and we kept our umbrellas up the greater part of the ride. Away we went along a road, half mud, half water; crossing streams, and wading pools considerably more than knee deep.

One marshy lagoon we waded must have been half a mile in width, very deep, and much too muddy to enable us to discover the road below. At another time we scrambled along the shingly and slippery banks of a small river, where a trifling slide would have plunged us headlong into the boiling stream below.
