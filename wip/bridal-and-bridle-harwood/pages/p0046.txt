We lost a day; but we secured the horses, by going ourselves to the post-house, where Emily’s German, and my passport, converted the bear into a lamb. We tried to hire a cart for the whole journey, to save time in changing at the post-houses; but the man asked, besides twenty-five florins for the hire, forty more as a deposit for the use of a peculiarly unsafe, open clothes’ basket upon wheels, which we therefore declined; and next endeavoured to purchase a small covered waggon. The price asked was a hundred florins, the real value being about thirty. A broken down old cart was next offered to us at eighty, and I gave up the bargain in disgust.
