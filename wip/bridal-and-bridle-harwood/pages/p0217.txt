The Albanians were the most active troops I had seen in the Ottoman Empire; but they are said to be most undisciplined and disorderly soldiers, hardly amenable to any law whatever.

We were refused shelter from the mid-day sun in a little Turkish village further on, where all the people were followers of Mahomet; but we could not help stopping to dine at the first Christian hut we reached, poor and miserable though it was. We at length reached our sleeping-place, a small hamlet of houses like beehives, lying within hedged and dog-guarded patches of garden-ground.
