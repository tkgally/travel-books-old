The soldier who had taken them, a tall handsome Albanian, with a fine figure, a dark eagle’s face, and the eye of a hawk, sat watching them as he smoked his pipe, with as much composure and satisfaction as a hunter who has bagged his two brace of hares. A strong cavalry guard was near; but as he smoked his chibouque and patted his prancing barb, he seemed fully able to have captured the whole gang single-handed. The police cavalry are generally picked soldiers, brave men and good horsemen, and they often ride out alone to hunt down the Rayah banditti.

The weather was exceedingly hot, and we stopped constantly to drink at the numerous stone fountains that Mahometan piety had erected in the desert waste. These fountains are of inestimable value on a long and weary march.
