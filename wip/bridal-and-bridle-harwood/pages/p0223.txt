We had a tolerably good dinner; but were very much annoyed by some Greek children, in a house whose window commanded ours, and who finished their agression by throwing a brickbat through the window with unerring aim, and much to the disparagement of the dinner equipage. Iovon rushed out into the street, fury in his eyes and a long stick in his hand; but caught no luckless offender to wreak his vengeance upon.
