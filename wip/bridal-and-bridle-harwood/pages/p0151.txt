They must be rich, those Bulgarian peasants, with all their outward show of poverty; for their lands need little tilling to be fruitful and productive, and their wants are few and simple, so I think they may well afford to spare something to the “true believers,” whose Crescent banner, and glittering sabres protect them from more unlicensed marauders.
