Finally, having persuaded the Magyars that he was an Ausonian Captain Dalgetty, he offered to take service with them. Seeing him to be an officer, and a Captain of horse, to boot—they promised him a regiment of Hussars, and left him unguarded. The military Ulysses then flew to the shed, where his English charger stood, leaped on his back, and grasping the halter, galloped quickly off, without saddle or bridle, hotly but vainly chased by the angry dragoons.
