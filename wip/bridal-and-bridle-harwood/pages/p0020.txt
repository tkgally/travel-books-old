All the farmers, who can afford it, wear high boots. The houses are nothing but stone cabins, very much sunk in the earth. We stopped at a post-house, where carriages were not, and where we were obliged to take a light two-horse cart, made of wicker-work, low, open, without seats of any kind, equally destitute of springs, and excessively like a large clothes’ basket on four wheels. I may as well mention, <i>en passant</i>, that these light waggons are the usual style of equipage in Croatia.
