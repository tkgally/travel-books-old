At last the worthy pair consented to give us house-room for the night; and, in fact, once roused to Samaritanism, the good folks grew marvellously civil, and provided us with a comfortable sofa, plenty of eggs, bread, and hot water. Tea we always carried with us.

We fell asleep, with a sense of luxury, enhanced by excess of fatigue and vexation.
