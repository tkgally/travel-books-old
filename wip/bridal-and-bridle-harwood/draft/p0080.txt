[[cont]]and gave me a curious old handjar, with stained ivory hilt, and purple blade; while, marvel of marvels! Jew as he was, he was perfectly contented with the gratuity I gave him. In fact, he scarcely looked at it.

By his advice we laid in a small store of lemons and oranges, things unattainable on the road, and doubly precious, as the wine of the country is sour, and pure cold water reckoned unwholesome. Spirits, both brandy and rakee, every one cautions the traveller against; though the Servians and Greeks are habitual dram drinkers. Tea we had, and coffee too, as Turkish coffee always is, black, muddy, and bad; but, alas, Giovanni, one day, in the plentitude of his wisdom, threw ours away.
