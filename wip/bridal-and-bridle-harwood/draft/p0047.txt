The road was bad and swampy, the bridges frequent, the driver stupidly sulky, and our progress, of course, very slow. Suddenly we observed a figure in white step from the bushes by the river, and pursue our track. By the dim and fitful light I could just distinguish that it was that of a Turk of powerful frame. He came nearer.
