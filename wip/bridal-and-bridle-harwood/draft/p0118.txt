He inquired whence we had come, and whether we were going to Stamboul, also if Emily could <i>write!</i>

On being answered through an interpreter in the affirmative, his wonder and delight grew absolutely childish, and he went away salaaming and smiling like a bearded angel.

Next in came a short, slight, well-dressed old man, in a white turban, and with a dark intelligent face, white hair, and a glittering eye. He accosted us in excellent <i>English,</i> and when I had taken a whiff or two of his chibouque, informed us that he was Kishld Prendrich, a celebrated Servian Tartar, who had been forty or fifty times to Constantinople with cabinet messengers. We had heard the fame of his exploits at Belgrade.
