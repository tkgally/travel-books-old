Iovon was sacrilegious enough to fire off his pistol at one, and the bird’s utter stupefaction shewed his astonishment at this sudden and unprecedented act of treachery and ferocity in the human species. Gates frequently cross the roads here, and the trouble of opening them forcibly recalled old rides among the Kentish hills to my recollection.
