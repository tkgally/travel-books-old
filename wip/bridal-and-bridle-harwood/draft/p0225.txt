All the <i>gens</i> of the Khan waited upon this worthy gentleman with great attention and servility, while we walked along the half-lit passages, gazed on the groups who sat or lay in our path, clad in all the varying picturesqueness of the flowing Oriental garb, musing on the mysterious youth of the East, and thinking of the caliph of caliphs, the defender of the people, the ubiquitous merchant of Moussul, Haroun Alraschid the Good.

The road from Tatar Bazardchick is tolerably practicable for carriages of an infrangible nature, and we accordingly hired a vetturino. But never was such a Lohn-Kutcher turned out before from Paris, Vienna, or Florence, and such a vehicle who ever beheld?
