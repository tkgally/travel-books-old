The scene was picturesque enough. The rushing river with its turbid waters and its fringe of dark trees, the ruddy camp-fires, the gloomy mountains, and our own party, were all seen by the broad bright flashes that lit up the night for a moment, to double the darkness by contrast when they disappeared. A shout or two came occasionally down the breeze, and we could now and then catch a glimpse of a grey horse and another steed careering the Maidan at a distance behind us; but whether mounted or not no one could tell.
