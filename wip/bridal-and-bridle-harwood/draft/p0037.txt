An army whose officers disgrace their epaulettes, by playing the part of amateur constables on the highroads, ceases to deserve the name, and becomes a rabble of cut-throats.

There was a Turkish merchant at Voynich returning into Bosnia, on his little spirited mountain horse, shaggy and trapped over with red housings. This man’s bold bronzed face, and sash filled with glittering arms, bright and serviceable, made rather a striking contrast to the equipment of an English bagman.
