[[cont]]idling about the spacious ante-chambers and lofty corridors, we advanced into an inner room, where stood Vlasuf Pacha. This said Vlasuf Pacha, the Governor of Belgrade, a man popular with Turks and Christians, equally a friend to the Franks, and a trusty pillar of the Ottoman empire, was a Pacha of the highest rank,—the three-tailed Bashaw of the ancients. He was a Field Marshal in the Turkish army, and receives now 8000<i>l.</i> a-year from the Sultan, and is immensely rich, besides having married the daughter of a celebrated and wealthy Pacha of Widdin, and being himself an Asiatic Turk of high degree, and, I believe, a native of the purely Osmanlee province of Anatolia.
