I walked up to the barracks, accompanied by the Medico, who having been in Italy, and wishing at some future time to visit France and England, asked me if there were not a railroad from Amsterdam to London!

The Milazim, who met us at the gate of the barracks, looked exceedingly like a sergeant, garnished with a white shoulder belt. The guard stood to arms, and the officers buzzed around us like a swarm of bees, with swords hilted with transparent horn; while queer little stars, brass crescents, and small cartridge boxes, covered the breasts of their uniforms.
