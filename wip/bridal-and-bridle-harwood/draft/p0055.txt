We rolled off, under a broiling sun, with the great black and purple Turkish Mountains of the Forbidden, the enchanted Bosnia, the land of giants, looming on our right. Near here we saw the first stork swooping heavily through the air. They grew common afterwards. The genii had repaired the broken bridge in the night, and clever workmen they must have been; for it was a stately structure when we passed over it; and even the perilous ford was shorn of its terrors by the presence of a ferry-boat, with a man in an Albanian dress for a Charon. Having crossed the Styx, we entered Jasyenovasz, quitted the Bannat of Croatia, and were soon in Slavonia.
