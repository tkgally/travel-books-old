In about a quarter of an hour the intruder returned, and we did the same, apparently intimidating him, as he again retired. Half an hour more, and the nocturnal marauder made another essay to force open the door. The attempt was this time productive of great clatter; and when, our patience quite exhausted, and I feeling as savage as a tiger, we rushed again into the room, determined, if possible, to catch the sneaking coward in the act, we found the outer door half open.
