Further on we passed through a village fête, where all the men were dressed in white petticoats and frilled trousers. The women, besides their ordinary dress, wore very handsome ruffles round their sleeves. Gold was actually refused at an inn near here. It really is almost worth a journey to Slavonia to see the mighty metal monarch of the civilized world looked upon with suspicion, and rejected as the vilest dross.
