Still the worthy infidel trooper was discontented, and walked up and down in the road below, mounting guard over us most assiduously, and menacing us from time to time with his sabre and pistol, in case we made another attempt to elude his vigilance. At last Iovon who had been absent all this time preparing our supper, returned with a large bowl of roast mutton, we were left in peace to eat our supper.

We occupied the whole of the little hut, which we barricaded as well as we could. Our attendants, civil and military, slept on a wooden platform below, very near the house, and fronting the road through the village.
