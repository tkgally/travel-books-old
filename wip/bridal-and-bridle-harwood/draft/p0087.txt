In five minutes, two pipe-bearers entered bringing long chibouques, which, with bended knee, they gracefully presented to the consul and myself, placing the carved bowl in a small brass stand. A piece of some aromatic wood burning steadily, kept the pipes constantly lighted; and as I applied the costly mouth-piece to my lips, the delicious fragrance of the Sheraz tobacco streamed upwards in its soft luxurious clouds.
