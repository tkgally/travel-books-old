This denial was, I suppose, a kind of bravado to establish a reputation for the civilization of their own country, and to hide as much as lay in their power the gaping wounds of the Ottoman Empire. I afterwards saw many bloody and ghastly proofs of the existence of robbers in Turkey, and cannot therefore credit the accuracy of the evidence of my informants at Nissa, to the contrary.
