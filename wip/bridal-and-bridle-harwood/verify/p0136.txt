The Pacha next informed me that he was not, like other Turks, uneducated and illiterate, but that he had travelled in Wallachia, and had even once been in part of Europe! He then asked the news from London, spoke with great respect of Lord Palmerston and Sir Stratford Canning, and informed me that the Reis Effendi was his cousin. I expressed due surprise and admiration of the fact, and he went on to say that he had long wished to see both London and Paris, but was now too old to travel, and was besides unable to speak any European languages.
