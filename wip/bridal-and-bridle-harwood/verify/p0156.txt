Just then honest Iovon entered, crossed himself devoutly, muttered a prayer or two, strode across the church, and took my candle from me. I then made my way to where the officiating priest stood. He wheeled himself and his little moveable desk out of the way, chanting and howling all the time, and allowed me at last to gain Emily’s side, whose candle-end was just beginning to burn her fingers.
