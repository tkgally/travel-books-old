We heard from him at dinner that there was a rebellion of Rayahs in Bulgaria, in the Pachalik of Nissa, which we should have to pass. A Swiss gentleman was present, who had travelled by that route. He fully believed the fatigues and hardships would prove too much for us; and when the consul said he believed the Nissian rebels to be nearly quelled, replied with a bland smile and incredulous expression, “<i>Croyez vous qu’ils sont domptés?</i>” in a tone which Liston could not have equalled.
