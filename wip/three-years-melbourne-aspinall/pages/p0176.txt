It struck me as rather a fine sight, to see one of these coaches, drawn by eight splendid horses, driven at full speed into the towns; indeed, I am sure that if any accomplished amateur whip, of Ascot or Newmarket renown, could witness the consummate grace and skill with which these coachmen pass their fingers through the reins, and, with one sonorous crack and slashing wave of their whip, dash off on their journeys, he would be lost in admiration at their prowess.
