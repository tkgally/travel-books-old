[[cont]]Kyneton do not complain much of the heat, the air here being peculiarly keen and bracing; and, in the winter, they have sometimes even a slight touch of frost. This place has been called the Cheltenham of Victoria, as many medical men send their patients here to recruit.

At Kyneton I met with that which is uncommon in Australia, namely, a clergyman who (with his excellent and charming wife) was greatly beloved by his congregation, to each member of which he was personally known. I could perceive the influence of this good man pervading the whole tone of society in Kyneton, from the highest to the lowest class. Since my return home I have heard of his removal to Melbourne, where he will have a wider sphere of usefulness.
