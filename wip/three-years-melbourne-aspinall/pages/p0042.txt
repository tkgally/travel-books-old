An acquaintance of mine told me that the worthy and esteemed secretary had been to him on one occasion with tears in his eyes, and altogether looking sadly stricken and dispirited; for he had just received what he felt to be a severe rebuff from a lady,—one, too, who most deservedly bears a high character for benevolence. It appeared that he had called upon her husband to request him to deliver a lecture, but this gentleman had only just recovered from an attack of colonial fever; and, therefore, the good secretary, on being informed that he could not see him, requested to have an interview with his wife.
