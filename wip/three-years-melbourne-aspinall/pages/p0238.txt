It is pleasant to turn from these chronicles of unsuccessful colonists to those of men who, through self-reliance, integrity, and indomitable perseverance, have, from small beginnings, attained great wealth.

I was informed that one of the principal shopkeepers in Melbourne, who now has his country-house, and is surrounded by every luxury which wealth can buy, emigrated with only one hundred pounds in his pocket. He first went up to the “diggings,” where he made a few more hundred pounds; he then set up a small shop in Melbourne, which flourished, and, by degrees, he made himself what he now is.
