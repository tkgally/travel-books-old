The new Melbourne Club is a vast improvement on the old building, which latter, being in the hollow of the street, was far from healthy, and infested by the insect tribe, mosquitoes, &c. The new Club has lately been built in the quiet part of Collins Street East, exactly opposite to the handsome town residence of Mr. Ebden, until lately the wealthiest <i>gentleman</i> in the Legislative Assembly, but now with his family visiting Europe. The Club is a very imposing-looking building, four stories high, with countless windows of plate-glass; and though it has not the Corinthian pillars and fine architectural proportions of the Conservative at home, it still would not at all disgrace St. James’s Street.
