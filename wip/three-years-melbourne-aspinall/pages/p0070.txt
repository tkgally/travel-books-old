I regret that I had not the curiosity to attend an opening of Parliament, which ceremony is conducted in all due form,—guns fired, and his Excellency the Governor of the Colony, resplendent in Windsor uniform, escorted by a mounted guard. I believe it is a pretty little imitation of Westminster. The galleries and members’ seats are filled with the beauty and fashion of Melbourne, arrayed in Australian splendour, and harmoniously blending with the delicate white and gold of the Legislative Council Chamber.
