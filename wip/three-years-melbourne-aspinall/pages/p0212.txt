He must have been a daring and vain-glorious man. I always thought of “Peter Piper”—one of the earliest of my nursery heroes—when I entered that street.

“I <i>must</i> take you down Piper Street,” said a friend, the day after I arrived in Kyneton; “we shall meet all the rank and fashion of the neighbourhood there.” I was astonished and charmed with some of the gentlemen’s houses in and about this town.
