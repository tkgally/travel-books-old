Besides all this, there was just a <i>soupçon</i> of romance about him which is useful on board ship, and which helped to give a poetic flavour to his physic. His favourite topics were Science and Love. He delighted in getting hold of the most scientific passengers on board to enjoy, as he said, an intellectual feast, and yet sometimes, in the midst of Learning, he could not refrain from wandering into the realms of Love, much to the discomfiture of the philosophers he was addressing, though they always listened deferentially to “the little doctor.” His physical courage was very great; in-
