# Chapter XV.

[[subtitle]] Voyage Home—Our noble Ship—The best Navigator and Commander in the World—Icebergs—Our Society on Board—Evenings on Deck—Bill of Fare.

I <sc>left</sc> Melbourne in the month of May 1861, in the screw-steam-ship——which ship, I do not hesitate to say—for it is almost an historical fact—is the finest merchant vessel in the world, and is commanded also by one of the best navigators in the world. In length she is about 333 feet; in breadth about 51 feet.
