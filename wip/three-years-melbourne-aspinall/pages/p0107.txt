I thought some of the brides, and the ladies generally, in these gardens, rather overdressed, and that some of the very youthful ones would have looked more suitably attired on a hot summer’s day in cool muslin rather than in heavy flounced silks. In this respect, they far out-did in splendour the fair Devonshire ladies, who are much more unpretending in their attire when they congregate in Mount Edgecumbe, or parade the enchanting Hoe at Plymouth on a band day.
