There are railways now open to St. Kilda (which is three miles from Melbourne), and to one or two other suburbs. A return ticket from Melbourne to St. Kilda and back costs 1<i>s.</i> 6<i>d.</i>

I must here record the honesty of a Melbourne omnibus-driver, as I think that there is many a cab-driver in London to whom he might be an example. We had returned home one night in one of these vehicles, when a gentleman of our party gave, by mistake, half a sovereign instead of a sixpence, which mistake the driver discovered immediately, and ran after us to return that which had been inadvertently given him.
