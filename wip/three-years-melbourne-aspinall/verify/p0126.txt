Servants’ wages are still high, and likely to continue so, I should think, for who will venture to make the first movement towards lowering them, knowing the class they will have to fall back upon, if they offer low wages? When I left Melbourne, housemaids were getting 30<i>l.</i> a-year,—a “cook and laundress” 36<i>l.</i> a-year. In many families the cook undertakes to be laundress as well, as the expense of putting out washing is still very great.
