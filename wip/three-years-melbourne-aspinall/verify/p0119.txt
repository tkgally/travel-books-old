[[cont]]was no such thing as a store-room or linen-closet in this house. Architects in the colony do not appear often to indulge housekeepers in such luxuries. Had it been situated in St. Kilda, or any other favourite locality, the rent of this house would have been higher than it was; but, it was in a decidedly <i>unfashionable</i> locality—a few minutes’ walk from the Houses of Parliament.

House-rent was gradually getting lower during the three years I was in the colony, and the rent of the next house we removed to, which was in a good situation, was 175<i>l.</i> per annum. This house contained many more rooms than the previous one. Besides two good sitting-rooms, it had four bed-rooms, two of them large and two small, a dressing-room and attics, kitchen, wash-house, and stables. It was also delightfully cool, and had a sloping garden in front. The drainage of it, however, was bad.
