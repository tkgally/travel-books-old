# Chapter II.
[[subtitle]] Collins Street—The Club—The Treasury and other Buildings—Bad Drainage.

I <sc>did</sc> not expect to find myself in such a handsome city; the streets wider than those of any provincial town I had ever seen in England. The first <i>coup d’œil,</i> as I drove up Collins Street (which is ninety-nine feet wide), amazed me. This street is lined on each side with handsome shops, banks, and private houses, three or four stories high, worthy of the neighbourhood of Hyde Park. Melbourne in reality is quite a different place to the Melbourne I had imagined.
