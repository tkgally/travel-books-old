And now, having glanced at some substantial adjuncts for a sea-voyage, let me also recommend all travellers to lay in, if possible, a large store of good-humour, cheerfulness, and determination to make the best of everything,—as these are requisites. Also, of refined vivacity; for this, I can speak from experience, is precious to invalids. Last, though not least, as in a first-class ship there is always a piano on board, let all passengers who are good musicians be sure to reserve amongst their accessible luggage their very best songs, both with and “without words.” Music used to charm away my sea-sickness when arrowroot quite failed to do so.
