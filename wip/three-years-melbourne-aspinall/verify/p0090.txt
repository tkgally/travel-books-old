The Chancellor and Vice-Chancellor were gorgeously arrayed in robes of black velvet and gold, with shoes, buckles, and hosen of the most courtly description.

I was struck with the great genius of the Chancellor, Sir Redmond Barry, in being able, so well and quickly, to compose (as he conferred on the numerous aspirants their respective degrees) a separate, impromptu, and most euphonious little speech, each one totally different from the others that had preceded it.

The University is an immense ecclesiastical-looking building, and contains the extensive and valuable public Museum. Each of the Professors has a suite of apartments within the building, allotted to himself and family (if he happen to have one), which appear to be most comfortable quarters, and are so built that in summer they are quite proof against the hot winds.
