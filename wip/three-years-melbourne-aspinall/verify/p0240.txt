It has often been said, and most true it is, that those who would do well at home are just those who may do well, and possibly better, in the colony, should Fortune favour them; as they might have opportunities in Australia which would not present themselves at home.

Again, those who cannot get on at home, owing to their own want of purpose and energy, are still less likely to get on in the colony. There are opportunities of success as great as there were ten years ago for the enterprising, for those who are sharp to discern opportunities and to profit by them, who have invincible determination, great energy of purpose, patience, and self-denial.
