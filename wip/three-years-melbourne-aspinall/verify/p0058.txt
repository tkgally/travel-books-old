Now, just about this time, the enthusiasm of the Volunteers was at its height—bordering upon frenzy; and these young warriors were either, in their excitement and ardour, so enveloped in an imaginary halo of glory that they could see nothing real, or else (blinded by zeal and gunpowder) they had mistaken us for an enemy; but, however this might be, they fired so closely to our boat that the other lady (not the one who had stepped on the snake) fainted and fell in the boat, her husband actually thinking she was shot.
