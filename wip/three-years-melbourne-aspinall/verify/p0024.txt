[[cont]]with the other shopping streets, it is decidedly very attractive. Ladies may walk in Collins Street, and find pleasant variety, irrespective of the excitement of buying a new dress or a hat. No lady ever ventures into the other streets, excepting on urgent business. There is an American, go-a-head spirit pervading them, very objectionable to the well-regulated minds of our sex. Everybody appears to be in a hurry; and if we are not equally so, our dresses, perhaps, are trodden on, or we get unceremoniously jostled; not that people are intentionally rude, but everybody is on business bent.

How different is Collins Street! Here all things are conducted calmly, quietly, and harmoniously.
