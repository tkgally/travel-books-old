# Chapter IV.

[[subtitle]] Society in Australia—Gaieties of Melbourne—Dancing—Pic-nics—A Snake—The Volunteers—Archery—Arrival of H.M.S.——in the Bay—The Melbourne Almacks <i>beaux</i>—Departure of H.M.S.——for Tasmania—A terrible Accident—News of the Wreck of the Royal Charter.

M<sc>y</sc> readers, especially those who contemplate emigrating to the colony, would doubtless like to hear something about Australian society. I can only say that I met with many charming and agreeable people in Australia; and I sincerely hope, some day, to have the opportunity of renewing the acquaintance of some of them in this country.
