A lecture, too, on Italy was delivered, the hero being of course Garibaldi,—who, by the bye, I observed, was a most useful personage at all kinds of <i>réunions</i>, for no matter upon what subject the lecture or public meeting might be, somebody generally managed adroitly to introduce in thrilling accents that heart-stirring name, thereby eliciting thunders of applause, which helped very much to make the evening pass off <i>con spirito</i>.
