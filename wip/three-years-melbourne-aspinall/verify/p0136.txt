One of the least expensive luxuries in the way of housekeeping in Melbourne is ice. There are two kinds sold—that from Wenham Lake, and the patent ice manufactured at Geelong. The latter is very much cheaper than the former, and I could not perceive any difference between the two; though <i>connoisseurs</i> maintained that the American <i>bonâ fide</i> article was colder than the artificial production.
