If either Millais, or Hunt, or Maclise, or Stanfield, were to go out to the colony, I doubt whether he would realise much more than would pay the rent of his studio. Still less would Michael Angelo or the “divine Raphael” make a living, or be appreciated, could either rise up in the capital of Victoria. The former might, perhaps, get a few orders for modelling or painting the Parliament Houses, and the latter might possibly have the honour of perpetuating the features of some <i>liberal</i> members of the Executive Council. I saw very few fine pictures in Australia.
