# Chapter VIII.

[[subtitle]] Climate—Hot Winds—Sudden Changes—An Australian Winter—Moonlight Nights—Sunsets—House-Rent—Servants—High Wages—A Cook <i>à la</i> Soyer—High Life below Stairs.

T<sc>he</sc> climate of Australia is very delightful; indeed, for about ten months in the year I thought it almost perfection, and that it could scarcely be surpassed by any climate in the world. Its only drawbacks are the hot winds and sudden changes from heat to cold, and <i>vice versâ</i>.
