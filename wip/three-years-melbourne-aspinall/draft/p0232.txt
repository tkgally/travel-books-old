And yet emigrants are still arriving out in the colony, buoyed up with the vain, delusive hope that, because they have an introduction to the Colonial Governor, or Chief Secretary, or others, (which introductions are valueless), they will be sure to receive some appointment on their arrival in Melbourne. Numberless were the instances which came to my knowledge of men of good birth and education, who were almost reduced to starvation; of others who, through intemperance, were on the highroad to self-destruction; and of others again who were earning a subsistence by menial employment.
