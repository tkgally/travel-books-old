Another lecture was given on Law, by a learned and accomplished gentleman; and, remarkable as it may appear, it is yet a fact, that there was a greater preponderance than ever of ladies on this occasion. It was far too dry a subject for gentlemen: they had enough of it in the Supreme Court every day. Still, out of friendship and regard for the lecturer, they sent their wives, daughters, and sisters in great force as their representatives, with strict injunctions to them to listen attentively, and to bring them, at all events, the cream of the lecture.
