The artist was, I believe, struck with the beauty of the child as he ran into a room in a state of demi-toilette (that is, wearing a loose pinafore, without a collar on it), and requested to be allowed to take him in this costume.

There are Philharmonic concerts held in the Exhibition Building occasionally, and some of Handel’s fine Oratorios are often most creditably performed. These, in my opinion, are the most enjoyable <i>réunions</i> in Melbourne. The Italian Opera is also remarkably well got up. The “Trovatore” was the favourite when I was there; and I think it must have been so with the opera-singers likewise (coming, perhaps, more within the compass of their voices than some of the other operas), for they always appeared in voice and spirits, and sang with infinite expression when performing this beautiful composition.
