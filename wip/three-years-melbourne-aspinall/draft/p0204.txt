The coloured lamps being lighted, looked very gay down each side of the street, and the rooms also being lighted, we could see the Chinamen at their different occupations—some eating, and some engraving most ingeniously on wood, without any pattern before them. Broad good humour was depicted on every countenance. I thought what a pity it was that such intelligent-looking people were not Christians.

The most memorable of all my journeys in Cobb's coaches was on a “hot-wind” day, in the month of November, from Castlemaine to Sandhurst. The coach was one of the handsomest and roomiest of its kind—imposing to look at, but terrible to travel in.
