It is strange how, in the remotest corners of the world, thousands of miles from the land of our birth, we sometimes meet accidentally with an old friend, or else, perhaps, with one whose name has been long familiar to us. Here, on this little circle of grass, in an arm of the land stretching out into the Southern Sea, I was introduced to Mr.——, whose relatives at home were some of my nearest and most agreeable neighbours.
