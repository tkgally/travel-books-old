For the gay, then, there are balls and small dances on the <i>tapis</i> continually; for the more quiet and conversational, dinner-parties; and for the musical, there are most agreeable <i>soirées musicales.</i>

Picnics, too, are greatly the fashion in summer, and very enjoyable to those who are vigorous, and can go through the exertion which they entail.
