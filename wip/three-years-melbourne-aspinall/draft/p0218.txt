Such is the gallantry of bachelors up the country in Australia, that the two youngest of our entertainers had actually travelled to Melbourne in order to procure the choicest fruits that could be had in that market; and these two young men had even had the wondrous consideration to provide a portable wash-hand stand, towels, and a piece of ambrosial soap (this is the only word I can think of to define its superfine quality) for the luxury and refreshment of the ladies. I think this will be acknowledged (by my astonished readers) to be the highest point of gallantry on record in any known civilised country?
