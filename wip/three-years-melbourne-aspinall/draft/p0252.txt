In our ship returning home, there were two brothers who had gone out a short time before, for the health of one of them. They appeared to have had a most delightful visit of a few months—and to have seen, under the best possible auspices, all that was worth seeing in the colony. It so happened that in going out, by the Overland Route, they had for a fellow-passenger one of the principal squatters in Australia. This gentleman being, I suppose, pleased with them, invited them to his station, which invitation they accepted, and this circumstance led to the subsequent enjoyment which I have mentioned.
