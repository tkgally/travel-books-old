The first time I saw a hare appear on a colonial table I was somewhat startled, and my imagination carried me back at once to the pleasant shooting season in old England, with all its genial country parties—with which there are none to compare in the colony. Visions of friends or relatives marching off on the 1st of September, armed <i>cap-à-pie</i> for slaughter, and of benignant old gentlemen, mounted on well-trained shooting-ponies and chuckling with delight, suddenly flitted before my eyes.
