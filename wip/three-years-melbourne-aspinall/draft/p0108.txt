I must say, I thought it rather creditable than otherwise in those gentlemen (without family ties) who “never missed.” In the first place coming to hear the band proved that they were fond of music—the concord of sweet sounds—; then, they showed their good taste in liking to spend their holiday afternoon in ladies’ society, and in the prettiest resort near Melbourne. Others there were who might have had the same enjoyment, but who preferred the tainted air of the Café de Paris, or the still worse atmosphere of the billiard-room. I do not mean to say, however, that all those who stayed away were dissipated characters—far from it; for some of the most domestic men I was acquainted with in Melbourne seldom went to “The Gardens” at all.
