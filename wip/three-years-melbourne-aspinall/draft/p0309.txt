I had been nursed day and night by an angel of goodness during my sickness, and had met with very great kindness from all with whom I had come in contact.

I remember that soon after I arrived in Melbourne I happened to be walking in the Botanical Gardens, and speaking to comparative strangers, when to my great gratification I espied a fellow-voyager, with whom I shook hands cordially.
