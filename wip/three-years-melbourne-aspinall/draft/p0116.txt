There is no twilight in Australia.

From the front of one house in which I lived, we had a lovely view of the Bay and distant Ranges. A little pet nephew of mine soon found out my weakness for sunsets, and as he regularly took an evening ride on his rocking-horse, he never would allow me to miss seeing one. In whatever part of the house I happened to be, I heard him bursting in at the front door like a little whirlwind, exclaiming in his highest key, “Auntie! Auntie! Sunset! Sunset!” and if I did not fly with the wings of love in half a second, he expressed no small amount of indignation at my lukewarmness, as he seemed to think that, having established a reputation for being fond of sunsets, I ought to display more alacrity in going to look at them.
