Whilst we were seated at an early dinner our friends arrived on board, some of them heavily laden with choice fruits and flowers. My earliest recollection of Hobson’s Bay is the fact of being compelled to devour a pear of extraordinary size before being allowed to speak; and also of a lovely little fellow-passenger, aged four years, bringing me an enchanting bouquet of roses, mignonette, and heliotrope, which she had separated from a monster nosegay brought on board by her father.
