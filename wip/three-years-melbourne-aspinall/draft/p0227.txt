Misery enough there is at home; but destitution, as a general rule, there prevails amongst those who—so to speak—born to it, are not ashamed to beg. In Australia, on the contrary, the direst misery is to be found amongst a better class—amongst men and women who have been delicately nurtured and accustomed to comparative affluence. Husbands, who have been disappointed and disheartened on their first arrival, have sought solace in ardent spirits, and have thus involved themselves deeper and deeper in difficulties; or, worse than all, deeper and deeper into an abyss of dishonour and vice;—for the billiard-table and the racecourse offer temptations to the emigrant.
