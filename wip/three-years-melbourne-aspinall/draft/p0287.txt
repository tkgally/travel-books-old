## The —— Gazette.

M<sc>r.</sc> J. W<sc>illiams</sc> <i>respectfully intimates to the Passengers of THE——, that, through the kindness of</i> C<sc>aptain</sc>——, <i>he will publish weekly on board the above Gazette; and solicits the aid of Ladies and Gentlemen to favour him with their Correspondence, both in prose and verse, suitable for a sea voyage.</i>

<i>THE——GAZETTE will be published every Saturday for the remainder of the voyage, commencing on the 29th inst., Price</i> S<sc>ix-pence</sc>. <i>Its columns will be open to all; but no Religious controversy, Personalities, or anything relative to the Management of the Ship, will be allowed to appear.</i>

<i>An Abstract of the</i> L<sc>og</sc> <i>will appear each week, and will be published in such portions as to make it complete at the end of the voyage.</i>

<i>The GAZETTE will be an excellent medium for Advertisements; the charges are as follows:—</i>

| For one insertion | 2<i>s.</i> 6<i>d.</i> per inch |
| „ two „ | 1<i>s.</i> 6<i>d.</i> „ |
| „ three „ | 1<i>s.</i> 0<i>d.</i> „ |

<i>It is requested that all Correspondence shall be as concise as possible, so as to render the GAZETTE a perfect miniature of a Miscellaneous Newspaper; and also, that all letters to the Editor be posted on or before Thursday in each week in the</i> G<sc>azette</sc> B<sc>ox</sc>, <i>placed at the Bar door.</i>

T<sc>uesday</sc>, J<sc>une</sc> 25th, 1861.
