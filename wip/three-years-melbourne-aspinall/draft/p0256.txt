How much of their military prowess they owe to their painstaking commander, Col. Pitt (who also holds a military appointment in Melbourne), I cannot say, but it was my own private opinion that to the inspiration of this gallant officer the colonial Volunteers owe much that is admirable in their system.

And now, kind reader, in the very happy and most comfortable assurance that my friends in the colony are well protected, and impervious to invasion, I leave it and them to give an account of my voyage home.
