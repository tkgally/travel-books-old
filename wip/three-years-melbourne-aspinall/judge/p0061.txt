It was about two years ago, that H.M.S.——arrived in the Bay, and the officers of this ship were a great acquisition to the society of Melbourne. Such an influx of gentlemen of the first <i>water</i> is not an every-day occurrence in the colony; and consequently it was very much appreciated. Melbourne became gayer than ever.
