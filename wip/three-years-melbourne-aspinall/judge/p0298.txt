# Chapter XVI.

[[subtitle]] Voyage Home continued—The Ship’s Birthday—Grand Banquet—Thrilling Speeches—Passing the Azores—Amateur Artists—Advice to Sea-Voyagers—Farewell.

I<sc>t</sc> was on a balmy and lovely day—three days after crossing the line—that our noble old ship entered her nineteenth year, so that the first thing I did on this joyous morning, before sitting down to breakfast, was to pay my <i>devoirs</i> to the Captain, and wish him very many happy returns of the day.
