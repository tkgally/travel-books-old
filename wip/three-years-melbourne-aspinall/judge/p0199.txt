[[cont]]but I have reason to be thankful that, owing to skilful treatment and the warmth of the climate, my injuries were not permanent. The gentleman of our party came off the best. As soon as I was able to raise my eyes, I perceived him with one scar upon his forehead, reclining gracefully against a gum-tree, looking as like melancholy Jaques as possible. The diggers, having seen the accident from a distance, ran promptly to our assistance. In the meantime the frantic horse had galloped, with one shaft dangling after it, to a neighbouring town three miles off, thereby causing frightful excitement amongst the inhabitants.
