It was on the twenty-seventh day after leaving Melbourne that we first saw a ship. Until then it had seemed to us as if we were all that was left of life in the universe. Quickly was the deck crowded with human beings when we heard the cry of—

“A sail! A sail!”

“Her nation—flag—how speaks the telescope?” was eagerly asked; and yet a month since we had looked unconcernedly on hundreds of vessels in Hobson’s Bay!
