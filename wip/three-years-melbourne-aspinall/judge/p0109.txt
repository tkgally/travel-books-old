I was very much struck with some of the latter, who, dressed in clean muslin dresses, brown straw hats, and with parasols in hand, looked very superior to the ordinary class of servants; indeed, I heard of several instances of nursery-maids who had not been born to servitude, but who had been reduced to it by the adverse circumstances of their parents who had emigrated with them.
