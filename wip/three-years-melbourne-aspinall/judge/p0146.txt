# Chapter X.

[[subtitle]] Churches—The Bishop—The Dean—Places of Worship in general—Charitable Institutions.

T<sc>here</sc> are about seven churches (of the Church of England) in the city of Melbourne, and one in each of the principal suburbs. I was greatly surprised to find how thinly most of these churches were attended, and how many people there were in Australia, even amongst the educated, and those in a good position, who never entered a place of worship.
