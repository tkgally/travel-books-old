[[center]] <sc>to</sc>

[[center]] PHILIP ROSE, ESQ.,

[[center]] <sc>of rayners, bucks.</sc>

M<sc>y dear</sc> R<sc>ose</sc>,

To you I owe my pleasant visit to Venezuela. To you, therefore, if to anyone, these pages ought to be inscribed; and I am so glad of an opportunity to thank you publicly for many acts of friendship, that I dedicate my book to you, without allowing myself to consider how much your name may lose, as mine must gain, by the association.

[[right]] Ever yours,

[[right]] EDWARD B. EASTWICK.

38, T<sc>hurloe</sc> S<sc>quare</sc>,

1868.
