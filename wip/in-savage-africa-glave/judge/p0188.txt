The natives occupying the huts near by told me that the elephants came into the banana plantations nearly every night, and as I intended to return back to Euelu’s hut to sleep, they promised that in case the animals made their usual visit they would inform me by drum signal. That night when all was quiet a faint tapping of a drum was heard, distinguishable only to the practiced ear.
