It is noticable that rites of the kind prescribed by the fetish-men to Iuka are only used preparatory to a draught of palm or other concocted beverages, and are omitted when drinking water at a stream or spring. The reason is that poison plays a prominent part in the drama of savage life. Often chieftains with whom I was not well acquainted upon giving me palm wine, have themselves first drunk some of the beverage, as a proof that it contained no deadly herbs. These observances imposed by the wielder of charms are most earnestly adhered to.
