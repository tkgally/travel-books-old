Every traveler who has penetrated into the heart of Africa has been compelled to witness the lamentable persecution which the natives incessantly suffer at the hands of the Arab slaver. All the great authorities on African history have graphically depicted the sufferings caused by this inhuman traffic. Stanley, Gordon, and Livingstone have drawn the world’s attention to the existing evils of African slavery. The most remorseless and cruel of all these persecuting hordes are certainly the Arabs, Manyemas, and Zanzibaris, at present so energetically employed on the head-waters of the Congo and its tributaries.
