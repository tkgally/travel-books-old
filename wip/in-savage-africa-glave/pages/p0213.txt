Having collected in the Malinga a cargo of several tons of ivory, I proceeded down stream with the “Florida” to our base of supplies at Kinsassa at the lower end of Stanley Pool.

The “Florida” was a most comfortable boat; she was over sixty feet in length and about thirteen feet beam; there were good cabin accommodations on board and she was fitted with powerful engines. She was, however, too large a boat for steaming up the small channels at the headwaters of rivers which it was my duty to explore.

We already had a smaller boat on the stocks at Kinsassa, which when completed would be handed over to me, and I would in future conduct my investigations with her instead of the “Florida,” which was needed to carry supplies to the different trading posts we had already established on the Congo and its tributaries.
