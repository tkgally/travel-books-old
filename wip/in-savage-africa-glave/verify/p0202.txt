If blood brotherhood is a perpetual relationship, I have amongst my relatives some very undesirable members of society. This old Chief of Malinga admitted to me in confidence that he was a cannibal, but only resorted to such practices when they were not catching any fish, and his people were suffering from hunger.

Here at Malinga the assembled chiefs voted me several tusks of ivory if I would live among them and defend them against the Lufembé, and enable them to resist the persecutions they were exposed to from the neighboring tribes, who were continually making raids into their districts and capturing their people.
