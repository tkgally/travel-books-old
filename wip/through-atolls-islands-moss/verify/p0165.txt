At last Leilikè, one of Kaipukè’s youngest and bravest chiefs, defied the oracle, and went forth with a few volunteers. The old men shook their heads, and they were right. Leilikè was soon brought back disabled by a shot in the groin. Could anything be more conclusive? The boo-boo man triumphed. Kaipukè felt the hopelessness of further resistance and made a treaty with Jibberik, exchanging dominions, surrendering his rich end of the island and going with his own people to the other.

This occurred about two years ago, and Jibberik, when I saw him, was regarded by his people as a great and conquering king. The scoundrel Capitamata had risen to be his boo-boo man and prime minister. King Jibberik is despotic, can at pleasure spear or otherwise slay any of his people who offend him, and is approached by them with due humility and awe. He rules with a firm and just hand (that, I believe, is the proper diplomatic phrase), and sternly upholds law and order, by which is meant, obedience to his own will and law.
