Always too with the same excuses. In Canada the French and English, but for this beneficent bureau, would have eaten each other up. At the Cape, the Dutchman and the Englishman would have performed the same sensible operation. In New Zealand and Australia the greedy colonists would have destroyed the natives, and now in Fiji the old cry is raised once more and the old system restored.

With what result? The Fijimen, who were fast throwing off the yoke of their chiefs, are again by colonial laws brought effectively under their domination. By the same laws they are effectively prevented from working for planters, and the old kindly, growing intercourse between the white and coloured population is barred.
