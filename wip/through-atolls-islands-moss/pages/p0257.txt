At Atafu only the year before last, nearly the whole population started on a pleasure trip to Nukunono and Fakaafu, distant fifty miles and 120 miles respectively. They tied six canoes in three pairs, and with these frail craft started gaily on their pleasure trip. After an absence of three weeks the pleasure party returned, but one shudders to think what might have been the fate of that gay crowd if wind had failed or the current had carried them away from their destination.

More pleasant was it to observe a large party that we took from Fakaafu to visit their friends at Atafu, carrying the finest of their young cocoanut plants and banana trees to improve the plantations of the friends they were going to see. It was a hopeful sign, and suggested the stimulus to progress that might be given if the missionary vessels made an effort in the same direction, not only with plants from other countries but with better breeds of birds and animals than the miserable kinds which the people now possess.
