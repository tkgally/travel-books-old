I hereby certify that Cakobau, the Vuni Valu of Bau, did this 2nd day of April, 1862, before me, her Britannic Majesty’s Consul, sign the foregoing document, and did express his approval of the object and intent thereof in the strongest terms; and that the subscribing witnesses, J. M. Brower, U.S. Consul, James Calvert, Wesleyan missionary, and Charles Wise, Consular interpreter, all to me personally known, and who signed their names in my presence, did duly hear the said expression of approval, and are witnesses to the same.

In witness whereof I have hereto affixed my hand and seal of office, the day and year above written.

I hereby certify that Cakobau, the Vuni Valu of Bau, did this 2nd day of April, 1862, before me acknowledge his signature to the foregoing document, and did express his approval of the object and intent thereof in the strongest terms, and that the subscribing witness, James Calvert, Wesleyan missionary, to me personally known, and who signed his name in my presence, did duly hear the said expression of approval, and is a witness to the same.

In witness whereof I have hereto affixed my name and official seal, the day and year above written.

[[right]] (Signed) J. M. B<sc>rower</sc>,
