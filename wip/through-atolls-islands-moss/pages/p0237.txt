The fondness of the natives for birds and animals is also very marked, and would lead one to suppose it well worth taking the trouble to introduce better breeds on the islands. They have some wretched cats and some dogs of no describable kind at all. Their special pet is the pig; and it is astonishing to see the cleanliness of his habits and the knowingness of his look when thus allowed to mix on friendly terms with the family. He trots about with the children, follows them like a dog, gambols and plays with them, and acquires a habit of throwing his head on one side and looking up out of one eye with comical effect. At some islands they tame the “frigate,” or “man-of-war” bird, which never fishes for itself, but circles in the air, watches till some other bird has picked up a fish; pounces upon the captor and robs him of his spoil.
