# Chapter IV.

[[subtitle]] Some Old-Fashioned Celebrities.

[[subtitle]] The trader of the old school—The trader of to-day—In the Majuro Lagoon—Bully Hayes—Hayes at Manihiki—His doings at Ujilan—To windward of his pursuers—Steals the <i>Lotus</i> at San Francisco—His exploits in the <i>Pioneer</i>—Renames her the <i>Lenore</i>—Future Hayes's impossible—Modern communications—Modern trading establishments—Samoa, Tonga, and Germany.

W<sc>elcome</sc> harbours of refuge these lonely islands must have been to the wild knights-errant of the Pacific on whom so much good poetry has been wasted, and who are now happily no more. Can I in common decency pass on without reference to them and the dusky broods they loved to rear? The men who burst all links of habit, and who wandered far away from island unto island at the gateways of the day. How hard I tried to find them and how glad, when made familiar with their several stories, to learn that they had long since gone to—let us hope—a better world.
