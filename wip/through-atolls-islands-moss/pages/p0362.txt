[[subtitle]] Convention between Great Britain and Germany as to Spheres of Respective Influence in the Western Pacific (A<sc>pril</sc> 6, 1886).

D<sc>eclaration</sc> between the Governments of Great Britain and the German Empire relating to the demarcation of the British and German spheres of influence in the Western Pacific. (Signed at Berlin, 6th April, 1886.)

The Government of her Majesty the Queen of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland, and the Government of his Majesty the German Emperor, having resolved to define the limits of the British and German spheres of influence in the Western Pacific.
