The place echoed with the clatter of the working hammers, now slackening, now hurrying, now bursting into a rapid succession of crashes, according as the wheel was driven fast or slow.

The articles made seemed to be the simplest kind of farming implements—spades, digging-forks, the simple ploughshares of the country, horseshoes, and all such articles which still find a ready sale among the conservative peasantry of Bosnia.
