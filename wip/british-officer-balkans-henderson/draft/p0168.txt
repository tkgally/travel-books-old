We were also shown some machinery for cutting tobacco, and making empty tubes for filling by the buyer, but these interested us less than the hand-work. In the basement was the sorting-room. The tobacco leaf is sorted into three qualities. The size of the Hercegovinian leaf is smaller than that grown in other parts of Turkey. The best leaf is the small yellow leaf that furnishes the Ginbeck tobacco.

The pay for sorting is small, but as it is easy work and whole families are sometimes employed, they manage to make a good thing by their aggregate earnings. More than three hundred women are employed by the factory.
