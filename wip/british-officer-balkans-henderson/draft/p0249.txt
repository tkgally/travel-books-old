[[image: Two Bosnian peasants in sheepskin coats and white trousers standing by a river, with houses and a wooded hill behind]]
[[caption]] <sc>Sheepskin Coats</sc>
[[caption]] These are of undressed skins, and are similar to the <i>pos'hteen</i> or Northern India. In the summer they are worn with the leather inside and the fur outside, but this is reversed in the winter.

[[image: A peasant woman in white carrying a wooden manger on her head, walking beside a laden donkey on a hillside road]]
[[caption]] <sc>Mangers as Cradles</sc>
[[caption]] Amongst the Bosnian peasantry, mangers or wooden troughs for animals to eat out of, are in general use as cradles. This woman is carrying her baby in this way.
