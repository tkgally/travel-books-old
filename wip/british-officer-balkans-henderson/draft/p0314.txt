[[cont]]alley. There was only one long table, at which a number of officers, officials, and ladies were seated. Each officer introduced himself. They were all very pleasant people, and the general’s wife, who talked English well, was a very nice-looking and particularly agreeable lady.

Our dinner, previously ordered, was quite excellent. Indeed, wherever throughout Bosnia officers and their wives are found dining at any restaurant or inn as a regular custom, there the cuisine is sure to be more or less good.

Contrary to our expectations, we also passed a good night, free from any Bosnian chamois-hunting <i>à la</i> Mark Twain!
