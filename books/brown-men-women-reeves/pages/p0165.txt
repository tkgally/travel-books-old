She is a larger young woman, of considerable <i>embonpoint.</i> She and her men are dressed in the same style, except that they all have large coils of beans down to their waists. This evidently is a much superior performer. Her motions are violent and exhilarating, so much so that her bosoms gyrate in a most alarming manner. She dances much with her hands—sitting—and
