## Decisions
- Contents: the printed contents gives every chapter's summary; the entries give them in full (`toc.subtitles: all`); the five appendices nest under "Appendix" with their printed titles. The draft typed the four contents pages four ways; made plain lines.
- The half-title above Chapter I ("The Golden Dagon; or, Up and Down the Irrawaddi") is typed as centred lines and so ends the epigraph division (JSON only); the epigraphs (Milton; the Maha Bandoola) are JSON only, as in *Rambles through Japan*. Assumed.
- Printer's errors marked: “Singapore, When” (comma for full stop, p. 25), “scarely” (p. 86), “disengnge” (p. 150). Folio of p. 45 (broken 4) read as 15 by the draft: set to 45.
- Stanza breaks at the tops of pp. 27 and 28 (the poem “Over the Side”) were joined by the draft: `[[cont]]` removed after a look at the scan. p. 311 continues p. 310 (the verifier had removed the `[[cont]]`).
- keep_hyphens: mer-urchins (p. 29), cape-like (p. 59), baccy-boxes (p. 124).
- Cover: the engraved pictorial title (p0009), cropped without the engravers' lines; the binding is plain cloth with a blind-stamped oval. The same crop is the picture on the title leaf (front matter, JSON only). Assumed.
