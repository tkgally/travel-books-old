Inside the shrine is the sorrowful mother, carved of wood, and having her heart, pierced with arrows, on the outside of her robe, showing that sculptors, like poets, have a license to do not as other mortals. “M<sc>ater</sc> D<sc>olorosa</sc>, O<sc>ra pro</sc> N<sc>obis</sc>.” Good Catholics are they who pass this shrine, for, one and all, they cross themselves devoutly.
