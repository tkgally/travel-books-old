Late as it was, however, Marie prepared some of the fish when she reached her mother’s house, and sent them to me with some fragrant limes and a spicy pepper. The delicate flesh as far surpasses that of the coarse, garbage-feeding lobster in flavor, as a “saddle-rock” does a coon oyster. With a dripping of lime-juice and a dash of West India pepper, some Peak & Freans’ biscuit and a bottle of Tennant’s pale ale, I supped so delightfully that all my mishaps were forgotten. I even queried whether crayfish-hunting, with a dusky maiden of sixteen, who extended a helping hand when you slipped, laughed merrily when you fell, talked musical patois as she pattered along, were not better than hunting through musty books.
