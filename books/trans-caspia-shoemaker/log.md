## Decisions
- Contents: the printed contents' chapter titles as `toc_title` (most chapters print only a number in the text); `"toc": {"subtitles": "all"}` keeps titles short. "Trans-Caspia: The Sealed Provinces of the Czar" above Chapter I is a `front` division (n 1), Chapter I n 2.
- Cover: the front cover is plain cloth, so `cover.jpg` is the title page; the facsimile signature on the title page is a picture (p0009-1).
- Openings after the drop capital in full capitals, as printed (the verifier had made some small capitals; checked on p0095).
- Pictures set within paragraphs where printed (the verifier moved them; the judge agreed); `[[cont]]` after three more (`image-splits`). Chapter XII's head-piece photograph moved after the heading.
- All captions in plain upper and lower case (5 had been typed in `<sc>`).
- Printer's errors marked: endevoring (p0113), expecially (p0180), a closing single quote for double in a footnote (p0229). Left as printed: "medresse"/"medressé", "visá", a paragraph inside a quotation without an opening mark (p0156).
