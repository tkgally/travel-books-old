A small chapel close by belongs, I was told, to an English family, anciently resident in Traù, but I could find no name or coat of arms in its precincts, and I do not believe the story. The sacristy contains some good intarsiatura work, a damaged Palma Giovine, and some ancient embroidered robes worth looking at. I heard afterwards that the church possesses some splendid gold and jewel work, but unfortunately I did not see them.

The episcopal chair of Traù is said to have been founded by the first Archbishop of Spalato. A prelate named Giovanni Orsini was elected in 1062; he was canonised some hundred years after his death, and is venerated as the patron saint of the whole district. He is buried by the altar in the cathedral; but the Venetians carried away one of his arms as a relic. The bishopric was suppressed in 1822, and incorporated into that of Spalato.
