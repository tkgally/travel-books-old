The Bulgarian is not akin to the various fragments of the Illyrian, Servian, or true South-Sclavonic family, in the same degree that they are connected with one another. In origin and descent he is different from them; though on this no stress need be laid, so long as the ethnologists know nothing of his first forefathers, and, even if they did, are all conjecture and no fact as regards the precise nature and value of hereditary transmitted aptitudes. In condition, habits, and character, he is widely different; and he is hardly less so in language. He speaks a Sclavonic dialect, it is true, which, according to modern German criticism, is one of the two sole living descendants of the old Cyrillian tongue.
