<i>Tre Scoglie</i>: capital big-game cover, but bad anchorage.

<i>Butrinto</i>: extensive marsh full of snipe; good cock-covers. By getting an order in Corfu to pass through the fisheries, which obstruct the river, into the lake, duck and wild goose are found in large quantities.

<i>Ftelia</i>: a beautiful land-locked harbour, and the best big-game cover on the coast.

<i>Kataito</i>: a good snipe-marsh and pleasant cock-ground, as they are found along the hedgerows on the plains. Bad anchorage.

<i>Pagagini</i>: good cock and big-game cover. Fine anchorage.

<i>Sayada</i>: good snipe-ground. In the Bacchante flats, near the mouth of the Kalama river, are myriads of wild-fowl, from swans down to teal; but a punt and a duck-gun are almost indispensable to a good bag. Fair anchorage at Livadakia, whence a few miles inland there is a capital snipe-marsh and a few small cock-covers.

<i>Gomenizza</i>: capital big game covers; cock-ground a little inland.

<i>Plataria</i>: plenty of cock in the abandoned vineyards. Good anchorage.

<i>Murto</i>: famous for its pig-covers. Coasting on towards Parga; there is some fair cock and snipe ground.
