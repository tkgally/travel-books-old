‘Ebbene! che ne dice?’ exclaimed the Zara gentleman who was lionising me. ‘Don’t you recognise it?’

‘Recognise what?’

‘Eh! sicuro! il suo Palazzo di Vestminster! They say this is a close copy of it!’

We then went to see the outside of the handsome Bibliotheca, of which the Zaratines are justly proud; it was the gift of a Zara gentleman, and it is, as yet, the only public library in Dalmatia. After this, having exhausted the lions of Zara, and the evening having closed in, I sat at the café, eating ices, and watching a number of Montenegrines, who looked strange enough in their beautiful dresses.
