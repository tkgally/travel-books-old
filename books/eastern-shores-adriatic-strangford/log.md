## Decisions
- The title “The Eastern Shores of the Adriatic.” printed above Chapter I: a `front` division (`n` 1), the chapter `n` 2. Frontispiece “Joannina” opens the Preface.
- Contents as printed (seven chapters, titles only); the book does not name Lord Strangford's chapters: notes_on_text says II, V and VII, by the Preface and their subjects (assumed).
- Chapter VII printed “CHAPTER VI.”: `[[corr VI.|VII.]]`. Other corrections: “Autorbiogaphy” (advertisements), “à week” (a stray grave, p. 274).
- Greek verse in footnotes (pp. 47–48, 77): run on in the note with “ / ” (no verse inside a note in the markup); said in notes_on_text.
- The portrait of Mirko has no printed caption; its alt text describes it; notes_on_text names it as the list does.
- The travellers' Note in Chapter I: `##`, its lists Seasons, Dogs, Battery, Positions of Game `###`. Advertisements: one division, the second page's heading `##`.
- Drop-capital openings in full capitals throughout (spot-read: all seven chapters and the Preface).
- keep_hyphens: Kako-Suli. A space before `[[fnref]]` in three pages gave double spaces in the editions (preflight `spacing`): removed.
- `[[cont]]` removed at p0092 (prose running on after the verse on p0091: the markup cannot continue it, a new paragraph) and restored at p0043 (flush left).
