## Decisions
- “Around the World through Japan” printed above Chapter I: a `front` division (`n` 1), the chapter `n` 2. The frontispiece opens the Preface.
- The printed contents (drafted in three different layouts) made one plain layout by script: `[[center]] Chapter N`, `[[center]] Title <pref>page</pref>`, the summary;
  titles in the chapter headings' upper and lower case. The contents of the editions: chapter titles (summaries are long). Ch. IV's summary differs from its chapter head
  (“Railway”/“Railways”), both as printed.
- p. 95 (p0155): the damaged top-left lines read from the second edition's OCR (cu31924023517422, same setting): visitors, free, shade, tower — the models had
  invented “away” and “little”. Said in notes_on_text; lesson.
- The domino on p. 74 (printed in the line): a picture after the paragraph; spacing waiver for the stop left alone. Two captions of the kimono figure joined in one.
- Index: page references made `<pref>` and small capitals one form throughout (the draft had two).
- Corrections: “a Chinamen” (p. 81). Broken English in the letters and notices (pp. 131–132, 224) as printed.
- Advertisements: one division (title “Advertisements”), the first page's heading `#`.
- keep_hyphens: Yatsushiro-ware, pony-traps, fire-crackers, mother-in-law.
