As we approached Hong Kong, the ship’s sun awnings were one by one taken down, and we laid aside our cholera-belts, worn since the day we landed at Port Said. These flannel belts and pyjamas of the same material are the greatest protection against cold and chills, which are so dangerous in the tropics.
