The ingenuity of the government in making both ends meet has been extended to the coinage. The largest piece made at the mint is the fifty-sen token, equivalent in value to our quarter of a dollar. The diagrams show how it has shrunk in size since the new fiscal policy became necessary, though it is still nearer intrinsic value than Uncle Sam’s twenty-five-cent piece. The change involved a considerable profit to the mint.
