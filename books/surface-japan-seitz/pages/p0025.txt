Tobacco is barred by high duties, something like three hundred per cent., to protect the government monopoly. But within the limit the declared amount is carefully stamped and canceled, so that no one may think the stray box of cigarettes has been smuggled and thereby cause annoyance.

The same idea prevails on the travel routes.
