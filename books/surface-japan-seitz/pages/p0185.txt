After the Imperial Family, the Mitsui Family and the Iwazaki Family, both business corporations, are called strongest in the financial world, while Baron Shibusawa and Mr. Murai, once the Tobacco King of Japan, stand at the head of the list of individuals who can be called rich. Marshal Prince Yamagata is very wealthy, and Premier Katsura is well fixed. But there are many more millionaires in Greenwich, Connecticut, than there are in all Japan.

Benefits of the war with Russia are not visible in Japan. Aside from the pride of victory, it might almost be said to have brought nothing but disadvantage. A huge war debt is draining taxes into foreign coffers by way of interest. An entire division of the army is sitting on the uneasy lid in Manchuria.
