Wrestlers are made, not born, in Japan. The boy selected to become an abnormality of size and strength is fattened on beef and beer until he grows to about three times the weight of the average Japanese, just as geese are stuffed in Strasburg. One specimen riding in a trolley car suggested a visitor from Brobdingnag compared with his fellow-travelers. Naturally wrestling is professional, and consists largely of heaving and hauling. There is not much chance for sleight-of-hand work where the principals are good men weighing more than two hundred and fifty pounds.
