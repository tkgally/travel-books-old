The ceiling panels are of brocaded silk with golden threads, and the smooth woodwork bears sprays of cherry-blossoms so skilfully painted as to rival the flowers of spring.

Every Japanese wears his family crest upon his gown, and this of Asano’s is the end of a folded fan suggesting the letter Z. “A little invention of my own,” he said, and with a flirt he threw his fan wide open. “See! Thus I widen my fortune.” The open fan is the fleet-flag of the Toyo Kisen Kaisha, and the widened fortune reaches around the world!

Profane expressions are not to be found in the Japanese vocabulary. No one is cursed or damned, nor do they swear either by the gods or the Great Horn Spoon. Expressions of scorn and contempt abound, and certain terms when employed reflect upon the other fellow’s social position.
