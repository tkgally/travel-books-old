<blockquote>
[[cont]]Outrageous!
<i>Keshikaran’!</i>
Don’t go on prating when you know nothing about it!
<i>Omae wa wakari mo shinai kuse ni, guzuguzu shaberu na.</i>
What are you (or is he) loitering thereabout?
<i>Nani wo guzu-guzu magotsuite oru no da?</i> or <i>Nani wo uji-uji shite i-yăgaru n’daro.</i>
Make haste (and come here)!
<i>Hayaku konai ka!</i>
What a noise! I won’t have that chattering.
<i>Yakamashii! shabetcha ikenai.</i>
Mind you don’t blab.
<i>Hoka ye itte shabetcha ikenai.</i>
I consider silence better than useless chatter.
<i>Muda na hanashi wo suru yori wa, damatte iru ho ga ii to omou.</i>
Don’t waste so much time in discussion.
<i>So kare-kore moshite, hima wo tsuiyashicha ikenai yo.</i>
That won’t do; it’s no use (trying that game on with me).
<i>Sore wa ikanai, muda da.</i>
<i>Sore wa ikan’, dame da.</i>
Well, your cleverness in making excuses is astonishing!
<i>Iya! omae no ii-nuke no jodzu na no ni wa, osoreitta.</i>
</blockquote>
