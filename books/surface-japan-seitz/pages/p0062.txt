The liners dash through the Inland Sea from Kobe to Moji at the straits of Shimonoseki, en route to Nagasaki and the Chinese ports, giving but glimpses of its loveliness. Yet here are the fairy islands. To cruise from Kobe to Miyajima in an Osaka boat takes but a day of time to fill a year of memory. The little steamer glides in and out of strange harbors, where its whistle affords the only foreign suggestion and where craft and trade remain undisturbed Japanese.
