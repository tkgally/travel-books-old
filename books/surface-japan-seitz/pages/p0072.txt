[[cont]]Such a useless fellow as that is like a cat’s tail; you might as well be without him as with him.
<i>Anna yaku ni tatanai hito wa, neko no shippo to doyo de, atte mo nakute mo ii.</i>
He is a quarrelsome fellow.
<i>Ano hito wa, goku kenkwa ga suki da.</i>
He is (or you are) a fearful chatterbox.
<i>Hidoi o shaberi da.</i>
He is a prattler.
<i>Are wa goku ta-ben no hito da.</i>
He is (or you are) a nasty fellow.
<i>Iya na yatsu da.</i>
He is so stupid that he cannot understand the simplest thing.
<i>Ano hito wa gu-don no yatsu de, donna yasashii koto demo wakaranai.</i>
It is impossible to argue with him; he is as obstinate as he is ignorant.
<i>Ano hito to wa totemo giron ga dekimasen!—mugaku dake no gojo wo motte iru hito da kara.</i>
That rascal!
<i>Ano yatsu</i> or <i>Aitsu.</i>
He is (or you are) an (awful) rascal (cruel).
<i>Hidoi yatsu da.</i>
