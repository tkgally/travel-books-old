“This is not the first English play performed in Japanese. ‘Hamlet,’ ‘Macbeth,’ ‘Othello,’ and others have already been played by professional actors as well as by amateurs. Each one of them proved a success, at any rate in respect to its cunning adaption to the naïve minds of the audience. The present play is also a success from this point of view. In the first act a party of noblemen are represented as they pay a visit to the mayor at Kyoto on their way home after their investigating trip to Manchuria with regard to the apparition of Halley’s comet. Here Viscount Kurachi, a member of the party, falls in love with the mayor’s daughter, and takes counsel of his partner, Count Seto, who makes a promise to act as the viscount at the fancy ball to be held that night at the mayor’s hall, and thus to win the daughter.
