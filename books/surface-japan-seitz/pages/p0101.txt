It is certain, however, that an extension of the franchise must come in the near future, because education is spreading. The tens of thousands of students pouring out of the universities will soon leaven the mass, and the idea that income is to be the standard of qualification for taking part in public affairs will be swept away.

Japanese babies do cry. The loudest yelping heard came from a bit of one riding in a baby-carriage, instead of clinging monkey-fashion to mamma’s back. When mamma puts baby down she lowers it on its knees instead of the posterior, as our Western mammas do. This is to teach its tender ligaments to bend easily for a lifelong squat in a land without chairs or tables and where the floor is the only resting-place and the heels are the main support. To an Occidental sitting on the plain floor is an agony; with one cushion it is barely endurable, though two make the attitude quite tolerable.
