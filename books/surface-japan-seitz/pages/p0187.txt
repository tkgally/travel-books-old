Can this handful continue to carry the strain, solve both the old and the new problems? This is the question. Habits and thoughts have been reversed so far as government and progress are concerned, and Japan is in the great competition. Can it hold what it has? The answer is that the man or nation that insists upon being treated as an equal usually becomes one! Here is a country where the citizen serves the state and the state serves the citizen, with a single eye to the common welfare. Men and things that get in the way do not live long!

The small earning power of the people, while increased since the war in some lines, is a factor of both advantage and disadvantage in competition with the rest of the nations.
