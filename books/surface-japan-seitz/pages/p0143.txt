“I hear they are very immoral,” said the Englishman, en route to visit the Japanese.

“So are we!”

“So we are,” he assented, as if a light had dawned.

And this is about all the comparative comment that is needed.

Japan alone has managed to adorn progress with simplicity, but social life and display will come rapidly now that the ladies are beginning to have something to say about it. It has been a long step from two swords to the claw-hammer, but it has been taken. Peace hath her victories no less dreadful than those of war!

Much of the reputed inscrutability of the East can be fairly credited to the fact that most observers are looking for more than there is to find.
