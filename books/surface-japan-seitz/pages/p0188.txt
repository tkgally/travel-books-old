Limitless credit and unlimited means are therefore pitted in any thought-of struggle against a people of small physical and financial resource, strong in their patriotism and admirable in brain power. Aside from the unreasonableness and senselessness of conflict, these fiscal facts are powerful and can be counted upon to cool the hottest blood and palliate a good deal of affront and imposition.

Wonder is often expressed at the success of the United States in assimilating four million aliens in the last dozen years. Japan has taken on a far heavier cargo in sixteen years, composed of much more difficult and unresponsive material. The people who came to America did so to satisfy ambition and to better their condition. Most of them brought energy and good-will with them. The case of Japan and her aliens is quite different.
