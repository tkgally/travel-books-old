[[cont]]to walk into the garden before a curio store at Miyanoshita and hear a soft voice say “Ohayo!”—welcome—to look in vain for its owner, and at last to have a big bird in a cage revealed as the speaker, who did not fail to say “Sayonara!”—farewell—to the departing visitors. Unlike the harsh screechings of the parrot, the Mynah speaks with the velvety tones of a Japanese maid—and no speech can be softer than this.

The white duck dinner-jacket affected in the East by the exiled British and, alas, by some expatriated Americans is a sartorial shocker. It is cut very short, barely touching the rear suspender buttons, and flares widely in front. A high collar and black tie complete the abomination. Mark Twain’s white suit, which once excited New York, was elegance indeed compared with this bobtailed garment. It was adapted from the hot-weather dress of English naval officers.
