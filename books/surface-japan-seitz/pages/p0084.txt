Bicycles are popular and much used. Small boys ride them dexterously despite wooden foot-gear and flowing kimonos, that ought to fall off or get snarled up in the driving chains, but never do. Tokyo has an automobile association.

All money value is on the basis of one-half of American coinage, and wages run from one-tenth to one-twentieth. Tokyo morning newspaper compositors earn $7.50 per month. They have a union. New York printers earn $32 per week at the same class of work.

The bundle is a work of art in Japan, and every one carries one either wrapped in a square of cloth tied by the corners or in a pucker-bag that looks small but holds half a trunkful. When Toguchi San brings in his wares to sell to the ladies he comes laden with parcels all closely cramped in the squares of cloth, so no paper is wasted in wrappings and the outer aspect of the package is always seemly, while the covering can be used over and over again.
