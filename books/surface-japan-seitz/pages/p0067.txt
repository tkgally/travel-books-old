[[cont]]Messrs. Kelly and Walsh, the Far East publishers, include in their Japanese phrase-book a series of remarks calculated to relieve such feelings and impress the objurgated. Here are some of them:

<blockquote>
You’re telling lies again.
<i>Mata uso wo itte iru.</i>
Why, you’re telling lies to my very face.
<i>Ima, me no mae de uso wo ittan’ ja nai ka.</i>
You liar!
<i>Uso-tsuki-me!</i>
You’re (or he is) an ill-mannered fellow.
<i>Burei na yatsu da.</i>
What do you mean by saying that to me?
<i>Dare no mae de so iu koto wo iu n’da?</i>
You had better look out, sirrah!
<i>Ki wo tsukeru ga ii zo.</i>
Don’t talk rubbish (don’t be an ass).
<i>Baka iu na,</i> or <i>kudaranai koto wo iu na.</i>
Hold your tongue!
<i>Damare!</i>
Don’t interrupt!
<i>Kuchi wo kikun’ ja nai zo!</i>
Stuff and nonsense!
<i>Baka ié!</i>
What does this mean? (angrily).
<i>Doshita mon’ da?</i>
</blockquote>
