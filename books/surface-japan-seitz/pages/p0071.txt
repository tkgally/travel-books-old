<blockquote>
[[cont]]Well, you puff it pretty well!
<i>Iya; berabo ni homeru ze!</i>
He is a terrible liar.
<i>Aitsu wa uso tsuite domo naran.</i>
Every word that fellow says is a lie.
<i>Ano hito no iu koto wa, mina uso desu.</i>
He is such a liar that one cannot depend on a word he says.
<i>Ano hito wa, makoto ni usotsuki de, nani wo moso to mo kesshite ate ni wa narimasen.</i>
That man is beneath mention.
<i>Ano hito wa hanashi ni mo naranai.</i>
He is (or you are) a slippery fellow.
<i>Hidoku zurui yatsu da.</i>
He is (or you are) not to be depended upon.
<i>Ate ni narimasen.</i>
He is (or you are) a regular sharper.
<i>Maru-de katari da.</i>
He is not a very good sort of person, it seems.
<i>Amari yoku nai jimbutsu so de gozaimasu.</i>
He is a person who had better be kept at a respectful distance.
<i>Ano hito nara, kei shite tozakeru ga yoroshiu gozaimasu.</i>
He is as slippery as an eel; utterly unreliable.
<i>Ano hito wa, hyotannamadzu, tonto shimari ga nai.</i>
</blockquote>
