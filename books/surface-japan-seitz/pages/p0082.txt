But the figures indicate rather impressively to the outsider who is getting the income and accumulating the wealth, and it is not to be wondered that the natives look hungrily at the business which produces such vast results when compared with the earnings of the multitude and endeavor to pull a fatter share of it their way.

High government officials are easy of access and singularly direct in manner and methods. General the Prince Katsura gives little sign of military training or the austerity which prime ministers are supposed to assume. He talks openly and robustly of matters of national and international concern—a man of courage and force in every look. Marquis Komura, Minister of Foreign Affairs, who made the treaty at Portsmouth, is so familiar with America and Americans that to meet him is like finding an old friend.
