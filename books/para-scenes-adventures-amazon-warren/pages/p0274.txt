[[break]]

But a dark pall was at length flung over our rural enjoyments, by the sudden illness of our beloved captain. He had been taken with a violent fever, and so rapid and alarming was its progress, that we were fearful that his speedy dissolution was about to ensue; no words can convey an adequate idea of our unutterable grief and dismay, when we first realized this appalling and threatening prospect. It was as if the sun of our happiness was rapidly sinking beneath a dark and tempestuous horizon, and as if the stream of life was being changed into a vast and stormy sea, a boundless ocean; without a single star to illumine its eternal and night-like gloom! Thus does the severance of a single tender tie, of true love or disinterested friendship, darken for a time all the bright realities of life, and cast its lone spectre like a shadow far away into the dim regions of the future!
