With spirits gay and our bodies all in a glow, we at last came out of the water. Parting day had sped; and when again we reached the house, bright stars were peeping from the sky!

It was evening, and never shall we forget it while the pulse of life throbs in our veins. The deep silence, the wild beauty of the scenery, the tranquillity of the river, spread out like a lake, and the reflection of the stars on its surface, presented a picture of natural beauty that we have but rarely seen surpassed!
