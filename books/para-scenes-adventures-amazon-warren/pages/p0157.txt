A dentical operation which we saw one day performed, we can only think of with horror. The patient was a poor old slave, decrepit, and grey-headed. For some weeks past he had been a martyr to the agonies of the toothache, and had at last resolved to have the offensive grinder extracted: for this purpose he applied to a tall, brawny mulatto, who was something of a barber, dentist, and sailor combined.

Having mutilated his victim's gums in a barbarous manner with a rusty knife, he placed his miserable turnkey upon his tooth, and, with a savage wrench, succeeded in breaking off a portion of it, which he triumphantly exposed to the gaze of the sufferer. Again and again he placed his turnkey upon the tooth, each time breaking off another small piece. At last he gave up in despair, being unable to get out the deeply-embedded fangs. But never can I forget the agonizing look of that venerable slave during the whole operation, although no shrieks, or groans, or murmurs of disapprobation broke from his lips; at the close, a half-suppressed sigh only fell upon our ears. For weeks afterwards his face was dreadfully swollen, and he was in far greater pain than he had been before.
