One of the damsels bearing a handsomely-carved salver of solid silver, presented it to us for the purpose of receiving our donations. Unfortunately we had but very little of the circulating medium on hand—merely a few <i>vintens</i>—all of which we threw at once upon the silver plate. Our pecuniary resources being now completely exhausted, judge of our consternation when the plate was handed to us a <i>second time</i> for further contributions.

I now threw a bunch of cigars on the plate, and the result was just such as I had anticipated. Instead of taking the slightest offence at what I had done, they seized the cigars with eagerness, and I was obliged to distribute all I had in the house among them, before they would be satisfied. The cigars being exhausted, wine was asked for, with which we proceeded to supply them. But, alas! what were the <i>two gallons of port</i> we had purchased the day before towards satisfying such a thirsty crowd?
