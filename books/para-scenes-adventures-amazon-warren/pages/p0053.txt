On one side of the entrance gate of the garden, was a small “summer house,” from which an excellent view of the Largo was presented. Nothing could exceed the romantic beauty of this extensive plot of ground by moonlight! A wild forest rises up around, while tall palms stand like faithful sentinels watching over the lovely scene! The little church, solitary and lone, fills the mind of the beholder with solemn associations; the low dwellings of the natives, shaded by overhanging trees, add to the strangeness of the landscape; and the “southern cross,” gleaming in the clear starry firmament above, brings to mind the immense distance of home, and impresses the wanderer with emotions of awe and sublimity such as no pen can adequately describe!
