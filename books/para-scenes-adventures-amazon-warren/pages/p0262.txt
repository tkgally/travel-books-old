While taking a promenade through the streets of the city, I met with Captain Hayden, with whom I strolled out to the Roscenia. He said that he had been ordered by the “President” of the Province to take the Bishop down to the village of Vigia in his steam vessel, the “Thetis,” and in the generosity of his heart he gave me an invitation to accompany him on this excursion. This kind proposal accorded so well with my desires, that I accepted it at once.
