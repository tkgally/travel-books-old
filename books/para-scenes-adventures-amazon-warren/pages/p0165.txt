[[cont]]further incidents of a surprising nature, although scenes of novelty and beauty were constantly presenting themselves to our delighted eyes. At last we perceived the thatched cottages of Cajueiro through the openings of the trees on our right.

Arriving at the wharf, we were met by several of the kind-hearted natives, who gave us a cordial welcome, and escorted us to the dwelling which was set apart for our occupation.

Having swung our hammocks and lighted our pipes, we sought relief from the fatigues of our journey, under the soothing influence of a noonday siesta.
