A fortnight later we made Starbuck at two in the afternoon. Fortunately the day was fine and a strong breeze enabled us to close in with the shore and sail along in search of a suitable spot for a boat to pass through the breakers. The <i>Diana</i> was lying on the reef, a complete wreck with her cargo of timber being gradually smashed to pieces in the surf. The men had hung a large tablecloth to a pole and we observed then running to and fro as we approached. It was impossible to get close enough to communicate from the ship and a boat was sent to what appeared a practicable point.

After pulling for some distance along the edge of the reef the boat was obliged to return as night closed in. The current being very strong we were forced to put to sea again and leave the poor fellows, whom we could see slowly and with downcast heads walking back to the hut they had built for shelter.
