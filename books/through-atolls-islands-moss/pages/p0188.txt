Mr. Harris gave me many interesting details about the island as it used to be when he kept as many as 500 hogs in his fences to sell to the whalers in the days when whale oil was supreme. About fifteen years ago some Kingsmill natives went to Ocean Island and taught the people there to make “sour toddy,” by fermenting the sweet liquid which drops freely from the severed green fruit shoot of the cocoanut tree. Seeing the mischief, the chiefs of Ocean Island made short work of the matter. They gave the Kingsmill visitors their choice, to leave in certain canoes which were presented to them and take their chance of landing elsewhere, or to remain behind and be killed. The visitors took the canoes and unhappily reached Pleasant Island safely; and that, said Mr. Harris, is how the “hellish toddy” came here. Till then every village had its big house in which the people used to dance and sing.
