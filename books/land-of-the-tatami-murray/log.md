## Decisions
- Contents: as printed, plus the opening chapter (not listed); `"toc": {"subtitles": "all"}`.
- Cover: plain cloth, so `cover.jpg` is the title page. 16 tail-pieces (`ornaments`; six were found only by the draft and cropped by the session). The two plates with two photographs under one caption (p0068, p0074) are one picture each.
- Printer's errors marked (12): cheeked, than (then), be (he), yonng, hnndreds, sefishness, ete., Ths, snd, yeas, Saujo (hotel); litt[e] with a letter not printed (`[[supplied l]]`). Left as printed: the heading "Sanjo" (contents "Sanyo"), Yoshiwarra, "Spot"/"sport", stanza 1 of "My Yellow Peril" unnumbered, the varied marks on romanized words (ō, õ, ŏ, û, ö: 8 õ restored that the draft had made ō, by an accent check on the scans).
- Three stanza joins at page tops removed (numbered stanzas: `verse-joins`). Section "1.—The Japanese Smile" (old-style figure, not "i.").
