## Decisions
- The Sonnet pass dropped 26 chapter headings as running heads (the running heads repeat the chapter titles); the judge could not undo 9: `pages/` taken from the draft, with the two verified changes that the scan confirmed (p0037 a new paragraph; p0040 no break ornament). Lesson written.
- Chapter titles printed in italic capitals and small capitals: roman upper and lower case (`<i>` removed from headings; `notes_on_text`). The book title above the first chapter (p0025) a `front` division (n 1). The index half-title (p0301) typed as a centred line; index divisions at p0301 and p0303.
- “Japaneso-English” (chapter page and running heads) kept as printed; the contents print “Japanese English” (`notes_on_text`).
- Printer's errors marked: “commerical” (p0284); missing stops after “sentence” and “cangue” (p0215).
- keep_hyphens: over-decorated, high-backed, pack-horses, lapis-lazuli; “carefully nurtured” joined.
- Quote-balance items p0126 and p0232: as printed.
- Book's picture: the front cover (title readable at full size).
