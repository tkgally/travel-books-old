At Canton the Government itself has established a cement factory where Chinese workmen under the guidance of German engineers are turning out five hundred barrels of cement per day. The Government works further include an arsenal, a factory of smokeless powder, a mint, and shops for the construction and repair of ships, all equipped with the latest European machinery and appliances. One hundred and twenty Chinese are employed in the same town by a thoroughly up-to-date electric light and power company which claims nearly three thousand customers, and a modern waterworks run entirely by Chinese, supplies seven million gallons of water per day to ten thousand subscribers.

Railways are being built all over the Empire, post and telegraph offices are ubiquitous, steamers on the Chinese seas and rivers are multiplying amazingly, the metric system of weights and measures has been officially adopted.
