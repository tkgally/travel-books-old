But let us not anticipate.

We were impressed, like all visitors of recent years, with Berlin’s brightness: it is now as new and clean as Paris was after Baron Haussmann’s clean sweep in the ’60’s. The floral decoration of the lofty stone houses, the wide streets embellished with trees, grass, and flowers, the display of opulence in the vast villadom of the Thiergarten quarter, have made Berlin the show-town of Europe.
