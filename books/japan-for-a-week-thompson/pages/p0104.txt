Here and there, almost buried in the deep shadow of the cryptomerias, are hamlets of huts, native inns, general stores, tea-houses, each a mere platform with sliding paper panels under a thatched roof propped by pine or bamboo corner-posts.

What privacy or comfort there can be in these dwellings, and where on earth the master of the house keeps his collar stud when he is not wearing it, are dark and inscrutable problems that constantly exercise and baffle my curious cogitation.
