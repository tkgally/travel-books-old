“How many of you chaps are here to defend this rock?” I asked.

I think he said there were about two hundred.

“Well,” I said, “you must have pretty easy times of it. How far does the British territory extend?”

And then he delivered the astounding statement that the British colony of Hong Kong extends over “four hundred miles”!

I made inquiries and found that he was right. The island was first “borrowed” by the British in 1840 for use as a store depôt.

In 1842 it was ceded to the British Crown by the Treaty of Nanking.

In 1860 the British “annexed” a strip of the opposite mainland with an area of four square miles.

And in 1898 a further district of 376 square miles was “granted on a ninety-nine years’ lease”!

Verily Pudd’nhead Wilson was right:

<blockquote>
“The British are mentioned in the Bible: ‘Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth.’”
</blockquote>
