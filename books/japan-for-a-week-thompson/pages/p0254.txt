I called the attention of the Christian Scientist to an announcement in a London paper received by a passenger at Shanghai, to the effect that the prayers of a worshipper amongst the Faith-healers of North London had evoked special Divine intervention to cure him of the habit of lying late in bed in the mornings!!!

Thereupon we unanimously decided that we had no right to laugh at the Chinese practices, and with great propriety went on blaspheming the beastly weather.

Next morning when we arose the howling of the wind had ceased. Dark purple mountains of clouds still tumbled over each other in titanic disorder overhead, the sea’s turbulence had not completely subsided, the foam had been lashed into white mist, and the rain continued to pour
