A famous Chinese rebel came on board at one of the ports and craved audience of the Prince. The Prince refused to see him. But I observed that Young China in the second class was less exclusive.

Slumber and waggle your heavy head, good Sea Lion, while you may! On the Eastern sky I see the ocean quiver to the gleam of a morning ray before whose glow the long night of mists and dreams and terrors must roll away, and heavy, sleepy heads waggling now in sluggish ease will waggle never more.

The hour of awakening is near, Sea Lion! Young China is knocking at the door to let in the New Day.
