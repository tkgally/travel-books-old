But while honest workers fare badly, the Yoshiwara thrives.

The number of “houses” licensed by the Government and contributing revenue to the State coffers is said to reach 20,000, and the number of women practising prostitution is estimated by the writer of a recent pamphlet on <i>The Social Evil</i> at over 1,400,000!
