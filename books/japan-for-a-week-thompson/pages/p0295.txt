In the troubled times threatening the British Empire nearer home, it would prevent war by conclusively persuading the scheming continental financiers that an attack on the British Empire would not “pay.”

It is maddening to see the Great Powers of the West drifting into suicidal conflict with each other, while the whole structure of civilisation’s achievement—which is the raising of the general standard of life—stands threatened by the advancing surge of economic, and perhaps military, invasion from the East!
