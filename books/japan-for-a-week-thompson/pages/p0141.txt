# Japanese Commercial Honesty

THE tedious commercial Britisher on the Yokohama steamer had been feeding me to the verge of nausea with what he was pleased to call “evidences of Japanese rascality.”

At last the instinct of self-preservation compelled me to assure him that my own personal interest in honesty was limited to a general scorn and loathing for all forms of eccentric affectation. “And as for the Japanese,” I continued, in my most blighting vein of irony, “I have found it more pleasant to be cheated by their roguery than bored to death by British honesty.”

You would have thought the man would have shrivelled up like a salted snail.

But he did not.

“Pleasant be d——d,” he retorted unabashed; “that’s their confounded craft.”

Whereupon I affectionately shook hands with him and bade him go to the Devil.
