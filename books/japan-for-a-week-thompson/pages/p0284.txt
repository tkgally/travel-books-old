That is why over a million Germans are members of the Navy League, and why the League’s official organ has the largest circulation of any paper in Germany. War? Ach! no, there will be no war; but we want to be ready to pick up what we can as opportunity occurs.”

There is no ill-feeling between the German and British peoples—“no offence i’ the world.” The Germans sincerely like the English and in many ways admire them. They have no wish to make war, and never will, if they can obtain their objects by peaceful strategy.

But—they believe in their nation and their nation’s mission and destiny. They also believe, more or less avowedly, that England has “seen her best day.” They believe that having doubled her possessions within the last fifty years, England has “bitten off more than she can chew”; they do not believe that England is strong enough to continue to hold a fifth of the globe’s entire surface.
