# China’s Awakening

FOR a century past the Chinese have mildly endured the unblushing depredations of European pirates.

Their people say: “Chinaman he velly nice and quiet and no want to fight. Then come him missionaly with big book Bible and top-side Pidgin” (religious converse), “and Chinaman he say nothing. Then come gunboat, and behind it come foleign devil with opium for Chinaman. Mandarin say, ‘no opium to-day, thankee allee samee.’ But Elopean officer say, ‘Yes, yes, you take velly nicee opium, muchee good for Chinaman, goddam.’ Then gunboat he spit fire, China towns burn, China men, and women, and childlen catchee die and get one piece coffin. Then Chinaman say: ‘Stop. We take opium.’ Then foleign devil he say, ‘You no want opium? You buy guns and dlive out foleign devils.’ Chinaman buy top-side guns and want to take back stolen tellitoly.
