Then came lavish consignments of flowers for the decoration of the captain’s quarters, which had been given up for his reception.

Finally, when the ship’s deck had been duly carpeted and the ship’s officers drawn up in line along the passage through which he was to walk, a smart little tender manned by Chinese man-o’-war’s men in British uniforms, with pigtails wrapped chignon-wise under their caps, drew briskly alongside, and His Imperial Highness, distinguished by a huge diamond in his head-dress, stepped up the carpeted gangway.

I caught a glimpse of a heavy but good-humoured face, clean shaven, and a lithe though stoutish figure clad in rustling silken robes, and lo, behold! he had vanished to the upper deck never to appear again through the six weeks of our travelling together.

The uncle of the Emperor was proceeding to Europe to study naval architecture.
