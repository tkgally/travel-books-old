## Decisions
- Chapter openings: full capitals after the drop capital, as printed (the draft and verifier typed eleven of eighteen as small capitals); made alike.
- The line “Scouting for Stanley, in East Africa.” above Chapter I: a `front` division; Chapter I `"n": 2`. Contents: chapter titles as printed.
- The frontispiece's facsimile signature is part of the picture; no caption line (the verifier dropped the draft's “Thomas Stevens.”, kept so).
- “Lions.” (p0185) kept as a `##` subheading, as printed.
- Corrections (16): huntiug, expense (stop), Cousul, Christain, mision, theatening, pcses, rememberd, Morever, belicose, argeed, themsleves, vegtables, ageed (14 printed misprints; the book is carelessly printed). Left as printed: canon (for cannon), braggard, agressive, naiberè/naibère, portégès. Specks, not accents: Nyânza (p0168), incognitá (p0024). Transcription errors fixed from the spot-read: Abbot's (caption p0197).
- “<i>Bagh</i>-” at the foot of p0021: the hyphen put outside the italics so that the page join makes “Baghdad” (rendered look found “Bagh- dad”).
- `[[cont]]` the verifier added at p0182 removed (indented on the scan); its removal at p0053 kept (chant lines).
- Unsure, left as typed: p0201 “done,” (perhaps “done;”), the stops of two captions (p0119, p0183).
