Everywhere on exposed places were patches of ground cleared of grass, and little groups of women and children fanning the ground with banana leaves to drive or attract white ants. They were brought to us for sale in banana-leaf cylinders, and as an extra recommendation for us to purchase, one or two would be taken out and rolled between the palms to show us how nice and fat they were. These termites, lightly roasted, are considered a great delicacy by the people of Machame.

The Sultan of Machame provided us with a guide for a hunting trip to the Letima Plains, below his country, after which we would return to Taveta, visiting Mandara of Moschi on the way.
