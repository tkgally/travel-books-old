I thought that there were two men on the top waving their clothes, but it turned out to be only a couple of ragged petticoats stuck on poles. I looked all round the rock to see how the men got up to put them there, but gave it up; it appeared inaccessible.

The road, ploughed up by numerous caravans, now led round to a side valley, where great low rolling hills of burnt sand stretched away on either hand, and no sign of life broke the dead desolation. I cantered alone along the bed of a dried-up river, for Conway was far behind investigating the figure and temple of the rock. The wind was raw, and I felt exceedingly miserable and outcast in this terrible desert, and my imagination dwelt on the thought of wandering alone, lost in this barren and awful waste. Should a storm or fog arise, and the sand obliterate the tracks, it would be hopeless to find the way. Valleys branched out of each other on every side, a very maze, and each one the same.
