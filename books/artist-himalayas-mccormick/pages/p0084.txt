After the first night, as the weather seemed hopeless, it was decided to send back to the village of Minimurg eighty-five of the coolies, and to bring them back when there seemed a prospect of getting on. The Pathans caused a lot of trouble, but when it cleared up a little the Gurkhas settled it by turning them out and sending them off, for it was too much of a nuisance to have to be on the alert all the time with such thieves near us.
