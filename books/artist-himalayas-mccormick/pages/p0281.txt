On, still on, and now my miserable, lazy beast of an animal would do no more than a crawl. How I hated that brute! I had turned into a broad, open valley, and lay at the edge of the road while the beast grazed on the thistles. There was a low-walled, flat-roofed series of houses, on the roofs of which the curiously ugly women with their wonderful headgear were spreading out some grass to dry. Out across the valley rose marvellous shaped spiky hills, with the still more marvellous buildings on top, mostly in a ruined state. Karbir joined me where I lay, and informed me that lunch had been taken by the party after crossing the pass, so he and I made our way to the <i>Serai</i> of Karbu, that being the name of the valley and the village. The villagers in charge received us, and I set them to work sweeping the built-up platform near the <i>Serai</i>, and amused myself making notes of them till my companions arrived.
