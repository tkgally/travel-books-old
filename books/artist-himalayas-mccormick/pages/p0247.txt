“He's gone!” Conway shouted, as his coolie missed a step.

The fear on the men's faces was a study, but they reached the up slope of the bridge, and a native went down to give them a hand at the end; and we breathed at last. I believe we held our breath the whole time they were crossing, and I was bathed in perspiration. Conway offered up a pious “thank God! there are no more of those horrible bridges.”
