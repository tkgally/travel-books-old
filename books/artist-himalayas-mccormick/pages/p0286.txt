[[cont]]After leaving Bazgo, and passing numerous <i>chortens</i>, we passed out into the sandy desolation we now knew so well, and leading the way with a good pony and whoop tally-ho, I broke into a gallop with Conway, while Harkbir clattered after. Going rapidly was a grand sensation, the wind swirling round one’s face in cool breezes as we greeted each man we met with his native <i>jooley</i>, or how d’ye do. Stopping at the <i>Serai</i> of Fiang, where two gaily-dressed women were resting, I dared Zurbriggen to speak to them, but he would not, though their gay glances were quite invitation enough; they were evidently a Kashmiri beauty and her attendant. Throwing their silk-breeched legs over their ponies’ backs, they started off in the opposite direction.
