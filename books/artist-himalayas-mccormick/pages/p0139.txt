[[cont]]we continued our way to the camping ground of Gulmet, bright and fresh with irises and crocuses, and under the shade of the fruit-trees we pitched our tents for a day's exploring and sketching.

In the evening the sound of horns and the <i>muezzin</i> calling to prayers drew me to the shade of the chinars, under which nestled a picturesque mosque, the only building outside the walls of the fort.
