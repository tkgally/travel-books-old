The coolies who were to take the greater part of the baggage to Srinagar direct, gathered around, but Conway was unable to get the governor to change a cheque. This caused much trouble and telegraphing, and not till next day was the matter arranged. Then they were sent off, and we started on our journey to the land of Buddha.
