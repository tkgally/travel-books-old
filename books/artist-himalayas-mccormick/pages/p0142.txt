The old Rajah, a villainous looking old scoundrel he was, and very old and infirm, paid us a visit. He sat squatted on the ground, while two of his attendants placed their backs at his disposal to lean against, for the natives were all most servile to him, and fought among themselves for the honour of doing any little service he might require. He invited us to visit his castle, and we promised to do so on our return from Hunza.

[[image: sketch of a seated man in a cap and an embroidered robe with a dark waistcoat]]

The scenery all around us was of different character from the bare valleys about Gilgit and Bagrot; for the hillsides on every hand were terraced with fields, and groves of fruit-trees filled the valley.
