We packed up and made our way back to the bangla, to find that Bruce and four Gurkhas had returned to Abbottabad, so we settled down to a lively evening with the remaining men. Two goats had been purchased and presented to them by Jack as a slight return for their services the day before, and of course we must see them killed in true Gurkha fashion—<i>i.e.,</i> beheaded with a kookri at one cut. The man deputed to show off took his stand beside the beast, facing the same way, while another held the animal's head level.
