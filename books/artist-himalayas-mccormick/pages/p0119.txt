A crisp tramp through the welcome shade, over dried leaves, soon restored our equanimity, and brought us to a pleasant camping-place, where, shortly after, our tents were up, and all was prepared for a fortnight's work in these valleys. We were now on the ground where the real surveying and exploring must commence, and an attempt would be made to cross some of the snow passes into the Hunza valley.
