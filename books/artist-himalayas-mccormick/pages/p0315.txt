When he looked out he saw one of the dreadful dead heaps blazing close by his tent. He was ill that night, but escaped after going through all the agonies of death. And after the cholera came fire. About a quarter of the town was burnt to the ground, as the ruined and blackened walls still standing on the bank of the river testified. But all these horrors were past. The present was with us, and a time for rest.
