# Chapter VII.

[[subtitle]] Hispar Pass to Askole.

[[image: pencil sketch of a group of robed figures standing on a snowy ridge, with mountains faintly drawn behind]]

WE had no intention of crossing the pass next morning when we rose at 4 a.m., so we merely left instructions with Karbir and Rahim Ali to make for another dark spur across a great side glacier, and camp there. After breakfast Conway and I, with Zurbriggen and Harkbir, went out on to the glacier again for the remaining portion of the survey on this side, intending to make the passage of the pass early next morning.
