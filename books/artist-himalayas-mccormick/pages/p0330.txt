I do not know if, in the unseen course of events, I shall ever be in the north of India again. It is, perhaps, as unlikely as it seems to me infinitely desirable. For the year I spent on this expedition was the fullest in my life, the strangest, and the most wonderful. If I should see no more of that Orient, which was a revelation to me, both of humanity and of nature, I learnt much and made many friends and got to understand things which were once a mystery to me. For there I came closest into contact with real men and real fighters; there I learnt what it is to engage in a hand-to-hand conflict with the mightiest forces of the universe, and there I saw what perseverance, foresight, and endurance can hope to accomplish.
