It was very cold, and I felt miserable and unwell. A bitter wind was blowing, and all the little streams were frozen over, but we made good way to the top of the Futu La. It was so exposed on the top that we made down the other side as quickly as possible.

Conway determined to remain behind and drive the baggage ponies, and I, not feeling well enough for that irritable job, went on ahead and caught up Zurbriggen near Karbu. We had entered the open valley, and, after a good canter, I was a little ahead and near a caravan on its way down, when I heard the German language exploding through the air
