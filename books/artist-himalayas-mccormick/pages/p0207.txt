During the afternoon Gofara, a Kashmir coolie of Bruce’s, arrived from Scardu, having been sent there from Askole with a parcel of papers, letters, and also “The Pictures of the Year,” with which I had a unique afternoon. Karbir was employed cooking for his companions, but I was very much amused with his comments on the pictures when he came to my tent. His tastes were classical, and the female form his delight; he roared with laughter at every picture of the nude, and examined it up and down, not forgetting to look at the other side of the leaf for a view of her back.
