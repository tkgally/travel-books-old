We were now to enter on the last stage of our journey to Srinagar, down the Dras valley to the Sind valley and the vale of Kashmir.

I secured a splendid pony next morning, whose greatest ambition was to show how fast he could go uphill; there was no holding him, he had a mouth that pulling made as much impression on as it would on a runaway engine. It was fun to see the road clear; the merchants dropped off their horses, and the coolies took to the hills, while the animal seemed to laugh at the excitement he made, and to enjoy showing me how near he could skirt the edge of a crumbling precipice. A very high <i>parri</i> nearly pumped him, and I was glad to dismount and tie him up and wait for the others, who did not turn up for over two hours.
