# Chapter VIII.

[[subtitle]] Askole to the Golden Throne.

OUR party was now rather a large one, for it consisted of Conway, Bruce, Zurbriggen, and myself, together with four Gurkhas, three servants, and seventy coolies. We had also ten Lambadhars, who left us after the first march, and twenty sheep, twelve goats, and Bruce’s dog, Pristi.

On starting from Askole, we retraced our way to the Biafo glacier, and had a hard scramble over its stone-covered snout. On reaching the other side we came across a big lake, made by an embankment of rocks. The road—if it can be called one—led over this dam, which was pierced and cut by many fast-running streams. It looked by no means an inviting way.
