I often noticed them doing this to each other; it may account for the way in which they get through such long marches with heavy loads. At any rate I felt quite a new man after they had done with me, and fit for many hours' more work.

The tea was quite a success, a small fire being made with dry twigs, and then I went on again. A steep, birch-covered slope descended from where we were sitting down to the pines on the road about 2,000 feet below, so I determined to glissade and thus save about two miles round. Jumping from the road down to a place I thought would be nice to start from, I went in over my head in a snow-drift. After struggling and getting perpendicular again I shot off, not on my feet,
