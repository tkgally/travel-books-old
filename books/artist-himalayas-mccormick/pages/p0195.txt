[[cont]]It was very amusing to watch the expression on their faces as they made their way across, doubt and fear giving place to an expression of happy accomplishment when they stood on firm land again. But as the sheep and goats had to be carried over the men returned, and, tying each a sheep or a goat in his blanket with the head out, strapped them over their shoulders. The curious face of the man and the blank expression on the sheep's beside it were most laughable. Pristi was brought over in the same way, and his bark of relief when he arrived expressed in a very human manner his satisfaction that such a peculiar experience was over.

We camped at the side of a small brook running down to the river, and very stupidly on the wrong side, as we found out next day when we prepared to start. Some glacier lake had evidently burst its bounds in the night, and in the morning we found our rivulet a swollen, roaring torrent. It was increasing in volume every minute, and the sooner we made the passage across it the better. The men tried forming a causeway by throwing rocks in, but they were swept away as if they floated.
