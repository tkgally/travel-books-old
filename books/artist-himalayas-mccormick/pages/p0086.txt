On the morning of the fifth day, against all expectations, the lambadhar brought the coolies back from Minimurg, and surprised us by getting their loads ready for crossing the pass. The morning did not look at all promising, but if they were prepared to go we were quite willing to attempt it, though heavy masses of cloud still hung low over the peaks above the pass.
