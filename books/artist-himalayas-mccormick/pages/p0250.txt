There was not a patch of snow or ice on this side of the mountain, though some old avalanche snow lay far down in the bed of the <i>couloir</i>, and a well-marked path traversed the ridges on this face of the mountain in zigzags. It was not bad going, and at last we stood in the bed of the Scora valley, and a few hundred yards further, in an old grass-covered enclosure, I got hold of something to eat.
