But Bruce was not with us all the time, while Zurbriggen never left us. Though Conway had not known him before, there is little doubt that if he were to lead another difficult expedition, it would be a great disappointment to him if he were unable to have the gallant and fiery but skilful Swiss, who was capable of boiling over like a geyser, and of subsiding afterwards in a most placid pool.
