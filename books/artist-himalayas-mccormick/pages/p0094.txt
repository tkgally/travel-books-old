[[cont]]and a gaudily attired young one, who turned out to be his son. They were accompanied by a numerous retinue of coarsely dressed followers, wearing the dard cap of the country. They welcomed us with great heartiness, and offered us a dish of nuts and dried fruit, with a few eggs. We suggested to the young Rajah that we should like the eggs cooked, so he took the dish away, and brought them back with Kashmir tea and great <i>chapattis</i> in addition, and sat with his ragged court around him, watching us eat.

The evening was spent in celebrating with a bowl of milk punch and songs, and general hilarity, Jack's coming of age the day before.
