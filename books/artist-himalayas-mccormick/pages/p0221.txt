Harkbir had next to cross, so getting well away from the edge to the full length of the rope that attached us, I shouted to him to jump. My Hindustani was not of sufficient clearness to explain to him that when he was in the air I would haul him to safety. Nevertheless he jumped and my timely pull landed him sprawling on the edge, from which he got up with his usual broad smile that wrinkled every muscle on his face in knobs like chestnuts. Bruce and the other Gurkhas who were behind found a new way over.
