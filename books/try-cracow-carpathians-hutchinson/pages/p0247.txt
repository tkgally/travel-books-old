The Moravians belong to the Sclav family also, but are of a better stamp than the Hungarian sample. The roads, too, have actually been repaired during the last ten years; the houses have water-tight roofs, and the walls are sound, without gaps or holes. Images disappear from the roadside, and factories are in full work, close to the line. The peasant women here discard all gay colours, and wear white linen shawls, and a roll of the same material round their heads, like a turban, the ends hanging down about a yard in length behind.

Another startling change! Actually, a pair of donkeys to be seen! It is a long time since we saw this patient beast. One could not fancy a Hungarian condescending to touch one. Horses, perhaps, are more scarce, or more valuable, for the peasant drives but one in his country cart, instead of a pair.
