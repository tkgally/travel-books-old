Plenty to eat and drink is one of the first objects on a day out in the mountains; and everyone appears to enjoy it amazingly, if one may judge by the spirits in which the <i>rétour</i> takes place: somewhat more varied and irregular perhaps than the <i>start,</i> with the steeds doing exactly as they like, and scampering down whichever road takes their fancy most,—but then <i>they</i> have had no champagne!
