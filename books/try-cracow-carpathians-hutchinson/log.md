## Decisions
- Published without the folding map, missing from the only IA scan (confirmed by Tom, 2026-11-22: a book missing only a map may be published); said in `notes_on_text`.
- The head title above Chapter I (p0016) is a `front` division (n 1), the chapter n 2, as in *From West Africa to Palestine*.
- Contents: the chapters and appendices print no titles; editor's titles from the printed summaries and appendix headings (`toc_title`), said in `notes_on_text`.
- Titlepage division from the engraved vignette title (p0006) through the printed title and its verso (p0009); frontispiece p0005-1. Book's picture: the printed title page (the cover is plain cloth).
- Printer's errors marked: “neitber” (p0275), “Schmcks” and “Solav” (contents, p0012), “jedeu” (p0281). Left as printed: “handsful”, “Dollinger”/“Döllinger”, “Dusseldorf”/“Düsseldorf”, the three forms of Czardás, “Appendix III,”.
- Polish primes (dzis′) typed as ′ (U+2032) throughout Appendix IV; the ditto marks as ,, as printed.
- Page-top joins: the Sonnet pass rightly removed `[[cont]]` at p0036 and p0164 (indented on the scan); wrongly at p0271 (restored).
