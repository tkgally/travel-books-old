## Decisions
- p0011 (tissue guard) and p0012 left out with waivers; the caption printed on the tissue is given with the frontispiece (p0010), as in *A Woman Alone*.
- Captions printed in small capitals: title case throughout (ten regularized by hand).
- Drop capital + capitals at each chapter opening and the preface: all typed in capitals (checked on the scan, p0015, p0025, p0061, p0103, p0181).
- In-text pictures: the Sonnet pass split each paragraph at its picture with `[[cont]]` after the caption (25 pages; two missed, found by `image-splits`, p0087, p0306); the compiler joins them.
- Pictures printed above a chapter's title (p0025, p0061, p0103, p0181, p0206, p0249) moved after the title and subtitle: otherwise they fall into the previous division.
- The four pictures on the contents and list pages stay with those pages (JSON only); said in `omitted_from_reading`. (assumed)
- p0013 title vignette: cropped from the unaltered scan with its curved printed caption inside the picture; the caption is also typed. (The cropping subagent had painted the caption out; not kept.)
- "terrestial" (p0129) printed so: `[[corr]]`. "Nirvâna" (p0099, draft) is a speck: Nirvana, as on seven other pages.
- Contents: chapter number and title only for all eight (the printed summaries run to 600 characters).
- Author's dates not found (web search 2026-11-22); rule 5 does not apply to JATSI books. Period "about 1902–03" from the mention of the Anglo-Japanese Alliance (1902).
