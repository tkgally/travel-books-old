Time was when only the Shogun and, on certain days, pilgrims might cross it, and nowadays, when the Shogun is a thing of the past and the pilgrim tends to merge more and more in the excursionist, the right belongs alone to the Emperor, and the lovely bridge is saved from the all-pervading tourist and his penknife by massive barriers at either end.
