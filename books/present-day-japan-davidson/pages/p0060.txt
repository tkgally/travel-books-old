For it was stained not only by the cruelty and grinding tyranny of the Regents, but also by what has ever been the greatest of all crimes in Japanese eyes—disloyalty to the heaven-descended Emperor. So the flames destroyed the famous city, and we see its abandoned site to-day as it lies in melancholy oblivion, with nothing to guard the memory of its famous past save the silent temples, themselves all too plainly sinking away towards the land where all things are forgotten.
