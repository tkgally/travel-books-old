# Chapter VII

[[subtitle]] Tokyo

[[image: Photograph of tall trees over a broad path, with a long row of stone lanterns and temple buildings behind]]
[[caption]] In Shiba Park.

HERE I am in Tokyo at last, after all this time in Japan. R—— and I came here by train on Wednesday week, long after dark, owing to the leisurely principle on which railways, like most other things, are managed in this country. Imagine my excitement on approaching this place, full of the romantic associations of the country’s past and the centre of the forces which are to mould its future, and with it who knows what besides of the destinies of the Far East and through it of Europe herself.
