## Decisions
- Divisions: half-title p0003 (front), title p0005, dedication p0007 (read), contents p0009–p0010, the book title repeated
  above Chapter I on p0011 (front, n 1) and Chapter I (n 2). Contents: the printed chapter titles, as printed.
- The printed contents was drafted as a table on p0009 and as lines on p0010: p0010 made a table like p0009.
- Provisions list (p0027–p0028): the brace's "2-lb. tins." moved to the line "Biscuits—" (said in notes_on_text);
  dot leaders left out; the two pages stay two tables.
- p0248: the boxed "P.T. / U.S.A. / 1870." as three [[center]] lines (said in notes_on_text).
- p0196: the asterisks standing for stars kept in the line as printed; "The stars" starts a new paragraph (indented).
- Printer's error: p0185 "countiug" (turned n) → [[corr countiug|counting]] (found by the audit).
- Accents all as printed (Opus subagent on crops): Antinoë p0080 / Antinoe p0082; Kafr é Zayat p0133 / Zayát elsewhere
  (Kafré p0050); Saïd (the saint) / Said (a crewman); three spellings of the Mahmoodéëh canal, each as printed.
- Author's dates: born 1829 (Wikipedia, Frederick Eden, cricketer and barrister, d. Venice 1916; another source 1828).
