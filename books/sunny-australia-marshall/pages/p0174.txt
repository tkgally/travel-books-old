But when that part of the voyage is over and there is no longer any one on board whose thoughts are not turned towards Australia as the end of the journey, then you begin to realize that you are an inhabitant of a country very small, very beautiful, and very old, in the cold northern seas, and that they are dwellers in a vast continent as far distant from England as it is possible for inhabited land to be, that their interests and yours are different, and the life they live is different.

There is not a man among them whom you would have taken for anything but an Englishman if you had met him in the streets of London a month ago. But there is not one of them who seems exactly like an Englishman now you have come to know them better.
