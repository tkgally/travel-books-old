## IX

A few days later a party of us went down the river and across the great bay to the penal settlement on the island of St. Helena, and to the other island, called Dunwich, where a paternal Government keeps about eleven hundred old men and women in a sort of pauper’s paradise.

However gratifying it may be to think of the aged failures of the State living in one of its beauty spots, it is sad that another of them should have to be given up to its malefactors. And there was a third settlement, in sight of which we passed, which awakens sadder thoughts still, for in it the lepers are segregated.
