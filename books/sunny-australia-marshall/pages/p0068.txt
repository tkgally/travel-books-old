If I were an Australian squatter who owned one of these old wooden houses, and had made enough money from two or three good seasons, as many of them do, to enable me to replace it by a big house of brick or stone, inside of which I could fancy myself in Hertfordshire, I would let Hertfordshire slide, and stick a few more rooms on to the old house instead. I am quite sure that many of them who have done away with their old houses and built new ones have found that they have destroyed an atmosphere which they regretted, although I am bound to say that I never came across one who acknowledged it.

Not late in the afternoon we were on the train again and on our long journey back to Sydney. We had only been a few hours off the railway, but we had seen about the most important thing that goes on in Australia.
