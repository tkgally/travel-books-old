## III

So we come back to Mildura, which is the object-lesson of a settled irrigation colony. It was founded, also in 1887, by private enterprise, and went through various vicissitudes, which it is not necessary to enter into here, until it was taken over and financed by the Government. This was in 1895, and since then the colony has attained the height of its prosperity. There is another settled colony at Renmark, lower down the river in South Australia, and adjoining Mildura is a colony in the making, which will also offer facilities to the immigrant.
