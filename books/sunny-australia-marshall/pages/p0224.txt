# VIII

[[subtitle]] Barren Jack Dam

## I

[[right]] S<sc>ydney</sc>, N.S.W

O<sc>ne</sc> of the great needs of Australia is water. On some parts of the coast, especially in the northern tropical regions, the rainfall is phenomenal, in others it is adequate, and in others again, although it may equal the average rainfall of England, its irregularity and the loss by evaporation make it insufficient. And as you get farther inland it shrinks down to a mere nothing. The land is rich, but long droughts prevent vegetable growth, stock suffers, and at times gets decimated; and even when there is rainfall enough to enable a country to carry sheep or cattle, the land is wasted, inasmuch as it will only keep one family where it might keep ten or more.
