# XII

[[subtitle]] Tasmania

## I

[[right]] H<sc>obart</sc>, T<sc>asmania</sc>

A<sc>ustralians</sc> are great travellers. They have to be, where distances are so great. They told me in Adelaide that no one thought anything there of running up to Brisbane on business, and that would mean a journey of three nights and over three days, with a few hours in Melbourne and Sydney on the way. To get to Western Australia means a four days’ journey by sea from Adelaide in the other direction. So a continent whose total population is considerably less than that of London is loosely linked up by rail and steam, and its inhabitants make the most of their opportunities, and think no more of a journey of days than Englishmen of a journey of hours.
