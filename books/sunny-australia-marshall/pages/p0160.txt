There were once two prospectors who had been for long without water. They met, by a miraculous chance in that desert, two other prospectors returning, who told them of a water-hole. When they reached it they found a dead camel in it. But they had to drink the water. There is another story of a prospector coming upon an Afghan washing his feet in a water-hole, and shooting him dead.

After all, water is of more importance than gold.

This was my last expedition in Australia. On February 15th, with friends to say good-bye to and friends on the boat, I embarked on the old <i>Ophir</i>, in which, eleven years before, I had sighted the polar pack-ice off the coast of Spitzbergen, at the other extremity of the globe, and was on my way home, glad enough to be going there, but full of pleasant memories of the sunny, kindly land I was leaving.
