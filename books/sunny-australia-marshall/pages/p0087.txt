[[cont]]After this feat inquiries were set on foot, and when it was discovered that “goggles” had played for Harrow and Cambridge and the Gentlemen of England, they went away and began to think it all over.

Mr. Ramsay’s is a cattle station, and when the King and Queen were in Australia as Duke and Duchess of York their train was stopped where the line runs through the paddocks, and the gentlemen in attendance had a great time helping to round up a mob of cattle that had been driven down for the purpose.

We motored to Toowoomba the next morning, and I caught the train on from there. Toowoomba is situated on a spur of the mountains, and the views are magnificent, over wide blue valleys, hills, and gorges. No other inland town that I visited has anything like the natural beauty of this one.

The next day and night I spent with relations at Warwick, which is the other important town of the fertile Darling Downs region. It is a pleasant town, with wide streets, and bungalow-looking houses standing in flowery gardens; and there are orchards round it, besides wheat land and grazing-land.
