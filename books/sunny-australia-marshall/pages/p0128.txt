## XVI

My last journey in South Australia, just before Christmas, was to the lands of the mallee scrub, not far from the Victorian border. I have written about what I saw there in the article on “Wheat.” I went with Mr. Angus, the Government agricultural expert; and a very agreeable trip he made of it.
