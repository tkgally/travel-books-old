## Decisions
- The tissue guard (p0009–p0010) had been drafted from show-through (the title page and the frontispiece again): emptied (notes_on_text; lesson).
- Part I is printed as twenty untitled numbered sections: kept as `##` numerals in one division, “Part I. Recollections”, with `toc_entries` of the editor’s (titles proposed by an Opus subagent from the text, shortened by the session), so each section is a page (Tom, 2026-11-22). Part II is a part with its nineteen chapters; the appendix entry names its subject.
- Judge spots (12, most on section numerals), the spot-read (Opus subagent: front matter, chapter openings, captions, Lawson’s poem, notes, quotations, preflight lists, tesseract comparison of pp. 1–272: about 25 candidates on crops, 1 real): [[corr]] practially, costal (“the great costal forests”, p0340: assumed, plainly “coastal”), a closing quotation mark left out in print (p0308); quotations continued over a page set as blockquotes; a verse `[[cont]]` removed (p0133) and a paragraph `[[cont]]` restored (p0140); contents entries flush left as printed.
- keep_hyphens p0196 (wage-earners), p0222 (English-looking).
- Cover picture: the title page (plain cloth cover).
- Rendered look (Opus subagent): the `toc_entries` first anchored on each section’s first words left each numeral at the end of the page before; anchored on the numerals (`starts`: “I”, “II” …) and checked in the build.
