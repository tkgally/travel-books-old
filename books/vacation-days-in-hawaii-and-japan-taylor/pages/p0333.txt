The temple is an odd, ancient-looking structure, built upon massive piles. Within are many things that interest us, and we meet scores of sight-seers, as well as pilgrims and other worshipers, at the shrine of the sacred image. Walls, pillars, ceilings, and every other available spot are lavishly adorned with paintings by the old Japanese masters.
