We walk to the temple of Futare-san (name of God), also to a number of other interesting spots, then returning to the jinrikishas ride to the Kegon-no-taki waterfall. This is a magnificent spectacle. The great volume of water dashes wildly over a lofty precipice into an enormous basin, walled in with rocks two hundred and fifty feet below. Descending the mountain side, we stand upon a projecting rock, from which we have a closer view of the falls and realize its extent and the force with which it precipitates itself into the whirlpool.
