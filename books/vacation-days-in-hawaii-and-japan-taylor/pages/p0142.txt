# Jinrikisha Rides and Novel Experiences.
[[subtitle]] A Japanese afternoon tea—Shoes in Japan—Yamashita—Mrs. Shimasaki’s—The great tattooer—Seeing the “Elephant”—Wooden tickets—Maganechio—Demi-monde—Earthquake—Sitting for our pictures—Along the canal.

O<sc>ur</sc> guide, ever thinking of some new pleasure for us, suggests a visit to his home, promising us a genuine Japanese afternoon tea. We accept his invitation gladly, and soon after our little wagons stop in front of a two-storied wooden cottage on the outskirts of Yokohama. Taking off our shoes we enter, and are ushered into a room on the second floor. The houses of Japan are generally built of wood, and are not more than two stories high. There is no furniture in the back room, through which we pass, but in the front room we find chairs and tables arranged quite in “American” fashion.
