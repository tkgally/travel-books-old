The rainy days are also useful in planning trips. We are attentively studying our maps with reference to a journey to the interior of this country.

After a good rest we start out with renewed strength for a little more sightseeing. The day being fair, our guide has forestalled all discussions as to plans by securing jinrikishas for a little tour to some neighboring places.
