[[cont]]and Mr. Chang Chou retires. Then taps and taps. We open to a number of them: Nan Sing, then Aha Sing, and many others. The monotony is broken only by the appearance of a Japanese gentleman. No samples or other wares bedeck his person. I wonder what he can want! Perhaps he is an undertaker, and thinks we wish to be buried from the annoyances of the last hour. He is not an undertaker nor a tailor. He is a shoemaker, and politely asks if we do not want some good American shoes? We put him off as we did the others, and at last are ready for breakfast.
