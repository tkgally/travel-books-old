# Some Sights in Northern California.

[[subtitle]] San Francisco—Places of interest—Chinatown—Prize fight—San José—Mt. Hamilton—The Lick Observatory—Santa Cruz—“Big Trees”—Monterey and the Del Monte Hotel—Seventeen-mile Drive—Chinese fishermen—On the “Australia.”

W<sc>e</sc> are in San Francisco. How odd and interesting the city appears—built on the hills, with the cable cars running up and down its steep streets that remind one of toboggan slides!

There are many interesting places to visit in and around the city—Sutro's Garden; Cliff House, with the Seal Rocks in full view from its piazza; the Presidio; Golden Gate Park; the shipyards; markets, where only one can realize the profusion and perfection of California fruits.

A visit to Chinatown is not the least of our curious experiences. It is hardly necessary to say that we make our arrangements to see the Celestial shopkeepers at night.
