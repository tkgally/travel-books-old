As we climb, we can see the road winding away below us. Now we rest at the Missawa tea house. From this point the scene is wilder and more picturesque. Again we plunge into the dense undergrowth of the woods, following a narrow path.

Another opening, where there are several tea houses. What a magnificent view! Far down in the cañon can be seen scattered tea houses, looking like children’s toys, while before us rise lofty summits, clothed in green.
