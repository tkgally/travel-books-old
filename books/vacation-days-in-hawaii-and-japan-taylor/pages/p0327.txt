Thus the gods keep the people, and the people keep the gods. Here are a mother and her pretty daughter making supplications to a god; and to insure the granting of their petition it is necessary for them to walk a hundred times around this small house. The older woman holds in her hand two hundred long narrow strips of paper, and each time they complete the circuit of the building she turns down two of the slips, one for her daughter and one for herself.
