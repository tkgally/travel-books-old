[[cont]]Yokohama. We enter with our guide, and upon making known our wishes are shown up-stairs to a dressing-room, where a charming little Japanese girl dresses the foreign ladies in Japanese costume. A Japanese gentleman is also engaged to attire the men. This assistance is quite necessary. When dressed, we are told to sit Japanese fashion, that is, to cross the legs under one; and we remain in this uncomfortable position until our photographs are taken.
