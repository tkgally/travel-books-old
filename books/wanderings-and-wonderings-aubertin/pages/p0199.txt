With the grizzly and grisly gentleman the case is somewhat different, and the sport is rarer. Kashmir for other sport, has, by all accounts, been shot out altogether. From the forest tent I came down, and down, on the following morning, and was towed up the “charming” mud-banks of the Jhelum; landing and tenting again at the Chenar Bagh on Wednesday, the 12th of June; but, this time, higher up and beyond the crowded trees. Thus ended my second excursion. My third and last was to be to the Pir Panjal Pass.
