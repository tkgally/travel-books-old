## Decisions
- The scan check's front-matter map was one leaf off (title p0006, not p0005): corrected from the drafts.
- p0011 (a second shot of Preface p. ii, made with the slip lifted) not transcribed: an extra draft of it matched
  p0009; waived in `leaves`. p0010 typed as the errata slip only (`# Errata.`, kind `errata`, left out of the reading
  editions); its contents page is p0012.
- Errata slip applied as `[[corr]]` (style.md): p0047, p0090, p0127, p0140, p0141, p0142, each checked at the place.
  p0033 ‘ Pidgin ” printed with a single opening and a double closing mark → `[[corr ‘Pidgin|“Pidgin]]` (crop).
- Judge: 1 spot, p0122 "Mississipi Bay": printed so; a place name, not corrected. The Sonnet pass changed 7 places.
- Structure: book title over Chapter I left out; contents made plain lines (dot leaders and table rows removed).
  Contents: printed summaries in full (`toc.subtitles all`); short `title` and full `toc_title` set for every chapter
  (the summaries under 80 characters made long pager links otherwise: lesson of *Journey through the Caucasus*).
- Ah Cum's visiting card (p0044) cropped; no printed caption, the card's words are in the alt text.
- Cover: the front cover (red sun on white; the library's "ASIA" label cropped off).
- Spot-read (Opus subagent, 14 pages: title, preface, slip, contents, card, list, footnote, bugle calls, appendix):
  no text differences; the contents' dot leaders and one small-capitals entry made consistent.
