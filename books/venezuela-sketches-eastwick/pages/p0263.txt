In Codazzi’s map Venezuela is divided into thirteen provinces, which, beginning from the south-east, and reckoning in a westerly direction, may be enumerated as follows:—1. Guayana; 2. Cumaná; 3. Barcelona; 4. Margarita; 5. Carácas; 6. Carabobo; 7. Apure; 8. Barinas; 9. Barquisimeto; 10. Coro; 11. Trujillo; 12. Merida; 13. Maracaibo. In the more modern map of 1865, will be found the new distribution into twenty-one provinces, which, reckoning in the same order from south-east to west are as follows:—
