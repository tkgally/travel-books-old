The Great Marshal Citizen, President of the Union, while exercising legal powers, without which the very existence of the Government would not be possible, has not overlooked the obligations weighing upon the Treasury, proposing to himself to discharge and fulfil them with justice, and without inflicting losses upon legitimate interests, so soon as the circumstances which demand the present determination shall have ceased to exist.

Therefore, the collecting offices shall hold at the disposal of this Exchequer the proceeds of the export duties, which are destined for the public service by the 2nd Article of the Budget Law above mentioned.
