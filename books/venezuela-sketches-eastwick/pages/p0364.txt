<i>Extract from “Apology for Sinking Funds.” By Wm. Lucas Sargant. London,</i> 1868: <i>Williams & Norgate.</i>

“In 1862 M. Block[[fnref *]] gave the following as the rates at which European nations could borrow:—10 per cent., Turkey; 6 to 7 per cent., Italy, Portugal, Austria, and Spain; 5 per cent., Russia; over 4 and under 5 per cent., France, Sweden, and Prussia; 4 per cent., Holland; something over 3⅝ per cent., Belgium and Denmark; 3¼ per cent., Great Britain only. Considerable changes have since taken place: Russia cannot borrow at 5 per cent., much less can Italy borrow under 7 per cent.”
