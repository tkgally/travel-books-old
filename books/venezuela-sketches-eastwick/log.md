## Decisions
- Contents: each chapter gets a short title of the editor's from the opening words of its summary (“Chapter II. At La Guaira”), the full summary following (`toc.subtitles: all`); the appendices are five divisions, (A), (B), (C), (C.) and (D) as printed, each with a short description; said in `notes_on_text`.
- The errata list (Engelckye → Engelke, pp. 126–128) applied as `[[corr]]` (seven places).
- Ditto marks printed as raised double commas typed ",,"; `spacing` waived on the twelve pages that have them.
- Seals on p0128 typed as text (“L. S.”, “Consular Seal.”); an empty seal circle left out.
- Marks standing on several rows of one table for one key line (p0263, p0425, p0427) typed as plain symbols with the key as a plain line; a quoted passage holding tables (p0307) closed before each table and reopened after.
