## Decisions
- Divisions: half-title, title page, preface (no printed heading: “Preface” is the editor's, said in notes_on_text), contents, list of illustrations, erratum, the line “Western Wanderings.” above Chapter I (`front`; Chapter I `"n": 2`). Contents: chapter titles only, as the printed summaries are long (all alike). The second contents page's running head “# Contents.” deleted; “The Law of Death” (a poem title) made a centred line, not `##`.
- The plate page references (“(P. 26.)”) left out, as in earlier books; said in notes_on_text.
- The extra leaf: first taken from n141 of the other scan, which was the plate's blank verso (the session mis-read a contact sheet of the other copy); found by the crop subagent; replaced by n140; its Drive part rebuilt as pages-008 without the leaf plus pages-023 with it; the wrong part trashed.
- Corrections: Yauhton (the printed erratum), “r -claimed” (an e dropped out of the type), “dead The” and “vanishes for ever” (stops left out), “Indian duns” in the contents (a broken d; first wrongly made “guns” from the judge's reading, put right after the spot-read). Left as printed: “tract” (p0143, probably track), “Pittisforum”, “Calveras”, Goethe's “Gestern”.
- The judge dropped the caption of p0312 (“View on the Columbia River.”) on the verifier's side: restored from the draft.
- `[[cont]]` removed at p0047 (after a verse block), p0301 and p0302 (stanza breaks at page feet, assumed from the stanza pattern); restored at p0332 (flush left on the scan).
- Ditto marks in the tree table (p0143) all „ as printed; the verifier/judge had three as ”.
