Throughout the whole of California the Chinese emigrant forms a striking feature amongst the population, but in San Francisco he seems to form the chief part of the stream of humanity that flows through its streets. The violent prejudice against the Celestials is so marked in this city that strangers naturally seek for the reasons for this antipathy.

Amongst the lowest classes—those who persecute John Chinaman most—the reason is evident enough, viz. because the Chinese are extremely industrious, and will work for less than half the wages of a white man. The better classes say that the Chinese impoverish the country, as they send all their savings home, and even import their own food and clothing; that they have no land, no real estate, nothing to give them an interest in the country; that they are heathen, and, worst of all, they are yellow.
