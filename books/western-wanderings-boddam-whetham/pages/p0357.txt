Once more in the beautiful Gulf of Georgia! but this time coasting up the island over a smooth sun-lit sea. Every mile discloses some new charm in the beautiful landscape. The numerous islands are composed of rock and sandstone, and the action of the sea has worn the soft material into caves, hollows, and many curious and fantastic forms, all of which are overhung with luxuriant vegetation, while above thick woods extend to the summits of the undulating heights. Winding through the straits and among the countless islets requires good steering, not only on account of the narrowness of the passage, but because of the almost invisible shoals and the swiftness of the current. Wild-fowl of all sorts abound in these waters—the uneatable species fluttering lazily about close to the vessel, widgeon, teal, and mallards keeping carefully just out of gunshot.
