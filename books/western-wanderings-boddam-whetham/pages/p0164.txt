We had not been many days in the Valley before we were anxious to ascend to some high peak and look down upon what we had hitherto been looking up to. We started, therefore, early one morning to make a long day’s excursion to Glacier Point and Sentinel Dome.

A steep but well-made trail, winding amongst ferns and flowering shrubs and under the shadows of great rocks, brought us, after a nearly three hours’ ride, to the summit of Glacier Point. The view from this Point is the most comprehensive of any, as it embraces the upper waterfalls, rocks, and cañons, and the magnificent views of the High Sierras.
