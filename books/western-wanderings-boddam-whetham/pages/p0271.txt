But it is because the Government is most indiscreet in its selection of agents to carry out its compacts with the Indians. The Government treats the Indians like men and brethren, and makes all sorts of promises, contracts very formal treaties, and when the big chiefs appear at Washington for a ‘pow-wow’ sends them back to their hungry tribes with promises of beef, beads, and blankets. It then furnishes unprincipled agents with the necessary goods and provisions, and they for their own gain at once set to work deliberately to swindle and cheat the Indians. While the Indian agent has the opportunity he generally makes the most of his chances, and robs the Indians without let or hindrance—‘Cheat the Indian while the contract lasts,’ is his motto.

There are some men who have never betrayed the trust reposed in them, but such is not the case with the Indian agent generally. Recent investigation has shown that some contractors used to cheat the Indians out of seven of every eight dollars appropriated for their support by the Government.
