## Decisions
- Cover image: the title page (the cloth cover is plain).
- Plates cropped in greyscale from full-size leaves; the four sideways plates turned upright.
- "Tea-House Waitress": the List of Illustrations says facing p. 71, but both the UC and the Cornell copies bind
  it as the frontispiece: kept as frontispiece, said in `notes_on_text`.
- Contents (book by book): the printed contents gives only the chapter number and a list of topics (long, about
  300 characters); the editions' entries are "Chapter I. A Stormy Start", the number and the title printed at the
  chapter head, alike for all 17. The topics stay in the JSON's printed contents. The printed contents pages
  made plain lines (`[[center]] Chapter N`, topics as a paragraph ending in `<pref>`).
- Boxed signboards (p0059, p0159) and the notice (p0077): centred lines; the two signboards side by side on p. 41
  a two-column table, as drafted. "Cailleur", "moutioue" (p0159) and "Rabicon" (p0214) are printed so (the
  author is quoting).
- p0214 "anguage": printed with a space where the l should be at the line start (a dropped sort):
  `[[corr anguage|language]]` (assumed; the passage quotes a pamphlet's broken English, but the gap shows the
  letter fell out of the type).
- p0214: the draft had set the quoted pamphlet as a blockquote; it is in the text type like p0213: made plain
  paragraphs. p0161: the sentence resuming after the verse at the page top has no `[[cont]]` (lessons).
- p0046: judge took the verifier's closing quote after "carriage."; the scan has none (the quotation runs on to
  "master,"): draft reading restored.
- Joins: the 11 "[[cont]] after a closed sentence" are all flush left on the scan (continuations). Hyphen joins
  fre-quently, grati-fication, iden-tical: hyphen removed (ordinary words).
