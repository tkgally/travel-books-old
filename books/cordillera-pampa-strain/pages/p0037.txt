If the subaltern officers and privates of volunteers, who served during the Mexican war, would not, in the event of again taking the field, prefer to be commanded by officers belonging to the regular military establishment, who would ensure efficiency and kind treatment, rather than those selected from [[corr eivil|civil]] life, who have neither the tact to make the men happy and comfortable, nor the knowledge necessary to make them efficient soldiers, I am greatly mistaken, and have been greatly deceived by the many with whom I have conversed on the subject.
