[[cont]]an Englishman. Nor a <i>vain traveller</i>, for reasons best known to myself. Nor a <i>delinquent traveller</i>, for I was submissively wending my way home in accordance with a sentence of my so-called peers. Nor an <i>unfortunate traveller</i>, unless I can be so considered in having met with no great dangers, and hair-breadth escapes, with which to astonish my friends, and add interest to my narrative.

Upon first inspecting the list I thought I might perchance be an <i>innocent</i>, or a <i>simple traveller</i>, but find myself excluded upon a close examination of the qualifications required.

Unable to decide to what class of travellers I <i>do</i> pertain, I must leave it an open question for the decision of the reader.
