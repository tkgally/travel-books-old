The provinces themselves are, for greater convenience, divided into departments, the administration of which is encharged to an inferior authority, who is styled governor. The most northern of these provinces is <i>Atacama</i>, the capital of which is <i>Copiapo</i>, one of the most important seaports. The great wealth of this province consists in its minerals, of which copper is the most important. The next province in order is <i>Coquimbo</i>, of which <i>Serena</i> is the capital, which, independent of mineral wealth, produces grain, fruits, and liquors. The port of this province is <i>Coquimbo</i>, one of the most secure in Chili. The next province is <i>Aconcagua</i>, extending from <i>Coquimbo</i> to the hills of <i>Chacabuco</i>, which separate it from <i>Santiago.</i> On the east it is bounded by the <i>Cordillera</i>, whence issue numerous streams, which make this one of the most fertile and productive of the provinces. Its resources are principally agricultural; although it possesses considerable mineral wealth. Its capital is the city of <i>San Felipe.</i> The province of <i>Valparaiso</i> is less fertile than most other regions in Chili, and derives its principal importance from its port, which, though not so good as either <i>Coquimbo</i> or <i>Talcahuano</i>, has become the commercial emporium of the country. It must not be understood, however, that this province is sterile, as it produces grains and fruits in considerable quantities, and supplies most abundantly the markets of its capital. <i>Santiago</i> is extremely fertile and well watered, the beautiful valley already mentioned in the description of the Chilian capital, extending nearly through its whole extent. Its principal wealth consists in grains, cattle, and fruits, although metals are found in various localities. The capital of the province of <i>Colchaqua</i> is <i>San Fernando</i>, and its wealth principally mineral and agricultural. Marble is found in this province.

<i>Talca</i>, with a capital of the same name, is comparatively a new district, but is rapidly progressing in prosperity.
