Arriving at the house of Mr. Gonzalez, we found that my baggage had been carefully packed in the portmanteau containing the mails, my horse ready saddled, pistols loaded, the courier and postilion awaiting only my arrival; so putting on my spurs, and taking an affectionate leave of Mr. G. and the Doctor, whose kindness I shall ever remember with gratitude, I mounted my horse, and at a rapid gallop soon left behind me the ancient and loyal city of Mendoza. <i>Gallop, gallop</i>, now I had really commenced my journey! and dashing over the pampas with the cool breeze of the morning fanning my face, I felt that I had entered upon a new and more exhilarating existence.

[[fn *]] Since writing the above I met an English gentleman who visited Mendoza a few months after my departure, and by whom I was informed that my kind and warm-hearted friend the Doctor was dead, having been assassinated by some unknown person while in his bed.
