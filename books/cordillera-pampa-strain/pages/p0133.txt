In fine, as may be inferred from the above sketch, the ministry of finance and the interior, perfectly alive to the interests of its country, has availed itself of every opportunity for the development of its resources and the economical administration of the affairs of the nation.

The total expence of the department of finance for the year 1845, which I suppose may be assumed as the average annual expenditure, was $734,923, while that of the interior, for the same period, was $347,710.
