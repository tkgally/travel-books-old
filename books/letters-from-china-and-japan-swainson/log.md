## Decisions
- Author shown as "L. D. S. [Lilias Dunlop Findlay Swainson]", the catalogue's attribution (assumed).
- Publisher's catalogue transcribed (JSON only), as in earlier books.
- Contents: none printed; one entry per letter, the editor's: place and date from the heading (day and month as printed, years inferred: 1870 to Letter XXIII, 1871 from XXIV, 1872 from XXXIV), or the main subject for the route and Japan letters (checked at each letter opening). The heading "Letters from China and Japan" above Letter I is a `front` division (not in the reading editions), Letter I `n` 2. Note A an appendix, titled "Note A" (printed "Note A., p. 167.").
- The engraved title page (p0006-1) is the frontispiece of the reading editions; the stamped front cover is `cover.jpg`.
- Printer's errors marked: two missing opening quotation marks (p0083, p0149), "á la fourchette" (p0166), "géne" (p0147). Left as printed: "marvel-ous" with one l (p0051, judge right), "187-." with a hyphen on p. 1 and an en dash elsewhere, Yamûn/Yamūn and Yûng/Yūng (the book uses both), "rechauffé" (p0176).
- Catalogue p0273: four letters of "narrative" not printed: `[[supplied rati]]`.
- Printer's imprint at the end of Note A stays in it (lesson: imprint after the end of the text).
