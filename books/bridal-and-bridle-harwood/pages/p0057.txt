The country is one huge swamp, through which the horses jingle, with their one tinkling bell hung round their necks; while huge Indian-looking canoes, or periaquias, swarm on the lagoons.

These boats are simply scooped out of the trunks of large trees, and having but little form or symmetry, are most primitive-looking concerns. The wells of the country are of great depth, and the bucket is lifted by means of a ponderous lever, made of the trunk of some gigantic tree, to one end of which is suspended the bucket, the other being weighed down by one or many heavy logs. These quaint-looking windlasses we afterwards found were very common in Turkey.
