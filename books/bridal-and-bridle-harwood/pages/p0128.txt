We entered the town at last, passing through gates guarded by Turkish sentinels and strong fortifications, through whose frowning bastions our way wound. Floundering, slipping, and stumbling over the break-neck pavement, headed by the soldier, we threaded a labyrinth of streets, and came at last to a huge, crazy, wooden bridge, which quivered and rocked in all its rotten, rickety piers, as the noble river rushed by in its strength.
