Their indignation grew intense; the proprietor and three other Greeks walked about vociferating violently and getting so furious that it seemed as if they meant either to eject or imprison us. It was natural that they should be surprised at our sitting there silently [[corr aud|and]] immovably, without either going to bed, ordering anything to drink, or taking our departure.
