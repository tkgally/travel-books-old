We rode for some hours dripping, drenched, and chilled, until we came to a solitary and dreary looking post-house, where we unloaded the baggage-horse, changed our clothes, having first turned the postmaster out of his only room for the purpose, got some hot water with great trouble, hunted out a flask of brandy from a bag, and made a wholesome and invigorating repast of unripe cherries, cucumbers, eggs, and black ligneous bread. It was about the middle of the next day when we arrived at Altzenissa, under as scorching a sun as ever threatened to addle northern brain.
