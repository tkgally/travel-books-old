We had come many miles to get away from tame people, and the wilder the tales of Illyrian Macheaths grew, the greater was our amusement.

At last our purchases were completed, and with three carpet bags, a bundle of cloaks, and a heap of arms, we started from the Albergo Nazionale in a rickety old post-carriage with three horses, and a broken door, Giovanni looking on us as on people doomed to perish before reaching Fiume.
