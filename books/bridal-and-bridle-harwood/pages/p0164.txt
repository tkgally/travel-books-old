The Greek girls, with their jet-black hair rolled up into plaits of immense thickness, and wearing coronals of scarlet cloth, spangled with brass crescents, came out in crowds to stare at us. Each of our horses wore a large blue amulet bead on his halter, which is never taken off, or tied securely in his tangled mane. This is supposed to be a charm against the evil or envious eye.

One of the troopers who escorted us had a beautiful pair of very long Albanian pistols, completely covered with frosted silver: they were about the most richly ornamented arms I ever saw. The police cavalry often wear splendid yataghans and pistols, and richly ornamented arms of every kind, which are all booty, taken from slain robbers, and which, of course, become the perquisite of the conqueror. Turkish marauders are very coxcombical about the fashion and adornment of their weapons.
