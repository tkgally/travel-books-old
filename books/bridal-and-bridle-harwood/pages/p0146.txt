In the pauses of the music, the nearest Captain constantly looked at me for applause, and appeared much and wondrously gratified by a few words of approval; and when the band, having been ordered to play me a salaam, gave a prolonged Oriental flourish and filed off in a body, the swarthy Arab band-master was presented to me by my worthy Asmodeus, the Medico. I praised his people’s performances, which appeared to please him greatly, and we left the Parade.
