We stopped for dinner where the houses were all Turkish. We were, therefore, unable to get admittance. and our mattrasses were spread under a shady verandah. It was a thoroughly Mussulman hamlet, with its ruinous mosques, its turban-crowned peasants, its cool bubbling fountains and waving trees.

No English village can equal a Turkish one in its supply of shade and fresh water.
