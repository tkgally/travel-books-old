One fellow stood opposite to me with a great stick, and seemed to be trying to screw up his courage to play the bully, but Hellenes seldom love danger, and I suppose he thought my broad blue Koran-lettered blade was more than a match for a Bulgarian club, to say nothing of the difference between a Frank and a Greek Rayah.
