There was a girl here, of some eighteen or twenty years of age, a most admirable specimen of savage beauty, as far as large black eyes, red lips, glossy braids of jet, white cannibalish teeth, so glittering that it almost made one shudder to look at them, combined with the air and step of an Indian queen, could realize the picture.
