Part of our route lay through a terrific mountain pass; a narrow defile, winding among giant walls of rock for the most part inaccessible.

It had a shallow, but rugged torrent flowing down it, which we had to cross no less than eighteen times. Two or three men with rifles on the heights, might have picked us off with ease, and even crushed us by rolling down masses of rock. For an ambuscade, no better place could ever have been found.
