There was a doctor among them, who pronounced Giovanni to have caught the fever of the country. I thought the poor fellow would have died of fright when he heard it. His teeth chattered, and his face assumed the ghastly expression of one already defunct, and who had been a great coward in life and died of terror. His attack of fever was a trifle when compared with one I afterwards had at Constantinople; but his fears magnified it beyond description.
