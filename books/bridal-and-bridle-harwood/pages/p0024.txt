To get something to eat was not difficult, but washing appears to every Croat a work of supererogation. For a long time the landlady protested that she knew of no basins in the house; but at length produced two pie dishes with great delay, and much difficulty and murmuring.

Croatian doors can rarely boast of any fastening; but we barricaded ours as well as we could, in the absence of all conveniences for the purpose, and went very tired to bed. At about one o’clock, or half-past, Emily woke me with the pleasing intelligence that some one was forcing an entrance into the outer room, where the much coveted tea and various other things were.
