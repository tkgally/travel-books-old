They and the bystanders joined vigorously in a chorus of abuse against Bosnia, of declarations of the impossibility of passing through the country, and of recommendations in favour of the Dampfschiffs, Danube, and Black Sea.

I could hardly imagine them <i>all</i> shareholders in the Steamboat Company; and began to fancy that Bosnia really must be in a state of insurrection. Gradinovatch next introduced us to a certain Graf N——, a Captain of Austrian cavalry, and a son of old Field Marshal N——.
