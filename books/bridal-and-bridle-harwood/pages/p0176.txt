The ruffianly gang of men whose comrades’ heads we had seen in the morning, lurked somewhere among these very mountains, in which was hidden many a pitiless barbarian, made an outlaw by fanaticism, slaughter, and crime.

These Turkish robbers seldom maltreated Moslem travellers, but Greeks and Jews were constantly their victims, and we knew as Christians what mercy we might expect at the hands of these savage infidels, in whose eyes an unbelieving Giaour is an unclean thing.
