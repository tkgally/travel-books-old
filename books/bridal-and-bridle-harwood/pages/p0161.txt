I asked what the Turks thought of the renegade Bem, now the apostate Murad Pacha. It seems they consider him quite as a fourth rate general, and keep him at Trutalui, like a sword in the sheath.

The army in general seemed to regret the destruction of the Janissaries, those lion-like champions of Islam. The Medico said, they remembered that in the days of Yanizar, the Sultan made great wars, and was treated on an equality with the Christian powers of Europe, before whom he was now forced to bow.
