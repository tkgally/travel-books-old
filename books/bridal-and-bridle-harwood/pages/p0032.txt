It appears that vendittas exist in Croatia, as well as in Corsica.

We left Carlstadt in a heavy vetturino carriage, and crept slowly along a bad road intersected with curiously deep ruts. This was the more provoking, as from Trieste the roads had been little inferior to some in England.
