# Chapter III.

I<sc>n</sc> walking through the town, we began greatly to distrust all we had heard of the phlegm and want of curiosity of the eastern nations when Europeans come among them. Never were two owls in the daylight more mobbed and worried by small birds; never were hawks more relentlessly chased across the sky by flocks of unwarlike sparrows, than we were harassed and tormented wherever we turned our steps.
