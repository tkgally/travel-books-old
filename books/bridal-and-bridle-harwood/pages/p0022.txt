For half an hour we stoutly resisted their blandishments and the increasing darkness; but at last the courier returned with the postmaster, who assured us the horses were in the country and not to be had until the morning. We were, therefore, <i>malgré nous</i>, housed in the <i>best rooms</i> in the only inn; and then, and not until then, did we discover the true Croat character, and how nearly a human being may approach in intellect to the condition of a mere animal.
