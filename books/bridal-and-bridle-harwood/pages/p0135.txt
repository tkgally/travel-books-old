As soon as we were seated on the divan, the Pacha clapped his hands, after the fashion of the Arabian Nights, and several servants appeared, to whom he growled out some words from between his grim moustache, and the obsequious attendants touched their foreheads and breasts, and retired, but only to return immediately with sweetmeats, sherbet, pipes, and coffee. The pipes were chibouques, six feet in length, made of lilac stems with the bark on.
