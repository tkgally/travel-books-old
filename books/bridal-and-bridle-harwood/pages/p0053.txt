We tried again. No; the horses were turned out to graze. We offered double payment, but were still refused. There was a deep river; the bridge was broken, the ford was dangerous, and who was to pay if his horses were drowned?

Then, as a last resource, we asked, would he himself take us in for the night? Again were we refused, and told to go away. But where, we asked, were we to go to? A question which evidently seemed to puzzle him; but he could not, he said, give us a room, as they were all filled with children.
