[[cont]]soon found myself on a velvet divan, the centre of a group of officers, and with the long snaky tube of a narghilly between my lips, with the rose-water bottle of cut glass in my hand, smoking fragrant puffs of the most delicate Persian tobacco.

I was asked a thousand questions about the English army and the state of India, and I, in my turn, took the opportunity of catechizing my Paynim entertainers. I learned that a milazim, or ensign’s pay, amounted to the magnificent sum of 24<i>l.</i> yearly, and that of a captain is 48<i>l.</i>, while a colonel in command of 4000 or 5000 men, has 5000<i>l.</i>, and a Pacha even 8000<i>l.</i> a-year.
