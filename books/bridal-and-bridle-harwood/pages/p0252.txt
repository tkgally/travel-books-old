We felt excessively exhausted and languid every morning, from the hardness of the couches we had to repose upon after a long day’s work. If people really wish to know what fatigue is, let them ride through Turkey, and they will have a good idea of it.

At Tchourlou or Eska-baba, I forget which, we bought some curiously-gilded pipe-heads of fawn-colour and gold, made in the town. I never saw the like at Constantinople, though gilded pipes are common.
