[[cont]]thunder fell upon our ear, and the clank of the Tartar’s chain bridle behind me announced that he was in a hurry.

Off we went, pell mell up the hill, galloping wildly in the rapidly falling darkness, while the growling thunder gradually increased into a sullen roar, and then broke into a succession of stunning peals that rolled and vibrated among the mountains, whose echoes answered fearfully to the artillery of the sky.

Crash upon crash, roll upon roll, burst from the veil of darkness, while incessant flashes lit up the heavens with blood-red lurid gleams of light. Streams of forked lightning fell constantly on the road and among the bushes at the side, and to hold the plunging horses was impossible, had we tried, which we did not.
