A series of well-applied kicks dislodged the military hero from his place at the lady’s feet; but the Suridgee was less easily got rid of.

Up came the Tartar, too, while I remained, too much amused to expostulate, addressing her in the coaxing terms by which men soothe a refractory child; and ejaculating in his pretty broken German, “No, good madam; no, good madam,” tried in his turn to lead her steed.
