And there was now neither doubt nor disappointment; for in the sinking sun glittered the snowy minarets, the flashing alabaster pillars and columns, the golden domes and lofty palaces, in the glorious city of the Sultan—the goal of our long and weary pilgrimage—the Queen of the East—the capital of the Moslem—the city that stands like Venus arisen from ocean, or like a pearl in its open shell—the far-famed Constantinople.
