The next morning, the rain still continued, though with abated violence, and the ground, as far as the eye could reach, was one vast puddle. The soldiers, thus imprisoned, amused themselves by dancing a strange kind of national dance in a circle, to the sound of a flageolet of reeds, to while away the time. By and bye a policeman took his long flint gun, and kicking off his heavy shoes, crawled down among the bushes that lined the road, most carefully concealing himself. Then another fellow took his piece and followed his companion in the same manner to the ambush.
