# Chapter VIII.

O<sc>ur</sc> journey was a most fatiguing one until we reached the summit of the mountain. On the way we had passed a party of straggling troopers of the police cavalry, and we now came to a station of these soldiers, perched, like an eagle’s eyrie, on a projecting crag, where, on showing our talismans, two Arnauts on foot, and one on horseback, joined us.
