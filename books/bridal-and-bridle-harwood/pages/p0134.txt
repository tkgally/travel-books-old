This functionary, the famous Viceroy of Bulgaria, of whose tyrannic rule so many stories are current in Belgrade, is a man of perhaps sixty years, with a white beard and an exceedingly swarthy complexion, such as I have remarked in many Anatolian Turks. His figure is broad, square-built, heavy, and powerful, but of clumsy proportions. He was attired in a frock-coat, loose white trousers, black shagreen slippers, a black stock, and starched collar.
