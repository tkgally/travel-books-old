[[cont]]duel was finished, and one of the combatants, a tall powerful man, with hair down to his shoulders, and a face which a painter would have chosen for Cain, got up from the ground, and weeping bitterly, mounted his gig, in which he rattled out of the yard and drove off. The other, a very short German, with hair to match in length, drove us to the end of our day’s journey with great steadiness, and the most calm and sober civility.

The questions of the postmasters and others are astonishingly blunt and simple.—“Are you a courier, or what are you?” one man asked me, with perfect composure.
