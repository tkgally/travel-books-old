He was very inquisitive about the population of London, and the number of carriages to be seen in Hyde Park. He was also anxious to know the state of India, whether I had ever seen the Turkish Elchi Bey in London, and, above all, whether the English Consul at Constantinople were my brother? On receiving a negative reply, he seemed surprised, and almost incredulous, saying, that as I was an Englishman it was very strange that we should not be related.
