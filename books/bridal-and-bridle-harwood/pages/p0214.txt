Two were foot-soldiers in the showy Albanian garb, with blue jackets which had Maltese crosses in embroidery, so ingeniously worked as, I suppose, to be unperceived by their Mussulman wearers; voluminous sashes, white kilts, scarlet greaves, and high red caps, with purple tassels. A long musquet slung at the back, a richly mounted yataghan, a dagger, silver bandeliers, and a pair of long pistols, blazing with frosted silver, completed their equipment.
