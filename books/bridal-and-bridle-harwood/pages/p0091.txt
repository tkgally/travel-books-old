Cold water is not a beverage generally recommended as wholesome to the traveller in Turkey. Every one advises the admixture of sherbet, lemon-juice, or the red wine of the country, which is so villainously bad that I frequently have been doubtful which jug contained the vinegar intended for the salad, and which the wine. Had the law of the Koran been delivered here, there would have been little need of Mahomet’s prohibitory precepts in respect to the bibulous propensities of his followers.
