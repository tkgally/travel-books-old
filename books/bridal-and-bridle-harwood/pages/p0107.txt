Somewhere near Batuschin, we saw numbers of bright-winged kingfishers, like flying rainbows, among the trees; and also, with many other curious specimens of the feathered tribe, a beautiful bird of a bright green colour, closely resembling a parroquet. Bright, gaudy-winged beetles were flitting about, and showy strange insects loaded the evening air.

On we went, tramping through the park-like scenery, now crawling at the rate of a walking funeral, now dashing onwards like a charge of cavalry, as the wild hearts of our attendants became exited by some passing event.
