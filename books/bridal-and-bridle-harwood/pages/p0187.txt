# Chapter VII.

T<sc>he</sc> streets of Sophia are some of them tolerably wide indeed. Most Turkish towns are better in this respect than Constantinople; and those travellers who have only seen Imperial Stamboul can ill judge of the appearance of other Ottoman cities, deeper buried in Paynim land. But these same streets of Sophia abound in little hillocks and quagmires, and pools of water, and exhibit a sovereign contempt of level, most truly Mussulman.
