Every one in Belgrade had warned us to ride early and late only; but we found it impossible to avoid travelling in the hottest part of the day. About noon we generally managed a two or three hours’ halt, when it was possible; but we were often in the saddle when Apollo sent down the rays from his burning quiver, as keenly as the arrows which pierced the children of Niobe.
