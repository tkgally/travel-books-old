# Chapter IV.

T<sc>he</sc> Servian forest is grand and stately enough, and its cool shady recesses only need the soft emerald turf of England, instead of the short parched brown herbage grass of Turkey, to be the most inviting places for pic-nics ever seen. Wherever you go, dark sweeping glades, and vistas covered with arching oaken boughs meet the eye.
