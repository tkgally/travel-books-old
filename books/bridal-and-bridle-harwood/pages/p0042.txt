At the lonely post-house of Vosternamost we had a peep at a true scene of savage life. The house was built of blocks and rough logs, thatched, and supported by wooden buttresses. The interior consisted of one huge room, or rather loft, reached by a ladder, and inhabited by five-and-twenty or thirty people, and a swarm of poultry in baskets. Abundance of cats were there, and a gipsey fire burned in the centre of the earthen floor, round which were squatted many of the inmates, without any reluctance inhaling the smoke, which, unprovided with a chimney, was left unchecked to find its own way out of the cabin.
