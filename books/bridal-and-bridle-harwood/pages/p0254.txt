Once we met a couple of fierce-looking Rayahs, mounted and armed to the teeth. They were Christian bandits, wearing black fur caps, and bristling all over with swords, yataghans, knives, daggers, and three pair of long Turkish pistols each. One scoundrel had a gun also.

They halted their horses; spoke to each other, then hesitated; and one fellow drew his pistol from his girdle. However, they rode slowly on, weapon in hand, glaring savagely and suspiciously at us, with two pair of wolfish eyes, that seemed rather to suit wild beasts than men.
