The next thing I had to inspect was the barracks for the artillery. Cannon, saddles, sabres, pistols, men, and horses, all were paraded before me, and I approved of all. The saddles were black and heavy; the sabres short, curved and light; the pistols, rough and plain; the guns of brass; the gunners, tall fine men, and the horses large, strong, and spirited. They come from Wallachia. As I was conducted round the stable, the chain halters clattered and rattled, and the brutes kicked, as if animated by the spirit of the most malicious of Mussulman Fakeers.
