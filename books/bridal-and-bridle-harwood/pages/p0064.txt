By the bye, soon after we lost sight of the respectable person of Mussulman persuasion, who followed us between Koustanz and Jasyenovasz, I remember a voice shouted out something from the bushes by the river’s side, to which our Slavonian driver answered by the one word “Glamoteh,” and the invisible questioner forthwith held his peace. Now, Glamoteh is the name of a small town in the west of Bosnia, between Trawnik and Basa-Serai, of which it is very probable our white-turbaned friend might have been a native.
