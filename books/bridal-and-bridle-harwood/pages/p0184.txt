[[cont]]steed, in particular, pranced, bounded, flung up his heels, spun round and twisted himself about more like an eel than a horse, to my great admiration, and to the intense horror of Giovanni, who screamed like a Bedlamite, and in the Neapolitan dialect yelled to “that maladetto Tartar” to take away the no less accursed camels.

Many a muddy ditch and deep stream, many a treacherous morass and miry pool were waded and leapt ere we gained the summit of a hill, from which we could faintly distinguish in the hazy distance the white minarets of Sophia. Many a weary mile did we ride across the boundless <i>maidan</i>, or large flat plain, before we reached it.
