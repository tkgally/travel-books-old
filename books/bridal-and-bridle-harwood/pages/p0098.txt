The howling was of so awful a nature as most effectually to secure us against any perils from disembodied assailants,—they might it is true, give warning to mortal robbers of our approach, and they evidently struck terror into the boldest demons of the Servian waste.

Every now and then, as we cantered on in front, we overtook a conveyance, drawn by malicious-eyed bullocks, looking excessively like evil spirits, in the dim light.
