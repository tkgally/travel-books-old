A golden sequin was often suspended by a single thread wire between their eyes, in the centre of their foreheads; their hair in front was most carefully plaited, and adorned with huge crowns of scarlet cloth and silver or gilt brass plates, made in a crescent form, and wreathed with red and yellow flowers, whose bright tints and gaudy hues contrasted strongly with the wearers’ swarthy features.
