We retreated in vast confusion, harrassed by the enemy, and at last found a house where the people were sullen, but passive, and where the organs of speech of the fair sex were gifted with less volubility of utterance. The room we occupied was not lofty, it is true, for a man of ordinary stature could not stand upright beneath its roof, and the door was certainly not four feet high; but it was embellished with fantastic daubs of paintings, representing various Greek saints, also with fringes and tassels of Indian corn, and, as usual, various articles of dress and arms. The window was about a foot square, and closed with a wooden slide.
