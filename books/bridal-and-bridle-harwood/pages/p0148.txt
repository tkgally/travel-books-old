It was curious to see how the Turkish officers crowded round the infidel Frank, and with what eager childishness they showed him their accoutrements, and asked if they were the same in Europe. Had I been the Duke of Wellington, they could not have been more anxious to gain my approbation of their military perfections; and the soldiers, when I stopped before them, seemed to regard me as if I were the Sultan’s Capidjee Bashi, the bearer of life and death.
