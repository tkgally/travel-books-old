The reapers yelled dismally; the horses floundered and splashed, and tugged and kicked, and plunged and sat down in the water, to admiration; and, in short, went through evolutions not to be surpassed by any horses ever trained by Ducrow, Franconi, or their English rival, Batty.

Many times we were nearly capsized, and as often the waggon was in the most imminent peril of being separated into its component elements, when at length, with a dislocating jolt, we flew over the bank, and were on firm, solid earth again.
