The men were mostly short, thickset fellows: here and there were some fine-looking Osmanli soldiers, with bold, hardy, intelligent features; but all were Mahometans, no others being admitted into the army, and most of them, natives of Roumelia or Anatolia.

About seven Nugroi Monsenegrins were attached to every company of a hundred men. There were very few Albanians, or Bosnians; these warlike and gallant barbarians being mostly retained for body guards to Pachas, or for a force of irregular police soldiery.
