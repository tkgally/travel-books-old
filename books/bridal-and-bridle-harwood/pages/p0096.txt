To return to our journey.

We entered on the road to Constantinople; and the Tartar, who had lingered behind, came up, caracoling and curvetting on his black steed, whose arched neck was flaked with white spots of foam.

So superb looking and stately a horseman I never before saw, as he pranced on to the front; his noble form set off to advantage by his tight white damask vest and huge crimson embroidered trousers—his swarthy features glowing with animation.
