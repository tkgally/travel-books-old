Their course was tracked to the hills of the north, and the bodies of their slain comrades, with their yataghans and pistols, splendidly ornamented with silver, remained the booty of the conquerors. But the spoil was divided; the limbs of the wretches were left to wolf and wild dog, eagle, or vulture, and the heads were brought in to be shown to the general in command of the cavalry regiments at Sarkjee, before whom also the rescued captive was taken in his grief, that he might give his evidence.
