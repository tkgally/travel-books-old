The reports of our intended journey had circulated rapidly from Carlstadt along the frontier, and reached Belgrade before our arrival, and as Emily was the first English lady who had ever attempted to perform the journey on horseback, or, as I believe, ever dreamed of it at all, we became the recognized and legitimate lions of Belgrade.

We happened, when strolling through the city, to see some red saddles of the genuine Tartar form, hanging at a shop door. We asked the price:—“Sixty zwansigers a-piece,” responded the unblushing saddler, at which the bystanders laughed loudly, and we left the place without stopping to bargain.
