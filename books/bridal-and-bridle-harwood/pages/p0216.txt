In this glen it was that the skirmish had ensued in which the four robbers had fallen, whose heads we had seen at Sarkjee; and here the soldiers had interrupted the banditti in the midst of their bloody massacre. The four remaining ruffians had escaped up the sides of the wooded defiles, where pursuit must have been nearly hopeless.
