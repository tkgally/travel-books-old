He said that as far as he was concerned, he would not ride three hours’ journey from the gates with any treasure, were he not well escorted. As usual, the dread of the desert country seemed to be the terrible Zohrab Khan, the king of the wild maidan.

Soon after we left Adrianople, at a village a few miles off, we saw some prisoners brought in by some cavalry, and went down to look at them. They sat on a wooden platform, heavily laden with clanking chains. They were four Rayahs, or Christian peasants, all young men, in black sheep-skin caps and coarse blue garb.
