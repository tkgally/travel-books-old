His comrades set up a wild shout of “Victoria!” from under the sheltering roof of the verandah, and the conquering hero came bounding along with looks of triumph, and one small thrush most feloniously slaughtered with the government powder and shot, dangling from his hand. Thrushes, it appears, are game in Servia.

Soon after this notable piece of sport, Iovon carried the Jeskiri to be inspected by the officers on the Ottoman side of the “<i>door,</i>” and returned, followed by the Suridgee, leading the Turkish post-horses, which had been brought from Nissa for us that morning, the Servian horses not being permitted to pass the boundary.
