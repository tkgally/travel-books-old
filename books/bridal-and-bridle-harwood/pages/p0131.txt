Just as we were going to begin dinner, however, his Eminence the Cardinal, with the doctor of the 5th Regiment, paid us a formal visit, attended by a pipe-bearer, carrying the Hellenic prelate’s chibouque. We most heartily wished the red-capped doctor, with his pure Italian, and the black-robed Greek ecclesiastic, with his talk about Æschylus, in a place, the climate whereof is considered to be seven times hotter than that of Demerara or Jamaica.
