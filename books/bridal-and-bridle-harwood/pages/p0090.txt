I amused myself with looking round the old audience chamber, and thinking what barbarous and relentless sentences, what deeds of blood and horror, the ancient walls of that palace chamber must have witnessed. Here, perhaps, was Czerny George, the heroic Servian Prince, with many a patriot warrior, condemned to the block. The Pacha thought of these things no more than Gallio. Seated on his deep-cushioned divan, with one leg carelessly doubled up under him, an ambrosial cloud of smoke issuing from his huge amber mouth-piece, he quietly contemplated me as an Englishman might a picture.
