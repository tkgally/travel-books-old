We walked out, bayed at every step we took by a number of great dogs, who showed the most cowardly ferocity; while the soldier, who thought it his duty to follow us, cursed the whole canine race with uncalled-for officiousness.

The women of the village kept perpetually crowding our door-way, and staring at us like so many savages. The only mode of getting rid of them was by pushing them unceremoniously away.
