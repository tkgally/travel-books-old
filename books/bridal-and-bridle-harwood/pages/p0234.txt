In Roumelia, I saw round every fire there were lying a number of small iron tongs, of peculiar shape; these are to pick out live coals, to illumine a chibouque. If any one wished to light a pipe in the street, he has only to hold it up to some shop or cafégee’s window, and a piece of burning charcoal is dropped into the bowl at once. In Turkey, people light each other’s pipes, much as in Muscovy they rub each other’s noses.
