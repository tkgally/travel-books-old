After a time I halted until our own cavalcade came sweeping down the gloomy hill.

A turning of the road now brought us to the lonely farm-house, where we were to sleep, and where the missing Suridgee himself received us.

On the banks of a large river we passed a row of shops, entirely composed of green boughs, and presenting a most singularly picturesque though comfortless appearance.

The town beyond had a very Russian air, and abounded in men in the European dress, which looked unutterably hideous, side by side with the flowing robes of the gaudy East.
