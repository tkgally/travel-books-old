This room is free to poultry and sometimes even to pigs and horses. Here we were installed with due formality; and here were a chicken and some tea brought to us in due time. Whenever we wanted anything in a Servian house, we sallied forth and imperatively commanded the first person we met to get it for us; and I must say they always executed our behests with unmurmuring alacrity.

In the East, if you do but usurp authority, the Orientals instantly, and as a matter of course, obey you.
