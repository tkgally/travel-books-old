## Decisions
- Cover: the decorated front cover (title over the Aztec calendar medallion). Frontispiece (the Calendar Stone) opens the reading editions. 22 plates cropped upright by an Opus subagent; 18 head- and tail-pieces as ornaments; the printer's device (p0008) stays in the title division (JSON only).
- Tissue guards (32 leaves) not drafted: they show the plate or the facing page through; each waived (`leaves`).
- Contents: the printed contents lists each letter with a long run of topics; the entries give each letter's heading instead (where and when written), said in notes_on_text. The head-title on p. 1 is typed as centred lines (front matter).
- Errata slip applied as `[[corr]]` (Borrow, monolith, sopilotes); its added sentence for p. 117 not inserted, quoted in notes_on_text.
- Printer's errors marked: “Andes,” (comma ending a paragraph, p. 28), “a sa” for “as a” (p. 64, note), “beeen” (p. 117), “nnmber” (p. 147), “commnnication” and “locking” for “looking” (p. 171).
- `leaf_order`: the Infiernillo plate (faces p. 39) and the two Cholula plates (face p. 157) read after the page they face, so they no longer end the letter or section before.
- Drop capitals: the words after them are printed in full capitals (ABOUT, WE, IN, ON, WHEN); one typed “We” made “WE”.
- Captions printed in italic capitals: all set in italic (the draft had marked some).
