## Decisions
- The endnotes (pp. 181–182): a “Notes” division in the reading editions, the numerals in the text kept as printed (superscripts), not linked (assumed; the markup has no endnotes: `workflow.md` §"Not yet solved"; a later version can link them).
- No chapters: eleven contents entries of the editor’s (dates and places, proposed by a Sonnet subagent from the draft, checked against the entries), each a page. The entry for early July begins with the printed “June 3rd.” (kept as printed; said in `notes_on_text`).
- The draft typed the endnote numerals as `[[fnref N]]`: made `<sup>N</sup>` (five, all printed; “4” is printed twice).
- Accents as printed, each occurrence on a crop (Opus subagent): Hvità/Hvitá, Thorsà/Thorsá, Oxera/Oxerà, Ströker/Strokër, Bruar À (the draft had Á on p0046). “Nidarosiense”: the subagent saw a diaeresis, the session did not; left plain. “Solanforso” (p0059): a possible space, left as typed.
- p0038: “June 2[[supplied 5]]th” (the digit's foot cut off in the scan; the draft guessed 0, the verifier 0; the dates around are 24th and 26th).
- Printer’s error: “Hvtá” (p0058). “necessaris” (p0169) kept as printed (not certainly a misprint for “necessaries” in an equipment list). Captions p0006 and p0111 made title case like the others.
- keep_hyphens: good-sized (p0090), before-named (p0116), one-half (p0137).
