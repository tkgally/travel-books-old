After crossing the river we ascended to the higher ground, which commanded a glorious view of the plain of Hecla and the surrounding mountains, cutting them off, as it were, from the more eastern portion of the Island. The Plain of Hecla, now lying behind us, is the most fertile plain in Iceland, and contains more farms than any other. One great peculiarity of the sandy and barren portions of these plains, is the multitude of apparently water-worn pebbles with which they are strewn; but the rapid shifting of streams, the great rainfall, and, though last not least, the attrition of the small sandy particles which are continually being blown along their faces, no doubt explain their appearance.
