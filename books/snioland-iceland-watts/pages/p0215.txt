# Flora of Iceland.

T<sc>he</sc> flora of Iceland is much more extensive than might be supposed from its latitude, owing to the excellent soil afforded by the disintegrated volcanic rocks and the salutary effect of the Gulf Stream, which washes the south shore of Iceland. Considering these two favourable circumstances, the absence of trees in Iceland and the pallor of the Icelandic flowers is somewhat remarkable. This, however, may be explained by the great absence of sunshine, the chilling influence of the huge and numerous ice Jökulls, and the nipping blasts of the N. and NW. wind from the Polar ice, The flora is essentially European, and there are only sixty-two species which are not natives of Great Britain.
