Hitherto these glaciers have ebbed and flowed in ratio as the repeated series of seasons have been either wet or dry; but there is no knowing how gravely a prolonged series of wet and cold seasons might affect them. Nor is the important part which Arctic and sub-Arctic glaciers play in the economy of the temperature of our climate duly recognised.
