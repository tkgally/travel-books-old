The Wallachians had been at war with the Turks, and having met with reverses, they were forced to conclude a peace, and the foregoing treaty contains the terms on which that peace was established.

The Danube often proved an inefficient barrier between the Christians of Moldo-Wallachia, and their fanatical neighbours. After the death of Mahomet II., the Turks made frequent inroads into the Principalities, and at one time seized upon the fortresses of Ibraila, Giurgevo, and Tourno. In 1593, however, the Vaïvode Michael put to the sword a body of three thousand janissaries, who were committing horrible ravages in the country, and finally drove the Turks across the Danube.
