Mr. Cunningham is Vice-Consul at Ibraila as well as Galatz; but he happened just then to be at the latter town. His Chancellier, however, applied to the Wallachian Governor of the town to have Georgie released; but the Governor replied that he could not interfere, and referred the Chancellier to the Russian General. Since the occupation of the Principalities by the Russians, all British Agents have received strict injunctions to abstain from official communication with the Russian military authorities, and not in any way to recognise their right to interfere in the internal administration of the country. The Chancellier, therefore, refused to apply to the Russian General, and said that he would hold the Governor responsible for Georgie’s arrest.
