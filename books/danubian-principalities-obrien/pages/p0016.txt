On landing at the quarantine-ground at Ibraila, a police officer asked me for my passport. I showed a teskéré, or order from the Turkish authorities at Constantinople to admit me, without let or hindrance, into Moldo-Wallachia. This he waved aside with contempt. I then showed him my English passport, properly viséd. To this latter document he made no objection; and accompanied by the other passengers, who had landed at the same time as myself, and a guard of Wallachian soldiers, I made my solemn entry into the quarantine of Ibraila. Everybody confined in this quarantine is supposed to have brought with him his bed and all other necessary domestic utensils. Luckily for me, a Greek merchant, going to Bucharest with his family, came into quarantine at the same time as myself, and he was so kind as to lend me a bed, and from the guardiano I hired a half barrel, which I saw before the door, and in that I performed my daily ablutions.
