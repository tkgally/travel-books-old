What the amount of this rise may be, I could not tell for want of instruments, but it must far [[corr exeeed|exceed]] what could be overcome by cutting, even with very numerous locks. Secondly, the ridge of hills running along the sea coast, which is, or appears to be, unbroken by any opening connecting the shore of the Black Sea with any of the valleys which lie towards the Danube.

The road now existing from Tchernavoda is, in most places, excellent, and might have been adopted by the Austrian company if they had followed up their plan of making Kustendji the place of embarkation for passengers and goods for Constantinople by the Danube Steam Company’s conveyance.

Posting has not been established along either of the above lines, but horses are easily procured, as are also small and convenient carts for passengers and goods.
