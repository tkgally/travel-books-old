The curate and the painter conned over the letters together, and the result was that they both went instantly to the house of a prince who is known to have a kind honest heart; and when the prince heard what they had to say, he sent for his advocate, who took the letters before the high tribunal of Rome, and in a little time our young friend the painter was recognised as the rightful heir to a dukedom, one of the oldest in Italy, and he is, at the present day, lord of more than one noble chateau and of many a broad acre. The mother of the young man had, strange to say, taken a dislike to him from the moment of his birth, and she gave the child to one of her women, determined never to acknowledge it as her son. The duke, her husband, was then dead, and report said that she had given her love to another.
