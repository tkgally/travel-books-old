# Chapter XI.

I<sc>t</sc> is impossible to suppose that the inhabitants of the kingdom of Greece believe that the sympathy professed for them by Russia is either sincere or disinterested. One fact alone will be sufficient to show what are the real views of the cabinet of St. Petersburg with regard that country.

In 1843 a conspiracy was got up in Greece, which had for its object to force King Otho to abandon his throne, and fly from the country, that a Hospodar appointed by Russia might be placed in his stead.
