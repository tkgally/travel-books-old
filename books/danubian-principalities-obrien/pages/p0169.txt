Fortunately, Mr. Cunningham added, the despatches in the messenger’s charge had been left at the Vice-Consular office whilst he went to get some refreshment. The despatches, therefore, were not in his possession when he was taken to prison, and an employé of the Vice-Consulate had started with them for their destination.

Luckily General Inglehardt had discretion enough to see that the matter, as it stood, was very serious, and that if he persisted in keeping Georgie in prison it might become much more so; he, therefore, after some delay, gave orders for his release.
