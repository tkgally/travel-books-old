On either side of the road is the public garden, which, for its size, is one of the prettiest in Europe. It is laid out in the English style, with gravel-walks, winding amidst thick foliage and bright parterres of flowers. In the midst of green spots, here and there are fountains flinging their spray into the air, and there is a small lake, on the shore of which is a picturesque grotto. In the centre of an open lawn is a pavilion, and here, on fête-days, a military band is stationed. This garden was planned and laid out by Prince Bebesco, the late Hospodar. The public promenade is also chiefly his work. In a part of the garden, through an opening in a little grove of acacias, one catches a glimpse of a very charming villa, the abode of a young lady, who is the ward of the present reigning Prince, and is one of the beauties of Bucharest.
