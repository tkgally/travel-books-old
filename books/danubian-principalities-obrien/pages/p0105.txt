Before the Christian subjects of the Sultan call out for the Protectorate of Russia, let them look first to Moldo-Wallachia, and see the baleful effects of that Protectorate in these Principalities. Since 1829, the power of the Porte in the Danubian provinces has been but a name. The Czar, under the modest title of Protector, has been, since that period, the virtual sovereign of these countries. Not a single appointment, from that of Hospodar, down to the elder of a village, can be made without his approval. The Russian Consuls-General have had the finances under their control, and the chief of the quarantine, which is a police institution, is a Russian.
