The only person on her deck was a long marine, in a mud-coloured great coat, hanging over the bulwark, and dropping bits of straw into the tide. According to the treaty of Adrianople, the island of St. George, on which Sulina is built, as well as the other islands of the Danube, ought to be uninhabited. The Russians, however, built a quarantine station at the south-eastern point of Lati Island, and shortly after they raised the little town of Sulina, of which they constituted themselves the masters. At the opposite point of St. George’s island, at the entrance of the channel, the Russians have also built a quarantine station. The rest of the island of St. George is a desolate swamp.
