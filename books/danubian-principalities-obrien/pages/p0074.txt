The population of Wallachia is less than three millions, but the country is capable of feeding five times that number. Its soil is one of the richest in the world. The superabundance of its produce contributes to the support of thousands who inhabit the British empire. But its resources are far—very far—from being developed. The commerce of the country is abandoned to strangers, agriculture is neglected, and the more refined arts and manufactures are unknown. Wallachia is intersected by six rivers, whose source is in the Carpathians. These rivers could at a small comparative expense be made navigable for the passage of rafts. The mountains in which they have their source are covered to their summits with magnificent forests.
