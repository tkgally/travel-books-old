One of the first objects of Prince Gortschakoff, on the breaking out of the war, seemed to be to induce the Turks to occupy that part of the Principalities. For that purpose, he withdrew his troops from Little Wallachia, leaving but a very small force in the neighbourhood of Crayova. Had his object been to prevent the Turks from taking possession of Kalafat, he might have fallen upon them in detail as they landed, for they came over in boats; or he might have attacked them before they commenced their works of defence. Such, however, was not his plan. He hopes, evidently, to make short work of the campaign, to fall upon the Turks when they have assembled in sufficient numbers on the left bank of the Danube, so that, if the river is to be crossed next spring, it can be effected with but little difficulty.
