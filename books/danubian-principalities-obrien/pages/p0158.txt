# Chapter XVII.

A<sc>fter</sc> the engagement at Oltanitza, there was some skirmishing, of no importance, between the Turks on Mokan and the Russian troops stationed at Giurgevo. On one occasion, the Russians brought some field-pieces down to the water’s edge, opposite to Mokan, during the night; and when the fog cleared away in the morning, they opened a brisk cannonade upon the Turks, and then, crossing over, drove them from the island.
