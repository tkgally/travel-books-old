The people had followed the troops into the open place before the palace. When they heard the movement was to get rid of the foreign employés, they joined in the cry, and they shouted out that the King should give them a constitution. Sir Edmund Lyons had not been long in conference with the King, when he came out and announced to the soldiers and the people, in the name of his Majesty, that the foreign employés should be all sent out of Greece, and that a constitution would be given to that country. This announcement was received with wild enthusiasm, the soldiers returned to their quarters, and the citizens carried Kalergi in triumph through the streets of Athens. Thus, as it sometimes happens, out of evil comes good.
