On the heights were several batteries of field artillery, with horses standing near; and amongst the soldiers moving about the town and along the river-side I observed the fustanelle and glittering arms of the Albanians.

The next town above Hirsova is Tchernavoda. Here there was also a strong Turkish force, somewhat similar to that at Hirsova. Built out into the river were several flour-mills; but the current in that part is not very strong, and the wheels turned but slowly. There were fishermen on the bank arranging their nets and repairing their long canoe-like boats, and beside them their half-naked children at play. We saw women passing with burthens on their heads, and labourers working in the fields hard by, and we could hear the drowsy hum of the wheels of the mills.
