When at times he turned round, laughing, with his flashing eyes, and glittering white teeth, and his elfin hair streaming in the wind, he looked a perfect imp.

It was a more serious thing than I thought, that of entering the Russian lines; for those pleasant fellows, the Cossacks, have a habit of firing at strangers; but I think it right to say that if they do not succeed in killing you at the first shot, they often enter very willingly into conversation, and are ready to receive anything you have to give. I had, therefore, to keep up the road as high as Dobrene, a distance of five posts, and then take the road by Negoyesti to Oltanitza. At Dobrene we came upon a brigade of infantry and a strong body of sappers and miners, marching in the same direction as ourselves.
