This system of appointing the Vaïvodes by a simple firman from the Porte, continued till after the treaty of Adrianople, in 1829, when M. de Kisselief, then Russian Plenipotentiary of the Principalities, drew up a form of government for Moldo-Wallachia, known as the “<i>Réglément Organic.</i>” Russia had then become a protecting Power, and had a voice even more potent than that of Turkey in the internal affairs of the Principalities. By the “<i>Réglément Organic,</i>” the system of electing Hospodars from amongst the native Boyards was restored, but the election was subject to the joint approval of both the Sublime Porte and the Court of St. Petersburg.
