The river Sereth, which separates the two Principalities, is larger and deeper than the rivers flowing through Wallachia, and its waters are consequently covered during the season with immense rafts of wood, consisting chiefly of oak and pine, both of which are good in building and masting ships.

In the Carpathians, veins of gold, silver, quicksilver, iron, copper, pitch, sulphur, and coals, have been traced, but are never worked. The Russians, when in possession of the country in 1811, made an attempt at working some of these mines, but shortly after, peace being proclaimed, the Russian army retired, and the mines were abandoned.
