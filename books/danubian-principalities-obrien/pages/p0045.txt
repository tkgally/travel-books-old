It is not pleasant in these out-of-the-way places to arrive late at night in a strange town, or even at a friend’s house; for the best of friends and truest of Amphytrions will be sure, in any country, to mingle his welcome with curses, if you rouse him out of his first sleep, and force him to do the honours of his house in his night shirt. I politely opened my mind on this subject to the youth who was to act as our postilion, and he, being a boy of the world, at once acknowledged the propriety of my reasoning. He called the conductor; my baggage was quickly hoisted up to the top of the coach, carefully corded down, and then covered with a tarpaulin. The conductor jumped on the imperial to see that all was right, the postilion cracked his whip, and eight hours after I found myself standing in the coach yard at Giurgevo, in the very place where I had held my conference with the postilion.
