Dinner, though no very Sybaritic enjoyment under the auspices of the improvident steward and unsavory cook of the “<i>Law</i>,” was always impatiently longed for and eagerly devoured. With what restless anxiety eyes were cast through the skylights on deck, at the preparations for the great event of the day, no one but a traveler with that inexplicable voracity engendered at sea can fully comprehend.
