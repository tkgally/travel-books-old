When the ice was first imported, it brought fifty cents a pound, and the Californian steamers were large purchasers at that price; but now these vessels supply themselves in San Francisco with ice at one cent a pound, brought from Sitka, in Russian America.

From the ice-house, a step or two across the hot beach, brings me to the Railroad Hospital, which, by one of those strange contrasts, ever occurring in daily life, is my next object of interest.
