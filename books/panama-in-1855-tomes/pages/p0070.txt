There was no need to investigate the physical causes which, in accordance with the laws of health, must lead to the deadly effects of malignant disease. There was no necessity of studying the statistics, which are but a continuous record of death, when the ghastly face of every man you met was a <i>memento mori.</i> It is not only the American white man who wilts, decays, and dies, in the malignant atmosphere, which is the breath of death and not of life, inspired by the inhabitant of Aspinwall.

The people of all countries, and of all races and color, whom the call of duty, the stimulus of enterprise, and the hard necessities of labor have gathered together in Aspinwall, are equally forced to pay the fatal tribute demanded by the inexorable fate which overshadows with its dark wings that region, and strikes down every one prostrate in resistless acknowledgment of its terrible tyranny.
