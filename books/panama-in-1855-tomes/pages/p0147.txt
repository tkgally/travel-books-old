The French wines are generally drank at Panama, and Monsieur Victor supplied us with some acrid specimens of St. Julien and Barsac. There are no native beverages but the chichas, fermented drinks made from the pine-apple, maize, and other native products. I attempted a calabash of chicha on one occasion, and although impressed with the advice of my friend, that “it was good for me,” I could not get beyond the first glutinous, mussy taste, and accordingly remained uncognizant of the benefits of chicha.
