The best things I could find to eat were the pigeons which abound on the Isthmus; and, when Monsieur Victor concentrated his energies on a <i>pigeon grillè</i>, I could forgive him for his fast-decaying reminiscences of the French cuisine, in the shape of his India-rubber <i>fillets</i>, and his <i>fricandeaus de veau</i> of tough rags. Fish, although abounding in the bay, I seldom could get, for the natives are too lazy to catch it or to bring it to market. Iguano steaks and monkey ragouts are pronounced excellent; but my stay being short on the Isthmus, I did not care to admit such foreigners to a naturalization in my native American stomach. Fruits of all kinds can be had at Panama, but never at a very moderate price, for here again the indolence of the natives interferes with an abundant supply.
