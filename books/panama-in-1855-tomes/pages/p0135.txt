Warmth of hospitality, however, can be dispensed with in the torrid zone; and we found naked, thick walls, bare cedar-floors, perpetually open windows, and meagre cots, without a mattress below or a coverlet above, more favorable to repose than all the appointments of a more luxurious upholstery.

Most of our company had but two days to spend at Panama, and much of that time had been appropriated in advance to the celebration as laid down in the programme. There was the excursion to Taboga to be undergone; the lunch of the English Steam Company to be eaten; and, as if a feast spread by the substantial hospitality of John Bull were not quite enough for at least twenty-four hours, there was to be digested, on the same day, the prodigious banquet of the Railroad Company, with its long speeches, which, although so well masticated, were rather tough morsels to swallow.
