Again, wherever man’s intrusive hand has bared the rock-ribbed mountain, or uncovered the fair bosom of the earth, a kind nature has thrown her flowing mantle of verdant growth.[[fnref *]]

The deficiencies of the road are being promptly met by the inexhaustible energies of the chief engineer, and thousands of laborers are busy ballasting and filling in the trestle-work. Great piles of <i>lignum vitœ</i>, or <i>guaiacum</i> ties, brought from the forests of Carthagena, are heaped up by the roadside at Aspinwall, and will be substituted for the decayed ones of native wood. It is however believed, that even the toughness of the <i>lignum vitœ</i> will soon yield to the rapid decomposition of the climate of the Isthmus.
