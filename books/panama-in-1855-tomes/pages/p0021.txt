On arriving at the dock I made my way aboard, under the cover of an iron-bound portmanteau, which staggered high upon the unsteady shoulders of a profane dock lad, whose dirty, ragged appearance, and by no means clean or tidy observations, cleared the road, like a pestilence, through the confused crowd of loaded-down hackney-coaches, mysteriously involved with heaped-up carts, of eager, hurrying passengers, staggering porters, drunken sailors and firemen, bellowing orange-women, shouting boys, and of blubbering women and children, that thronged the dock and the passages to the steamer.
