Dick, I fear, was sadly debauched by his too intimate association with aristocracy, and so rapidly assimilated by his easy compliance, that he came at once within the category embraced by the old proverb, “Like master, like valet.”

These sea-voyages are all alike, so I need not say how we ate and drank all day and slept all night, with occasionally reversing the process, and doing considerable diurnal sleeping and nocturnal drinking, for variety's sake. Day followed day in lazy succession, as the ship floated smoothly over the calm seas, and with the gentle breezes of the tropics.
