# Chapter V.

[[subtitle]] Walk About Panama.

OUR party, overflowing with holiday spirit, in full possession of the bare rooms, wide halls, and spreading balconies of the Aspinwall House, created a new soul within the ribs of the old hotel, which was fast dying of inanition, and cheered the desponding landlord with a hope of paying his quarter’s rent.
