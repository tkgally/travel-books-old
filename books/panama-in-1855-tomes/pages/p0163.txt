Those drunken sailors—runaways—roaming about the beach, and quarreling with the natives, and those scattered, equivocal-looking people, neither one thing nor the other, made up from a confused medley of features, brought from Broadway, Dry Dock, down East, Kentucky, Wapping, Liverpool, and Hong-Kong; gusty-looking sea-captains, steamer-people, neither fish nor fowl; engineers, pursers, stewards, firemen, waiters, and expectant voyagers. These serve to complete a tolerably fair view of the island of Taboga, under the new dispensation.
