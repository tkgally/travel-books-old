Now, beyond an occasional señorita, half-hid in her black vail, expectant of her lover; an Indian nurse, with a pale, sickly white child, gasping for a breath of wholesome air; a contemplative old Spaniard, smoking his cigar, and ventilating his Panama hat and linen jacket in the sea-breeze, or a curious stranger, there are no visitors to the Rampart. There is still a scant show of military possession in the neighborhood of the fortification; but we question whether among those Mulatto fellows, in bare feet, loose tow-cloth jackets and trowsers, and red flannel caps, who keep up a perpetual fire of cigars from the windows of the ruined barracks, down in the trench behind the Rampart there is a force or military skill enough to load one of the old cannon.
