He died lamented by all the women of Taboga; and left a large circle of small children to mourn the premature loss of their spiritual Padre, and temporal, though unmarried, parent. Dolores, the beauty of the village, has gone the way of all flesh, such as hers. She has been swallowed up in the vortex of wickedness, and has left the paradise of Taboga for the less innocent region of Panama, where her character and her beauty are fast fading together. The hills of Taboga yet echo the shrill accents of Dona Juana, who resists decay like an Egyptian mummy. Frank, the Maltese, keeps up his supply of brandy, and blesses his stars that there is no Maine Liquor Law at Taboga; and, as for Slingman, he has gone to sea, to be wrecked the hundredth time, and thrown upon some distant shore, to resume his congenial occupation of beach combing.
