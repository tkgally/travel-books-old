I was somewhat lost, I must confess, when, after taking leave of my good-natured, but I fear somewhat crack-brained guide, I attempted to find my way in the real city to that beautiful park and that pleasant promenade. In fact, I could not get any farther in the alphabet of the avenues than A and B, and was puzzled to count as far as number III. in the numerals. Avenue C appeared an impenetrable jungle to my confused brain; and although I had counted streets I. and II. as I passed over two wooden bridges so denominated, I could not for the world of me discover how I was to get through number III. unless, like Cæsar, I plunged into a very considerable arm of the sea which flows into the centre of the town, at the imminent hazard of drowning, and with the certainty of a tertian ague, which were the unpleasant consequences of a similar imprudence to the afore-mentioned distinguished Roman.
