[[cont]]and afterwards, when he joined us at the durbar and learnt that the priests had made us take off our shoes before ascending the steps and had refused us admission to the idol, he broke up the durbar (very properly) and refused the presents, much to the dismay of the priests who surrounded us. The whole way to the tank and temple we were followed by a vast multitude of people as well as by the elephant. It was an extraordinary spectacle,—these vast crowds being more or less under the influence of the temple, and the idol well worked by the artful priests for their own profit. It is Lourdes over again, only in a grosser form. We could not have seen a more striking instance of Hindoo fanaticism.
