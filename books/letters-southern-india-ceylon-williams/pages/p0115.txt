All these tribes are gradually diminishing in number owing to intermarriage amongst themselves. I came across a family of Todas. Very black they were, with a large quantity of thick, black hair, growing low down on the forehead, but not ungainly either in face or figure, somewhat an exaggerated type of our gipsies. They had their small herd of buffaloes and tract of grazing ground round their huts, but nevertheless pursued us, begging.
