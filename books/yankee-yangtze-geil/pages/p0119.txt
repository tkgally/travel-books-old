# Chapter V.

[[subtitle]] Through the Great Yangtze Gorges—Ichang to the Wild Rapids—In a Chinese Gunboat—The Red Heifer—River Disasters—The Sacred Edict—Saluting the American Flag.

[[center]] 禮多人不怪

[[center]] Much courtesy forestalls offence.

[[image: a Chinese soldier boat with a striped canvas awning and a flag, beside the initial letter I]]
[[caption]] Soldier Boat.

IT was a young and beautiful morning with a cloudless sky, this beginning of the twenty-seventh Sun of the eleventh Moon of the Old Tiger. It was less than a quarter after six, and the striped canvas of the native gunboat had been noiselessly struck and everything made snug and smart in ten minutes’ time. The man in the prow was getting the black cannon ready, and the sun was rising back of (bang went the cannon!) the city. The echo as it rolled over the river and up the opposite mountain side was reinforced by a second and then a third shot. Thus my “honourable country” and myself were honoured. The bamboo mooring line was quickly slipped, and in a jiffy we were well out into the stream.
