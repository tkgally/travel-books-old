Men like Woodbridge have won the confidence of officials; natives are themselves spreading Christianity. The third failure is not yet. Why should there be a third failure? Jesus of Nazareth was an Asiatic, and Asiatics ought to understand Him better than we do. If they will, then will East and West understand one another better, and no yellow peril need be feared.

But the hope for

<verse>
Peace upon earth,
Goodwill among men
</verse>

is likely to be idle unless the nations accept Him who was born at Bethlehem, as their Saviour, their Ideal, their Lord.
