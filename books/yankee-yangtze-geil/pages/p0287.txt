[[caption]] The Two Great Pagodas of Yunnan Fu. An Earthquake destroyed the Eastern Pagoda, which was rebuilt by public subscription during the viceroyalty of Tsen. There are a thousand temples in this city of an hundred thousand people. The Temple of the god of War is very fine, and was built between the fourteenth and twenty-fourth years of the present reign at a cost of 25,438.75 taels of silver.
