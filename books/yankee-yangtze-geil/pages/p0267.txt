IT was with great reluctance that I left Chowtung Mission House and the interesting family of the Bible Missionary. What an object-lesson to the idolator is a clean, neat Christian home! No shabby reception room with hideous ornaments and cheap pictures of idiotic gods! We departed directly after Early Rice, going down “Collection-of-Worthies-Street,” and swung into Carpenter Street, which is flanked by the grounds of the Brigadier-General and, on the other side, by carpenters’ shops. Opposite the doors were fir trees without roots, planted simply for the first Moon. The effect was striking, and one would suppose the Chinese might plant permanent shade trees.
