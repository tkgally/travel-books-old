This little incident was afterward embodied in one of the native songs, and Bongo Nsanda became as stanch a friend to me as man ever had.

Our arrival in the village was the signal for great uproar; their disordered minds imagined we had come to fight; they grasped their knives, spears, and shields, and prepared to attack. I hastened to explain to the chief, Molumbu, that I came as a friend, but as I approached him he drew his big knife, raised it threateningly over his head, and rushed at me.
