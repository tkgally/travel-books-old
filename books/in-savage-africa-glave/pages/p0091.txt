When abroad the fetish-man is always a conspicuous figure in a village. He wears a tall hat of animal skin; around his neck hang suspended by strings a few small specimens of his wares, and slung around his shoulders are little parcels of charms, into which are stuck birds’ feathers. Metal rings, to which mysterious little packages are attached, clash and clang as he walks, serving,
