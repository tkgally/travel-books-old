# Chapter VI.

[[caption]] Musical Instruments, Upper Congo.

The sad death of my comrade Keys was a great blow to me, and left me in an awful state of depression.

It was but yesterday we two were talking of home, and anticipating our future careers in Africa, with hearts full of hopefulness and boyish ambition. At night our overflow of good spirits had found lusty relief, as we sat by the log fire and boisterously sang our songs till the gloomy jungles around us echoed them back.

To-night pitiless disaster had hauled the flag half-mast, and the nocturnal silence was unbroken except by cry of a savage beast or the weird call of the vampire to remind me of the grievous fact that I was alone.

But it was absolutely necessary to overcome fate's merciless assault, for my responsible position as commander of Lukolela demanded that my energy and judgment should be in thorough, robust condition, and so I was determined to dismiss the sad subject as much as possible from my mind, by keeping my time fully employed in my station work, and in my duties of research in the native settlements.
