He was going to risk his life again in the attempt to make friends with them.

The little steamers, the “A. I. A.” and “Royal,” had started a day or two before. I was to travel in the “En Avant.” As we steamed away from the picturesque bay, Stanley in his tiny boat was cheered by the whole garrison, both white and black, who turned out and lined the beach to bid us good-by.
