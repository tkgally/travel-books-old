V<sc>ery</sc> early in life I made up my mind that I would some day see for myself the wonderful countries that I read of in books of travel and adventure that formed the whole of my schoolboy library. I lived in imagination in strange countries and among wild tribes—my heroes were all pioneers, trappers, and hunters of big game; and after I had eagerly turned over the pages of Stanley's search for Livingstone, and followed with breathless attention the narrative of his thrilling journey, “Through the Dark Continent,” I would close the book and wonder whether it would ever be my good fortune to cross the seas or live under the tropical sun.
