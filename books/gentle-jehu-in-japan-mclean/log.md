## Decisions
- Pictures cropped inside their frames, in colour (hand-coloured photographs), by an Opus subagent; the frames and the
  chrysanthemum bars left out (notes_on_text) — assumed: they are part of the plate frames, not chapter ornaments. The
  ornament lines the draft had made images (p0013, p0014, p0077, p0092) removed; alt texts shortened to what is shown.
- Divisions: half-title p0009 (with the limitation notice), frontispiece p0012 (`frontispiece`: shown at the start of
  the Dedication, the first reading division), title p0013, dedication p0015, contents p0017, the book title above
  Chapter I (front, n 1), Chapter I (n 2). Contents as printed (number and title).
- Printer's errors: p0058 exhaused, p0102 nakpin, p0136 Tkōyō → [[corr]]. Kept as printed: "Numobiki" (caption and
  text), "Tokyo" and "Tōkyō" both printed, p0170 "CAN this be really I?”" with no opening quote (decorated initial).
- p0089: the draft dropped "are" ("both are well worth"); the corrections pass proposed it but the correction did not
  apply; fixed on the scan.
- Period: the year of the journey is not printed; "c. 1911" (copyright 1912), said in the summary.
