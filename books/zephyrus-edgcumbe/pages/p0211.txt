Buenos Ayres, however, possesses the traffic which Ensenada hopes to get, and whether Ensenada will be able to divert it, or any substantial portion of it, can only be determined by the lapse of time.

Ensenada is a very long straggly place, and on the rainy afternoon I was there presented a most dismal appearance. The state of the road for a mile from where the tram put me down was one continuous quagmire of the most awful character. I gave myself up for lost more than once, and fully expected to be dug out in some remote geological period—a curious and interesting fossil.
