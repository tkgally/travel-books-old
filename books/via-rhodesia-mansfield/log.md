# Via Rhodesia — Charlotte Mansfield (1911)

IA: viarhodesiajourn00mans (University of California Libraries, UCLA copy; 464 leaves). The only IA scan.
Eligibility: re-checked 2026-11-20: not in pg_catalog.csv; web search: only second-hand copies, a HathiTrust record
and a Forgotten Books listing (print-on-demand facsimile); no reprint, no other digital text. Author 1881–1936
(Wikipedia). Pictures: no photographer named on the title page; credit lines "(By kind permission of …)": Mr. Stuart
Wells, Father Nassau, Umtali Railway Institute, C.G. Railways, Percy M. Clark (Victoria Falls photographer, 1874–1937,
Bridgeman Images record), Mennel and Chubb, Mr. Sloper, Mrs. Usher, E. Averay Jones, Dr. Chisholme, P. Brandt; the
rest presumably the author's own (she mentions her Kodak). Amateurs thanked for single photographs = minor
illustrators under selection.md rule 5 (Tom, 2026-11-20). Medal on p0139 designed by John Tweed (d. 1933).
Taken up 2026-11-20 after *A Woman's Winter in Africa* was rejected (two leaves hidden in its only scan).

## Decisions
- Scan checks of judge spots: drop-capital openings are in full capitals (p0037, p0141: judge right; all chapter openings
  made so, 17 pages, "ICANNOT" in the draft of p0135 → "I CANNOT"); p0124 "483," a comma whose tail did not print (the
  verifier's full stop rejected); p0248 "acrcss" a damaged o, transcribed "across"; p0314 *résistance* (draft); p0035 the
  telegram's quotation marks at each line (draft); p0093 credit "Father Nassau" (the draft's "Faher" misread).
- Printer's errors marked: p0147 "Clark." → "Clark," (caption credit); p0363 "B C.A." → "B.C.A." (caption).
- p0452 (index): two page numbers cut off at the gutter → `[[gap]]`.
- Quotations of letters, reports and newspapers: printed in the text type, set apart by space; drafts made 7 pages of
  them `<blockquote>` (and three `[[cont]]` at page tops had nothing to continue): made plain paragraphs throughout;
  the telegram (p0035) and the pass (p0381) stay set off. Said in notes_on_text. (assumed)
- p0023: the book title printed over Chapter I removed (it repeats the title page). (assumed)
- p0225, p0395: the drawing and the photograph printed above the chapter heading moved under it (notes_on_text).
- Captions: 12 typed in small capitals by the drafts → upper and lower case like the other 136; two plates with
  caption lines (p0313, p0432) joined into one caption each.
- preflight `image-splits` (new this session) listed 5 paragraphs cut by a picture mid-sentence (p0146, p0253, p0307,
  p0313, p0351): all real, `[[cont]]` added.
- Contents: the printed contents' chapter titles, all given (`toc.subtitles: all`); Chapter XXXVIII is "East Coast" as
  headed in the text (the printed contents: "The East Coast"). Appendices A–D as divisions of their own.
- keep_hyphens: p0253 shirt-sleeves, p0432 good-bye. Leaves p0002–p0006 (endpapers) waived; p0009 (back of the map) blank.
- Journey: 1909 (dates in the text). Regions: South Africa, Zimbabwe, Zambia, Malawi, Tanzania, Mozambique.
