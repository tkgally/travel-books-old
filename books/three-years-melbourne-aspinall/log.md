## Decisions
- The picture: the title page (the cover has no title).
- Divisions: title page, dedication, contents (out), the head title above Chapter I (out, `front`, n 1), 16 chapters, the printer’s imprint (out, `back`). Contents: the printed chapter summaries in full (`toc.subtitles: all`), as the printed contents gives them.
- Run-on paragraphs after quoted verse at p0036 and p0065 (flush left on the scan): new paragraphs (the markup cannot continue them); said in `notes_on_text`.
