[[cont]]men have told me that they much prefer the sports of Australia to those of England. There is more freedom, they say, and more country to go over; and, also, there are no gamekeepers lurking about to remind them that they are encroaching on forbidden ground should they accidentally overstep a Rubicon.

I wish I could describe a kangaroo hunt, which, I believe, is the most exciting of all colonial sports; but, as I never happened to see one, and almost forget the very graphic descriptions given to me of them by enthusiastic young Nimrods, I cannot venture to give an account of one of them.
