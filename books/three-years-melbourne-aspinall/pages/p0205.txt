Outside, suspended from the top of the coach, was a dead snake, which had recently been rendered harmless by having its back broken, and which now only shook its head occasionally at us through the window; and a dust-storm—a real “Brickfielder”—was blowing, so that the face of vegetable nature was completely hidden from my view. I had nothing, then, to do but to study human nature, which was before me; and, with memory for my Pickwickian tablets, I made memoranda wherewith I hoped to entertain my friends at the journey’s end.
