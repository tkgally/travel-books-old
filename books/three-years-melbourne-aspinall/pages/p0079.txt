A review takes place on the same morning, and a day or two afterwards Her Majesty’s Birthday is celebrated by a ball, when from ten to fifteen hundred guests assemble in the Exhibition Building. Invitations are sent some hundred miles up the country. The crush is excessive at these balls, and dancing is done under difficulties; but, as everybody knows he will meet everybody, everybody generally goes.

These are the balls at which young ladies in the colony “come out.” I attended one of these balls, and it reminded me very much of a mayor’s ball in an English provincial town; where various classes are congregated together, from the county families around to the burgesses of the city, and where people enjoy themselves collectively and individually each in his and her own particular clique.
