These opinions were expressed to me continually by persons who tried to excuse the lukewarmness of some of the colonists. I must confess to having always experienced a peculiar emotion of gratification on those Sundays when I perceived that the bishop was going to officiate; irrespective of the pleasure of listening to one of his excellent discourses, his tone and dialect were those of one of our highly-educated English clergy, and such as I had generally been accustomed to in England.
