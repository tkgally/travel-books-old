There are other houses, too, which are called weatherboard, and are well plastered inside; and these, the house-agent assured me, were cool as a grotto in summer and extremely comfortable, warm, and cosy in winter; but the more enthusiastic he grew about them, the more sceptical I felt, as the rent of them was much less than that of a brick house.

In addition to such houses as I have described, there are endless varieties of what are termed “palatial residences,” inhabited by wealthy Midases who have possessed the charm of turning into gold all that they have touched or speculated upon.
