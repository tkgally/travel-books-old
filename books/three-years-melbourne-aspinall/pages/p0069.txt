It is with extreme diffidence that I, in my ignorance, venture the opinion, that the present calibre of the Parliament has been brought about by <i>universal suffrage</i>.

At present, men of education and integrity appear very reluctant to enter Parliament (though there are still some few such remaining), and naturally shrink with horror from facing the virulent opposition of some of the objectionable members who have seats in it.
