It so happened that at the Bush Inn where they put up after finding the notes, neither landlord nor landlady were at home, only a very pretty daughter, which fact somewhat prolonged their stay at the inn. As a tribute of their appreciation of this rose of the wilderness, they had the great gratification of being able to restore the notes to their owner, who turned out to be the landlord of this inn, and who, with his wife, returned home in great distress of mind about his loss, whilst the travellers were still lingering beneath his roof.
