There is one kind of school which has not yet been established in Melbourne, and which, I think, would be a great boon to parents in the colony. I mean a lady’s preparatory school for little boys between seven and ten years old, similar to many of the same kind in England which have been established with much success. I should fear, however, that a lady endowed with the attainments and rare merits for such an undertaking would scarcely be inclined to leave England, where, in almost any populous neighbourhood, she would succeed; unless, indeed, the more genial climate of Australia could tempt her to try the experiment out there.
