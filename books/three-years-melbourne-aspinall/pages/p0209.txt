The man was found guilty of murder, and condemned accordingly; but, because it was merely circumstantial evidence, and as there were some discrepancies in the statements of witnesses, the sentence of death was remitted.

There is nothing to admire in the town and neighbourhood of Sandhurst. It looked to me just as dreary as Castlemaine looked pleasant. <i>Pall Mall</i>—the leading street in the town—is not worthy of its name. A neat stone church, which has cost rather a large sum of money, has been built here; but unfortunately its roof is too high, and is said to be too heavy for the rest of the building, and has once, in a gale of wind, threatened to give way.
