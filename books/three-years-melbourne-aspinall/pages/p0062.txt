These officers only remained a few months in Hobson’s Bay, and then removed to Tasmania; they have since distinguished themselves in the New Zealand war. Their departure was much regretted in the Melbourne <i>beau monde</i>. There were only a few in this same world, who, it was whispered to me, did not perhaps altogether participate in the feeling of regret, when they saw the steam corvette——weigh anchor in the Bay. These few had felt themselves grievously wronged; their feelings had lately been continually lacerated.
