People in the colony are not generally early risers (owing to their keeping late hours at night), and so the newspaper boys, having, I suppose, become wearied of rousing the maid-servants by incessant ringing before they could get the door opened, have hit upon the expedient of depositing their paper on the nearest oleander, rose-bush, or wattle-tree which they pass by on their rounds.
