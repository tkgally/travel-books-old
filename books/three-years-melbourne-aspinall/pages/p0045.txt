I told the anecdote to friends in the Colony of a dear old gentleman of my acquaintance, who used to come down for the season to his shooting-box in the county of——. A few months before I sailed from England, I happened to be sitting next to him at dinner, and, somewhat encouraged by his gallantry in selecting for me what he considered to be a <i>bonne bouche</i> in a haunch of venison, I was going to tell him that before he came down again to shoot the rooks, I should be, probably, at the Antipodes.
