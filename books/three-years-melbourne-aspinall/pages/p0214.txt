Munificent contributions were sent from the ladies’ schools and the shops, and the carpenters and work-people gave their labour <i>gratis</i> for this good cause. Even gay and volatile bachelors were induced to strain every nerve on the occasion: the labours of one in particular, a distinguished amateur artist, shone out conspicuously on the walls of the building in which the bazaar was held. The result was, that an unusually large sum of money was raised, for which the clergyman thanked his congregation from his pulpit the following Sunday.

Let me add, that, at the suggestion of an heroic young lady on the committee, the bazaar was decorated with the Garibaldi colours; the utmost taste was displayed in every arrangement, and the <i>tout ensemble</i> elicited intense admiration from the many appreciating visitors who attended.
