I (as well as his fond mother) laughed heartily one day when this same little boy, at the age of three, said to us at his dinner, in the critical tone of an epicure, “These are the nicest <i>baters</i> (potatoes) I ever tasted in the <i>corny</i>” (colony).

And these little colonial-born children are so agile, too, and can dance, with scarcely any instruction, with a grace and facility that would have astonished that celebrated professor, D’Egville, could he have witnessed them.
