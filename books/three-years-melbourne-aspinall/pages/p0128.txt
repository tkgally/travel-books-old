The servants up the country get still higher wages than in Melbourne. A general servant receives 42<i>l.</i> a-year, and where two are kept the wages are proportionably high. Families up the country have to write to one of the numerous registry offices in Melbourne when they want a new servant; and it is a mere chance whether they get a desirable one or not, as they are sent very indiscriminately from some of the offices.
