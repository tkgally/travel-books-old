<verse>
The winter-robed Pacific,
Th’ inhospitable “Horn,”
The midnight icebergs’ gleam terrific,
Are things in memory born.

The tropics’ golden splendours
Are gone like starry dreams,
Once more old Ursa Major
For us his tribute beams.

But other thoughts we’re thinking,
Cross of the Southern night!
Our hearts Australian still are sinking
With thy retiring light.
</verse>
