Early recollections of terrific storms in an exposed situation have caused me extremely to dislike hearing the wind beating against a house, and whistling furiously down chimneys. And since my return to England, when it has been blowing a hurricane, I have sometimes amused my friends by expressing to them a semi-serious wish, that I were safe in the old ship again; and I have entertained them by repeating that sea-song of Dibdin’s, of which I used to hear melodious and cheering snatches whenever the wind rose at sea, and which I subjoin, as it may have the effect of inspiring those of my timid readers, who are contemplating a voyage, with courage for the undertaking:—
