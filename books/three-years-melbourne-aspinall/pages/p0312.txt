It is the same with invalids, who become wonderfully capricious and fitful—for many days perhaps not able to bear the sight of food, and then, after only a short interval of fasting, fainting for want of a biscuit.

Every passenger, too, should have a private library of his or her own, and not trust to that of the ship. Food for the mind supplied on board ship is not at all proportionate in quantity to that supplied for the body. The only badly appointed part of our ship was, I thought, the library.
