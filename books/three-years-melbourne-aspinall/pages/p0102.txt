It is true that what are at home exotics grow beautifully in the open air in Australia, but still the general effect is, in my opinion, destroyed by the disadvantages I have mentioned. In one of the gardens, the white Arum, that very graceful and classical-looking plant, grew in great profusion, and flowered in the winter; and, although a lovely little boy, then aged two years and a quarter, went his daily rounds to destroy as many of these flowers as he could, his exertions never seemed to diminish them in the least.
