The spirit and energy displayed in the erection of public buildings is very much to be admired; but I wish that some of the money which is being laid out in ornamenting the town, could be spent in thoroughly draining it and its suburbs. Owing to this want, some direful malady may—we know not how soon—visit this city, and make desolate the hearths of many of those through whose influence a good drainage ought to have been effected. I was staying at one time in a handsome house where the atmosphere of the sitting-rooms was, at certain times, and more especially in wet weather, so obnoxious, that we could not remain in them, feeling that we were inhaling poison.
