She is bright and attractive in appearance, and seemed to be a joyous, simple-minded, warm-hearted girl, whose devotion to her mother, who accompanied her, and who was somewhat of an invalid during part of the voyage, won from all hearts much admiration and respect. Several amateur performances were got up, and both Miss Avonia Jones and Mr. G. V. Brooke were always willing to contribute towards promoting amusement on board. There were several families returning home, and many children for the sake of education. There were also (including widows) eight or ten unmarried ladies, some with their parents, others under the care of our Captain.
