It is possible that a Colonial Governor might sometimes prefer dancing with younger and more agile partners, but even this privilege is denied to him; he is the only gentleman who cannot indiscriminately follow the dictates of his own fancy in these little matters. Grave motives of policy must guide a Governor in every action, although his life may be regarded by colonial citizens as merely pleasant pastime. A Governor’s business is supposed to be transacted every day at the Government offices, in the bosom of his right trusty and well-beloved cabinet ministers; and here, <i>doubtless</i>, is where a good Governor is really in his element.
