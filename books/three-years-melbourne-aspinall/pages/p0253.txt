It would indeed fill volumes, and require a far more practised pen than my own, graphically to describe all the stirring events and gaieties which were enacted in Melbourne whilst I was there.

There were several great “epochs” during my visit to the colony to which I have not alluded, because I did not participate in them. A strong aversion to dense crowds, and to wandering about for hours under a hot sun, caused me to prefer listening, in the cool verandah, to a faithful description of these events, from some observing authority, to performing a part in them.
