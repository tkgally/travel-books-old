I believe he has been immortalised in the Melbourne “Punch,” where he was represented as bedizened with wedding-favours, with a bride on each arm, and a little troop of them following in the rear.

There was a great preponderance of young men on board, full of exuberant fun and life. It really did one good to watch them sometimes bounding up and down the shrouds and ratlines, looking so spirited and venturesome, and at the same time <i>so un-sailor-like;</i> and waving their hats triumphantly from the top-sail, as much as to say, “Am I not every inch a sailor?”
