I believe emigrants are still wanted, such as agriculturists, agricultural labourers, mechanics, stonemasons; but, with regard to other classes, it always struck me that the colony could well dispense with hundreds, if not tens of hundreds, of its present inhabitants. The population of Victoria is 500,000, but the number of those who are earning, or who are in the enjoyment of, a comfortable subsistence, falls very far short of this calculation—simply because many of those who have emigrated are exactly the kind of people who are not wanted in the colony.
