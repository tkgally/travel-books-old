I did not hear of any of those infallible, middle-aged nurses, such as get in England 20<i>l.</i> a-year. A general servant, where only one is kept, has 30<i>l.</i> a-year; a married couple 30<i>l.</i> a-year each; a <i>good</i> cook, 25<i>s.</i> a-week; but few families indulge in this luxury. I knew one household, where the cook received these wages, but she was certainly infallible, as far as her cookery went, and sent everything up <i>à la Soyer</i> (at least so I was informed by some colonial epicures).
