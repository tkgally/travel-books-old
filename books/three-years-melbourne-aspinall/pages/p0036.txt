There is a spacious Town-hall in St. Kilda, in which, about every other week, a lecture is delivered by one of the gentlemen residing in the neighbourhood. These lectures are very well attended by nearly all the <i>rational</i> inhabitants of St. Kilda,—those who have leisure to attend them. The room is sometimes densely crowded, especially when the king of lecturers, Mr. Michie, a leading member of the bar, delivers one. I feel it, however, my duty to state, that the greater proportion of the audience consists of the fair sex.
