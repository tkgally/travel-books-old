But let me assure my timid readers that a sea-voyage is not so terrible a thing as they may imagine. Many ladies have told me that they should not have the courage to take one. The passage to Australia is considered a particularly safe one; and I can only say that, although I have sailed round the world, I have never seen a tempest.
