Parents! if you have wild, unsteady, reckless, and hot-headed boys, who are devoid of purpose and energy, and easily led for good or for evil—yet generous to a fault, and who glory in being munificent—like a monarch bestowing <i>largesse</i>—do not, if you cannot provide at home for such, trust them in Australia. Send out, rather, the boy who possesses none of these characteristics—the one who is prudent and cautious, who knows the value of a sixpence, who has self-reliance, self-denial, perseverance, and that kind of genius which has been defined as “common sense intensified, or, the power of making efforts.” Seldom is a youth endowed with these qualities!
