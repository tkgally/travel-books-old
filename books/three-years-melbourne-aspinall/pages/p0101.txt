What an anxious and exciting time is this arrival of the English mail;—filling the minds of those who have friends at home with alternate hopes and fears (the latter preponderating), for what may not have happened within the space of four long weeks!

The first information which the public in general receive of the coming mail is by hearing the newspaper boys crying it out in the streets, and offering for sale a second edition of the daily papers containing a telegram of home events.
