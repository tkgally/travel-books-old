I was staying with some friends in the town of Maryborough, and just outside their garden were a few deserted gold-holes, to which three Chinamen came every day with their pickaxes and cradles. Now, when I saw the Celestials <i>en masse</i>, I could never distinguish one from another (just as bachelors cannot, for the life of them, tell one baby from another); however, I became each day more and more familiar with the faces of these Chinamen, and should know my three friends even now amongst a thousand of their countrymen.
