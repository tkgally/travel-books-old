# Chapter XII.

[[subtitle]] A Visit up the Country—Cobb's Coaches—Skilful Driving—A Rough Journey—Scenery in Australia—The Bush—Trees—Wild Flowers—A beautiful Climber—Carisbrook—Avoca—Government Officers—Society in the Bush—A grand Gathering at Avoca—A Canvas Town—Chinamen—Their good Qualities—Chinese Theatricals—An Accident—Colonial Horses—Polite Diggers.

As I paid a visit of five months up the country, I was able to form some idea of life in the Bush and “on the diggings.” It was in July, in the depth of winter, on a cold cutting day, that I set off on my travels, and it was on a “hot-wind” day in December, the beginning of the summer, that I returned to Melbourne.
