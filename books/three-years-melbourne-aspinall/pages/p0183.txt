The most luxuriant country I saw on my travels (and I went a hundred and twenty miles inland, as far as the Pyrenees) was in the vicinity of Kyneton. The two townships which most attracted my attention were Carisbrook and Avoca. The latter place is worthy of its name; especially when viewed from the cottage homes of the police-magistrate and warden of this pretty township.

Avoca rests almost at the foot of the Pyrenees range, which mountains are thickly wooded with lofty timber.
