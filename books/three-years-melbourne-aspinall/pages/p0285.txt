I have not been round the world without discovering, in spite of what misanthropes may assert to the contrary, that there is a great deal of disinterested kindness to be met with in it.

But this is a digression, and I must proceed to say that “one of the little boys returned thanks for Miss——, and the party broke up; the elderlies to dress for dinner. Miss——received some handsome birthday presents, the one of which she was the most proud being a ‘real gold’ seal from a gentleman.”
