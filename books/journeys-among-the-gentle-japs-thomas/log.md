## Decisions
- Cover: the title page (the cloth cover is partly hidden by library labels). The portrait opens the reading editions; the folding map stays where it is bound, after p. 250 (it follows the book's last paragraph).
- Contents: the printed contents gives titles only; the entries follow it (each chapter's long summary stays in the chapter). Chapter XIV's heading prints “Shikoku.” where the contents has “Shikoku (continued)”: kept as printed.
- Pages 227, 241, 242 and 243 are wholly in the small type of the quotations they continue; the draft set them as body text: wrapped as block quotations (every page checked at a glance for small type by a subagent).
- Printer's errors marked: “tbe” (p. 72); a missing opening quotation mark before “I have assented” (p. 129). In the catalogue, a price whose “s” did not print: supplied.
- Accents: 32 groups checked on the scan; all as printed (the book is inconsistent: Kyôto/Kyôtô, sayonâra/Sayônara, Shogun/Shôgun …) except one speck on p. 240 read as an acute (“Kuramáguchi”): made plain.
- keep_hyphens: Ko-Murasaki, sight-seeing, chimney-stacks, turn-out, north-western.
