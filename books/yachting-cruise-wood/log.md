# A Yachting Cruise in the South Seas — C. F. Wood (1875)

IA: yachtingcruisein00wood (Getty Research Institute; 500 ppi; 262 leaves incl. IA's hidden colour cards p0000, p0261). Another scan: ayachtingcruise00woodgoog (Oxford/Google).
Eligibility: re-checked 2026-11-21 (Opus subagent): no reading text (Online Books Page none; Wikisource mentions only; NZETC / Early New Zealand Books none; Faded Page none; Gutenberg by web search and the 2026-11-20 catalogue check; a HathiTrust machine-OCR dump at distantreader.org is not a reading edition); reprints only print-on-demand. Author: C. F. Wood, "the late C. F. Wood" in the Pitt Rivers Museum annual report for 1921 (his widow gave his Pacific collection): died before 1921, rule 5 met. Photographs by "Mr. George Smith" (Preface), the expedition's photographer in 1873; dates not found: a minor illustrator (Tom, 2026-11-20, *Corona and Coronet*) — assumed.

## Decisions
- Contents: none printed, chapters numbered only: editor's entries with places and dates (proposed by a Sonnet subagent from the text, checked against the chapter openings).
- Cover: plain cloth, so `cover.jpg` is the title page. 17 ornaments (`ornaments`): head-pieces, tail-pieces, half-title and title-page ornaments; the ornamental initials are text. The head-piece above the book title on p0021 placed before "# Chapter I." so that it opens Chapter I.
- p0009 "New Books of Travel" heading restored (the verifier dropped it; the second page's repeat is a running head). A decorated initial typed as a picture (p0038) removed.
- Openings after the ornamental initial in full capitals (p0099, p0167 fixed by the spot-read).
- Left as printed: "San Christoral. Solomon Island." on the frontispiece plate (note on the text), "Espíritu" once and "Espiritu" elsewhere, the pidgin letter (p0244), the printer's imprint after The End.
- George Smith (photographs): dates not found; minor illustrator (assumed, by Tom's rule of 2026-11-20).
