From Harbin the road runs south to Dalny, Tientsin, and Peking. From Peking the traveller may cross the country to Hankow and from that point take an excellent steamer to Shanghai, <i>via</i> the Yangtse River. The Chinese government has under construction a railroad from Hankow to Canton and Hong Kong, by which means, within a few years, the traveller can practically reach the Far East from Europe by rail.
