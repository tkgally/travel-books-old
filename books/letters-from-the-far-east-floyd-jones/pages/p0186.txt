OUR stay in Madras was longer than anticipated (being some twenty-two hours), which gave us a good opportunity of seeing the city. Approaching Madras from the sea, you get a fine view of its public buildings, several of which are located immediately upon the shore, leaving only a wide drive and traffic-way between them and the water. The post office is the most prominent, and presents a good appearance, with its conspicuous towers, visible a long way up and down the coast. None of the other public buildings are especially noticeable.
