Every care seemed to be given to the protection of the head from the burning sun, and immense turbans of varied colors covered them, but their bodies were often attired with only the scantiest description of clothing. We met one or two patriarchal-looking men wearing the green turban—an unmistakable sign, I was told, that they had made the pilgrimage to Mecca—and they looked so well satisfied that I'm sure they were enjoying the fruits of the long journey to their Holy Land.

The harbor of Aden owes its protection to the formation of the coast, and the port lies at the upper end of quite an extended bay. It is here the Governor resides and the various steam lines have their agencies. The old town is five miles away, but is connected with the port by an excellent Mcadamized road. I judged from the appearance of the shores that there was a very considerable tide, and upon inquiry found that its rise and fall were between eight and nine feet.
