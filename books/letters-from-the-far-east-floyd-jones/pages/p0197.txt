# XVII.

[[subtitle]] Ceylon to Hong Kong.

<sc>stmr. “hydaspes,” straits of malacca, nearing penang, about lat. 5.38 n., long., 95.58 e.,</sc>

[[right]] <i>March 1st, 1886.</i>

ALTHOUGH my stay in Colombo was short, by making good use of the time I managed to accomplish considerable, and visited many of the places of interest, a description of which was given in my last. I omitted, however, to speak of a pleasant afternoon’s excursion to Mount Lavinia, which I made in company with Mr. H. H. Sparkes, of Bangalore, a fellow passenger from Madras to Colombo on the steamer Argo. Mount Lavinia is a small eminence on the coast, some ten miles south of Colombo, and is reached by rail.
