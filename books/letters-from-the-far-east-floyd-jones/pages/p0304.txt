W<sc>hen</sc> one has over 4,500 miles of water separating them from “the land where they would be,” a fair amount of caution suggests that they select in time a good and comfortable ship in which to cross it. Such were my reflections when, at the agency of the Pacific Mail, in Hong-Kong, some months in advance of my contemplated crossing the Pacific Ocean, I requested the agent to secure me a good stateroom (English cabin) on this popular and well-fitted ship. At best it is a long journey and well tries the patience and resources of those who have to take it, and therefore the voyage should be made under the most favorable auspices.
