<i>9th.</i>—Palace Hotel, San Francisco; arrived as anticipated; passed Custom House inspection without difficulty, and were at this comfortable hotel by 11 <sc>a. m.</sc>

The completion of this ocean voyage—the tenth since leaving New York City—virtually terminates my journey around the world, although our continent has still to be crossed before reaching the starting point; yet it is so well known that I shall hardly venture to keep up this journal in the detailed form that I have been doing. I may, however, if I visit places of special interest, send one or more letters.
