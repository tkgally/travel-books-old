I CLOSED my last at Brindisi, Italy, but had not told you anything of the character of the various ports we touched at; hence it is not unseasonable to retrace our steps and go back to Venice, the starting point on this journey to the East. The entrance to the harbor of this city is made through a narrow and tortuous channel, the line of which is well defined by a series of wooden piles, in some cases driven in clusters—some of these sufficiently strong to bear a heavy strain, as at the narrow turns the ship had to be warped around.
