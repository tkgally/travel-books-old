MY last closed on October 17th, as we were steaming through the Irish Sea, with our prow pointed towards Liverpool. We were not lucky in regard to the tide, and when the “Germanic” reached the bar of the Mersey river, at 10 o’clock <sc>p. m.</sc>, it was found it could not be crossed ’till the following morning—hence we anchored for the night. I, not being among the impatient ones, rather liked the idea of sailing up the river in broad daylight, than of arriving in pitch-darkness. We were under weigh again about 7 <sc>a. m.</sc> the following morning and steamed rapidly up the twelve miles that intervene between the bar and the city.
