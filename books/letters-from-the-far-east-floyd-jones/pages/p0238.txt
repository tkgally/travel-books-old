[[right]] <i>April 6th, 1886.</i>

WHEN I closed my letter from India, I rather intimated you might hear from me again at some point farther to the eastward, and I must confess in doing so I had China in my mind. Thinking that in that distant and little explored land there would be some military novelty that I could pick up for the benefit of your readers—something like a guard mount, a parade, or some military procession, or a barrack scene, so different from aught else I've witnessed, that would serve as a real treat to the patrons of the M<sc>ilitary</sc> S<sc>ervice</sc> J<sc>ournal</sc>;[[fnref *]] but I fear my hopes are likely to be thwarted, as three weeks in China have failed to produce anything that seems especially appropriate for a military letter.
