In the former we saw the cantaloupe melon growing upon trellis work, each melon being supported by a bracket or hanging basket. I mention this as showing at what great expense this fruit is raised in England, and how limited must be the number that can indulge their taste for it. We reached home, from our brisk walk of more than an hour, in time to dress for dinner and feeling all the better for the exercise. The gong gave us warning of the dinner-hour. By-the-by, this fashion of announcing the hour of breakfast and dinner by the Chinese gong is quite general throughout England, and its propriety becomes very apparent when one is lodged in a house of forty or fifty rooms. Scarcely any other method could make the guest aware of the hour, as English houses are not, like French ones, liberally supplied with clocks. Dinner was served mainly in the French style, with the exception of one or two dishes which were carved by the host and hostess. The three men servants who attended at table, performed their duties, and moved about so [[corr noislessly|noiselessly]] that their presence was hardly perceived, and to my taste, there are no better trained nor more expert servants than one finds in a well regulated English home.

We retired quite early, and our day of active work secured for us a sound and refreshing sleep.
