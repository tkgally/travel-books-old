Her adopted name is Emaline Baker, and she resides at house number 6,700 of the rancherie. During the [[corr adminstration|administration]] of Captain Beardslee, of the U. S. Navy, in 1879 all the houses of the Indians were numbered; and for some very good reason no doubt, which was not explained to me, to each number are added two cyphers, so that the home of the Princess was in reality No. 67, though the four figures over the door read 6,700. Her Royal Highness was hardly ready to receive at this early hour of the morning, as was evidenced by her bare feet, which we afterwards saw daintily shod as she sat with her subjects on the steps of the Government House, but they were as clean as though just from the Russian bath around the corner, and she welcomed us with the same obsequious politeness with which the Chatham Street clothier would ask “Don’t you want to buy a nice coat?” She is very fat, of course not very fair, and much over forty, and when we entered the palace offered us chairs while she went for the articles of <i>bijouterie</i>
