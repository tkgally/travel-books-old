## Decisions
- The verifier (Sonnet) dropped five italic subheadings (the judge kept them, but could not undo: the page files were taken from the draft, whose only other difference was an alt text). All 120 subheadings made `## <i>…</i>` (all italic in print; the draft had 79 without <i>). p0132's subheading had been typed as a second `[[subtitle]]`.
- Frontispiece caption “The Little Mothers of Japan”: printed on the tissue guard (p0011, not drafted, waived with p0012); added to p0010's picture; said in notes_on_text.
- Plates of several photographs: one caption line per printed group, names then plate title (p0139, p0147, p0363); p0231 two pictures, one caption; p0363 four portraits.
- A heading “A Woman Alone in the Heart of Japan” printed above Chapter I (p0021): a `front` division; Chapter I `"n": 2`.
- Year of the journey not printed: “about 1901–04” (inferred from events, by a Sonnet subagent's reading: the Woman's University opened April 1901, the Crown Prince's child born during the Nikko stay, Bishop Nicolai's forty-three years); said in the summary and notes_on_text. Assumed.
- “Ta-ra-ra-ra-boom-de-ay” (p0352): printed with four “ra”; every stage had typed the familiar three (spot-read).
- p0222 “mĕnu”: a speck, typed menu. Index `<pref>` markup uneven across p0375–p0377 (JSON only; left).
- Crops (Opus subagent, 2026-11-22): 40 photographs from 33 plates, colour, upright, no captions in the crops; cover.jpg = title page (grey).
