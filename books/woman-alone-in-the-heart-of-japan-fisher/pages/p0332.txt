## <i>The Nippon Yusen Kaisha</i>

The Nippon Yusen Kaisha sale was the climax of the day. All was over in the shouting scene, as the brokers clogged homeward, with the day’s work done, when its shares were hung at seventy-nine. Some years ago its capital of twenty-two million yen was held by 440,000 sharers, at fifty yen a share. The successful close of the war with China boomed the company, whose shares for a time reached the abnormal value of 105 yen. With seventy-six strong steamers, it has had an aggregate tonnage of 242,000. In extent of service and of tonnage it is the seventh line in the world.
