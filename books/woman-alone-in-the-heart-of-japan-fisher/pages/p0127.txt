## <i>Lake Hakone</i>

The trip to Lake Hakone is the tourist’s delight and I was swung aloft in a wicker chair, with high back, broad arms, rest-basket for the feet, attached to bamboo poles, and borne on the shoulders of four natives. Up the narrow foot-path, down the steep descent they plodded, keeping time with a steady step and frequent grunt. Each thud and jounce struck home to my stomach, as the lofty chariot swayed. “An ocean swell and a seasick voyage,” I thought. “Cuish,” they all sang, as a signal to swing me to the other shoulder.
