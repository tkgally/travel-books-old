# <i>A Woman Alone in the Heart of Japan</i>

# Chapter I

[[subtitle]] First Impressions

## <i>A Fascinating Port of Entry</i>

I<sc>t</sc> pays to visit the Flowery Kingdom, even though one becomes acquainted with the seaport towns only. Yokohama, for example, is most interesting, and is full of piquant fascination. Though it is tinctured with foreign life, there is still much that is native.
