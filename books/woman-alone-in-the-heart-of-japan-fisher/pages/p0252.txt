## <i>The School of Massage</i>

The room of the massage was a most interesting scene, as it stood for one of the best known trades throughout Japan. The visitor is soon struck by the plaintive note which resounds at night in the byways, as the masseur strikes the weird call which tells of his approach. There was science in the management of nerves and muscles, as teachers manipulated the pupils, scholars kneaded themselves, and pupil worked with pupil. Girls were stretched on the floor, propped on the little wooden pillows, with clothing loosened, while quilts were dropped lightly on the exposed figures. The blind girl was a strange sight, as she felt her way over the body, skilfully tracing muscles and nerves.
