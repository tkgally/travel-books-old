# Chapter XII

[[subtitle]] The Great Japanese Industries and the Stock Market

## <i>The Tea Industry</i>

T<sc>he</sc> wanderer to Japan, who does not care for rice and tea, will often find himself lacking occupation when the time comes to eat and drink. As rice is the staple food, so is tea the polite form. In store, temple, and theatre, in private home and public tea-house, at solemn rites and merry functions, tea is always offered, the emblem of good cheer, the symbol of hospitality. One is expected to empty the tiny cup, and a refusal to drink would often mean insult.
