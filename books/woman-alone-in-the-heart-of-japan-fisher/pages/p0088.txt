The nervous woman or the tactless man would be out of place in the dark tunnel, for it is very gruesome. The chum, prone on the floor at my side, was pale and restless, as we swung into the sunshine.

“Are you sick?” she feebly gasped.

“Sick in a cradle, the water is a sheet of glass!” I said, and she answered mildly, “It is many a year since my cradle days, but this thud strikes terror to my head and stomach and racks every nerve in my body.”

Indeed it was a test, and the second-class people were moaning and groaning, leaning over the gunwale and offering up their agony.
