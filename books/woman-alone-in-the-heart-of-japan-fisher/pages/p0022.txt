The customs inspection is strict, but polite. Bows and smiles prevailed with the bland little man who inquired anxiously about the typewriter, whether it was for sale or for use. He was rather stiff, too, about the shiny new bicycle which reposed in its crate; but the kodak lay snuggled among innocent wares, and escaped unnoticed. The man had no interest in personal finery and only wanted to hold up things mercantile.

## <i>A First Riksha Ride</i>

Emerged from the shadow of inspection, the novice must make acquaintance with the riksha, that native vehicle, resembling a cradle with the hood raised, hung on tender shafts which end in boat-hooks. This cross between chariot and coffin shakes and trembles as one mounts, and I wondered how far this human mite, in blue sleeves and bare legs, with inverted dish-pan on his head, could carry me.
