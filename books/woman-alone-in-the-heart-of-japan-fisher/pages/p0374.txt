May it be the last great boom of woman’s higher education that in her greatness she shall put aside all pettiness. May her broader culture and her larger vision lift her out of self, to make her just and generous to rival friend or foe. Till that day dawns, at home or abroad, woman’s education has failed of success, and she is neither well educated nor truly great.
