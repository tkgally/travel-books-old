The humblest peasant has his English primer, and opportunity is given to girl or boy, since all the nation knows that by the power of modern learning Japan has taken a front rank among civilized peoples. Japanese statesmen now realize the fact that the little girls of to-day are the mothers of to-morrow, and that the training of citizen, soldier, patriot rests largely with them. Woman, once relegated to obscurity, has now come to the foreground. Schools for girls are many, with a curriculum based on that of foreign nations, and often conducted by foreigners or by foreign-trained teachers.

## <i>The Girls’ Industrial School</i>

The Girls’ Industrial School of Tokio fits its pupils for a practical, honest livelihood. For twenty years it has been established under a president and board of directors. The natives have no fear of long hours, and do not call for short days,—till after they leave Japan,—and from eight <sc>a. m.</sc> till four <sc>p. m.</sc> the girls work at their chosen calling.
