## <i>Instruction in English</i>

Especially is it true of English that its chief stumbling-block is the pronunciation, and those who have studied long and learned much have so little hold on our strange combinations that their speech is often nearly impossible to follow. An advanced class, reading “Evangeline,” could by no means have been understood, except by one well used to the pupils, or well versed in the poem. That such mutilated jargon could exist in “The forest primeval, the wavering pines and the hemlocks,” one would never have guessed.
