It was always smoky, and generally crowded, and the seats were hard and narrow, but here was life, and it was not bad, and I decidedly liked it. Fat babies from over their mammas’ shoulders grinned and cooed, and tugged at my plumes; perhaps my next neighbour threw back his kimono and scratched his bare leg, far above the knee, but it seemed so natural, when the leg needed scratching, that I did not object.

## <i>An Attempted Theft</i>

The successful traveller aims to adapt himself to the ways of the country, and I only questioned them, when a suave chap in long sleeves tried to relieve me of my watch. Even the delights of Japan might end in robbery, as in other lands. He leaned across, to get the view, and incidentally, the watch, for I felt a sharp tugging at my belt.
