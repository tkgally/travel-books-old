The gentleman transported this gifted leader to his own country, believing him the one man capable to impart that musical love and life which would be a lasting blessing in Japan. The Boston teacher first tilled the ground and sowed the seed which others have cultivated, till a mighty growth results, and branches from the mother-tree have sprung up all over the island. New ideas are always hard to plant. The school which stands for broad musical culture had its fierce struggle, and its long day of probation, when it was small and obscure, an unrecognized factor in the community.
