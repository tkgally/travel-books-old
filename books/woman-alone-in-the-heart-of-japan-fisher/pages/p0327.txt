## <i>The Rice Exchange</i>

From the planting of the kernel to the selling of the same, rice is of paramount interest in Japan. But the trip to the exchange was not so funny, nor so thrilling, as I had expected. The rikman dropped me before a long, low shed, which would have been an insult to an American stable, and I passed behind a railing, while merchants huddled on the floor like a flock of sheep, and grumbled and rumbled a steady stream of small talk in polite and proper tones. This was a very correct affair, not the wild row of New York, nor the mad lunacy of the Paris Bourse, heard for blocks away. My advent caused a lull in business, which also seemed different from New York. No woman's presence subdues that pandemonium. But many a winged native left the market to lean on the rail and puff his smoke serenely in my face, as he studied the foreign Eve who had the nerve to invade his paradise.
