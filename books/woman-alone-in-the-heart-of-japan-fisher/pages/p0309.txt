## <i>Kahei Otani</i>

The tea-culture is not simply for Japan, but the far countries import great quantities of the product. Kahei Otani, a busy man in matters municipal and political, and a prominent member of the Chamber of Commerce, is the international link in the tea trade. He is an important business factor in the seaport of Yokohama, who buys the herb from the growers, fires it, and then exports it to distant nations. He is a patriarch among his people, honoured at home and respected abroad.

His personality is strong. His keen eye and sharp-cut features show unusual character. His smile encourages, his manner is dignified, and one feels that he is a man of business who has no time to waste. He has carried foreign dress to that extent which always amuses the foreigner.
