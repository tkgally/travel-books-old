The rikmen, bent on what was cleanly and healthful, dropped to their necks in the vats, and began a sulphurous scrub. Their efforts extended to the clothes they wore. Spreading them on the boards, they soaped and washed and rinsed away every trace of the tiresome trip, and hung the wet garments to dry on the bushes. Then they donned the clean suits which were under the seat of the riksha, and were natty and neat for the homeward spin. How many American cabmen stand as near as the little Japanese rikman to that quality which is next to godliness?
