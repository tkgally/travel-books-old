In five nightly performances of forty happy minutes each the little maids created winsome fairy-land, and held the people under magic spell; and, for the stranger, the beating of tom-toms, the spanking of drum-heads, the sad caterwaul, and the fog-horn note were forgotten in the beauteous vision of sifting petals.

## <i>Public Procession of Prostitutes</i>

Kioto alone retains a strange remnant of the barbarism formerly practised, but now abolished, throughout all the other cities, and tourists from all quarters planned attendance at the annual procession of April 21st. It is a date to be marked and remembered if the traveller would see the most unique pageant in the land, but it is not a sight for the prude, and the ordinary Christian throws away scruples and principles in a measure when he lends his countenance to the strange, sad spectacle. Conventional folks would prefer to be masked, and one who understands the wherefore of the scene would not care to be recognized by casual acquaintances.
