Thatched homes and gardens contrasted tenderly with the gloomy region of extinct volcano. Green were the fields and the distant range compared with the wild and desert waste. But first among the panoramic views of that changeful day will stand for ever the dazzling purity of that clear-cut cone of snowy Fuji, sacred mountain of the Nipponese.
